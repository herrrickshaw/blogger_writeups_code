The Forest Rights Act, 2006 has recognised 25.42 lakh titles on about 238 lakh acres by the latest reported count; this piece sets the claims funnel and state split from the Ministry of Tribal Affairs table of May 2025 beside the 2026 headline figures, and lists what the sources do not show.

Forest Rights Act: 25.42 lakh titles on about 238 lakh acres, 47% of claims received

Skip to article content
Text Size

Top line

  • 47.1% of about 54.0 lakh claims had become titles by 31 August 2026 (25.42 lakh titles); on the official 31 May 2025 table the share was 49.02%. The 2026 figures are from secondary summaries of a PIB backgrounder that this piece could not locate.
  • 7.50 lakh claims were pending on 31 May 2025, and four states hold 84.1% of them: Telangana (3.29 lakh), Odisha, Assam and Gujarat.
  • 160 acres is the average area of a community title and 2.2 acres of an individual title in the 18 states that report area; community titles are 4.8% of titles and 78.2% of the area.

What would fix this

  1. Ministry of Tribal Affairs: publish the monthly progress report for August 2026 by state, with a stated definition of claims disposed and a reconciliation to the 31 May 2025 table.
  2. Ministry of Tribal Affairs: report rejected claims by state with reasons and the count restored after review, in line with the Supreme Court direction of 2019.
  3. State governments of Telangana, Odisha, Assam and Gujarat: publish pending claims by stage and the date by which each stage will be cleared.
  4. Ministry of Tribal Affairs: publish area per title for every state, with Assam, Uttar Pradesh and Jammu and Kashmir reporting the area they currently mark as not available.
  5. Ministry of Tribal Affairs: publish the field list and counts of the National FRA Digital Platform, so that its claims data can be compared with the state monthly reports.

These are proposals from this blog, not recommendations any agency has adopted.

Method and definitions
  • The state table of 31 May 2025 is the Annexure to PIB release 2150804 (31 July 2025); every column was summed and matched to the printed total, and each state row was checked: individual plus community equals total, titles plus rejected equals disposed, and disposed plus pending equals received.
  • Assam’s rejected claims are printed as not available or not reported; disposed equals titles for Assam, so rejected is treated as zero in sums and Assam’s pending of 96,209 is claims less titles.
  • Area figures are from PIB release 2147815 (24 July 2025) and PIB release 1985799 (13 December 2023). Average area per title divides area by titles in the 18 states that report area.
  • For 31 August 2026 only secondary summaries of a PIB backgrounder were found, through search excerpts. Claims received of about 54.0 lakh are checked against 43.56 lakh disposed at 80.64%. Rejected and pending counts are computed from rounded figures.
  • Percentages and sums are computed here and checked by assertions in the builder.
What this piece could not establish
  • The PIB backgrounder of 16 November 2026 itself, and the Ministry of Tribal Affairs monthly progress report for 2026: the ministry site was refused by the research network and the backgrounder did not appear in searches, so every 2026 figure is from secondary summaries.
  • Why the disposed count is 43.56 lakh in August 2026 against 43.73 lakh in May 2025 while claims rose. A different definition of disposed, a correction of data or a different base are all possible, and none is documented in the sources read.
  • What disposed includes. The 2025 table counts titles plus rejected claims as disposed; the 2026 summaries do not say whether the same rule applies.
  • The area per title in 2026 and the state split of the 2026 figures. The 238 lakh acres is reported as nearly, and no state table for August 2026 was found.
  • State coverage in 2026: summaries say the Act applies in 20 states and Jammu and Kashmir, which matches the 21 rows of the 2025 table, but no 2026 table was seen.
  • Whether Uttarakhand’s 185 titles carry zero area or unreported area, and the area of Assam, Uttar Pradesh and Jammu and Kashmir.
  • Anything about the National FRA Digital Platform beyond PIB’s description that it will bring claims from Gram Sabha submission to digitally signed titles onto one system; the PIB release of 11 November 2026 says it will be launched, and the launch itself is reported only by summaries.
  • The October 2026 reports of about 11 lakh pending and over 18 lakh rejected were read as search excerpts only.
