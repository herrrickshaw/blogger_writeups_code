India's 100-item import push: PVC capacity meets 39% of demand

Skip to article content
Text Size

Top line

  • 100 items is the list DPIIT has drawn up of heavily imported intermediates and capital goods, from PVC and ammonia to tunnel boring machines; proposals go to the Finance Ministry for Budget 2027-28.
  • 39% of PVC demand is met by domestic capacity, 1,615 of 4,139 kilotonnes a year; an import floor price of $0.766 a kg runs for six months from 24 July 2026.
  • 45% of the $1.63 billion of PVC resin imported in 2025-26 came from China, $735.4 million; the China goods deficit is reported above $100 billion.

What it means: If the 3,000 kilotonnes arrive in 2026-27, capacity of 4,615 kilotonnes passes demand of 4,139 and the shortfall turns into a surplus, so the floor price protects a market that the ministry itself expects to flip within a year.

What to do: The Commerce Ministry should state before the six-month floor lapses in January 2027 which new plants are under construction and when each commissions, so that the extension decision can be tested against capacity.

What it means: A floor price of $0.766 a kg touches virtually every major supplier, and GTRI expects it to raise domestic prices without reducing import dependence.

What to do: The Commerce Ministry should publish the monthly import volume and landed price of PVC resin by supplier through the six-month period, so that the effect on prices and on imports can be read from data.

What to do: The Finance Ministry should decide support for the list item by item against the import value and the time to build capacity, since a blanket incentive would reward items that need none.

What would fix this

  1. DPIIT: publish the 100 items with import value, source country and the date a domestic plant would commission.
  2. Ministry of Commerce and Industry: publish monthly PVC import volume and landed price by supplier during the minimum-price period, with the decision date on extension.
  3. Ministry of Commerce and Industry: state whether the China goods deficit above $100 billion refers to FY2025-26 or to the part of FY2026-27 so far, and publish the series.
  4. Ministry of New and Renewable Energy: publish solar cell and wafer capacity under construction by plant.

These are proposals from this blog, not recommendations any agency has adopted.

Method and definitions
  • Capacity and demand are in kilotonnes a year as the Commerce Ministry gave them; the shortfall and shares are computed here.
  • The minimum import price is a CIF value floor: imports at or below $0.766 a kg are restricted, imports above it are free, for six months from the gazette date of 24 July 2026.
  • 2025 import values in the last chart are implied by dividing the 2026 value by one plus the reported growth rate.
What this piece could not establish
  • The list of 100 items: it has not been published; only sectors and examples are in press reports.
  • The period of the China deficit: one report says the current fiscal year, another says FY2025-26.
  • The date of the floor price: one Indian Express report gives 24 June and another 24 July; the gazette is dated 24 July 2026 and is used here.
  • The solar capacity figures and the import growth rates come from secondary summaries of the Indian Express and PTI, not from the originals.
  • Whether the 3,000 kilotonnes of new capacity is under construction; the ministry says it is “expected”.
