# Why masaladeutsch fell from ~120 to 20 indexed pages (late Oct 2026)

Data: URL Inspection on 366 of 508 posts (out/gsc_index/inspect.jsonl, 10 Nov 2026), plus live checks.

## What it is not
- Not a technical block. Posts return 200 with no x-robots-tag and no robots meta; the canonical is self-referencing;
  robots.txt allows crawling; Google's canonical matches ours on every inspected URL (0 mismatches).
- The theme's noindex rule fires only on archive, search, label and error pages (checked live). It was rewritten to use
  data:blog checks anyway (commit 38fbece).
- Not internal linking: the article index links 497 of 507 posts statically, and a typical post has 3-4 links from other posts.
- Not duplicate URLs: the four "_0123..." slugs have no twins.

## What it is
| Coverage state | URLs | Comment |
|---|---|---|
| Crawled - currently not indexed | 178 | Mostly August posts. Crawled (75 on 28 Aug alone), indexed in Sept, dropped late Oct |
| Discovered - currently not indexed | 146 | Mostly Sept-Oct posts. Google knows them but has not spent crawl on them |
| URL is unknown to Google | 41 | Newest posts |
| Submitted and indexed | 1 | a-tax-play-grow-ethanol-sgst-revenue |

That is Google's site-level quality and priority judgement for a young site. The site published 507 posts in about
3.5 months (213 in August alone, about 7 a day), many on one template, and edited 234 of them on one day (7 Nov).
Google indexed a sample, re-evaluated it and pulled most of it, and is now crawling little that is new.

## Fix plan (in order)
1. Slow down. Hold the 15 how-to drafts; publish at most 2-3 posts a week for the next 6-8 weeks. Do data refreshes
   in batches of about 20 posts a week, not hundreds a day.
2. Consolidate the templated how-to guides. They are the most self-similar group: 14 of the 20 most similar
   post pairs are how-tos. Merge them into a few hub posts by field, e.g. "Open-source process-design kits": ethanol,
   CBG, lignocellulosic ethanol, coal-to-urea, biomass-to-syngas. Keep repo detail on GitHub, and revert the absorbed
   posts to draft. Pairs to merge or differentiate are in out/gsc_index/ (overlap list).
3. Prune the weakest analysis posts: zero impressions in 90 days (python3 tools/gsc_report.py --days 90) and stale
   trackers. Revert them to draft, or refresh and merge them into a stronger sibling.
4. Strengthen the best 20-30 posts, the ones with original data or tools: clear author bylines, dates, sources. Then
   use URL Inspection's "Request indexing" in Search Console, about 10 a day.
5. Sitemaps: in Search Console check that sitemap.xml shows Success with about 500 discovered URLs, and resubmit.
6. Re-run python3 tools/gsc_index.py weekly (the 142 posts not yet inspected first) to track recovery.
