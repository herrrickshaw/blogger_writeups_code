Discom losses: AT&C down to 15.04% in FY25, smart meters 28% installed

Skip to article content
Text Size

Top line

  • 15.04% is the national AT&C loss for FY25, down from 21.91% in FY21 but still above the 12 to 15% the scheme aims for; distribution utilities made a profit after tax of Rs 2,701 crore for the first time.
  • 5.73 crore smart meters were installed under RDSS by 30 June 2026 of 20.34 crore sanctioned, 28%; the total across all schemes is 7.24 crore.
  • 0.03% of Tamil Nadu's 3.05 crore sanctioned meters were installed, and Kerala 1.4%, Punjab 0.16%; Bihar was at 88%.

What it means: A fall of 6.87 points in four years is real, but FY24 was higher than FY23 at 16.12%, and a profit of Rs 2,701 crore on an aggregate base is small against the scheme’s outlay of Rs 3,03,758 crore.

What to do: The Ministry of Power should publish FY26 loss and profit figures by utility as soon as PFC’s report is ready, and state in the meantime whether FY26 is on the FY25 path.

What it means: Four states with the lowest installation, Tamil Nadu, West Bengal, Kerala and Punjab, hold 7.30 crore sanctioned meters that are not yet installed, which is why the national rate sits near 28% while some states are almost done.

What to do: The Ministry of Power should publish quarterly installation against sanction by state with the tender and delay status of each, so that the four states with almost no installation are visible as a group.

What would fix this

  1. Ministry of Power: publish quarterly smart-meter installation against sanction by state, with the tender status of each.
  2. Ministry of Power: publish funds released against meters installed and loss reduced, by state.
  3. PFC: publish FY26 utility-wise AT&C loss with billing and collection efficiency, earlier than January 2027.
  4. Standing Committee on Energy: ask for the four lowest-installation states to explain their delays, since the committee heard the Power Ministry and REC on 30 October 2026.

These are proposals from this blog, not recommendations any agency has adopted.

Method and definitions
  • AT&C loss is the energy lost in distribution plus the revenue not collected, as a share of energy input. FY14 figures are from a different baseline series.
  • Installation share divides smart meters installed under RDSS by meters sanctioned under RDSS in each state; meters installed under state plans are excluded and total 1.51 crore nationally (7.24 crore less 5.73 crore).
  • The utility gap is FY25 actual minus FY25 target, in percentage points.
What this piece could not establish
  • FY26 losses, which PFC’s report is expected to publish by January 2027.
  • Why installation in Tamil Nadu, West Bengal, Kerala and Punjab is so low; the replies give counts, not reasons.
  • All 59 utilities: ten rows of the annexure were read; the other 49 were not.
  • Any finding of the Standing Committee on Energy, which met on 30 October 2026 for a briefing and has not published a report.
  • A conflict in the record: a Ministry of Power reply gives national FY25 loss as 15.04%, while another published figure is 16.16%; the blog covered the difference in an earlier post.
