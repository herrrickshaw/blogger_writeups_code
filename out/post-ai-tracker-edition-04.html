Consistency & methodology notes

Every board item links one source. It is labelled Verified where that source is a first-party page, repository or changelog, or reputable press found by its own URL, and Reported where it rests on a single secondary source. Benchmark, usage and customer figures are the companies’ own unless a card says otherwise.

What changed this edition (window 10 October to 9 November): 28 new movers, 8 earlier items with a new development, and three carried over unchanged (Gemini agent, OpenAI’s Agents API and Copilot Autopilot). 15 items were retired because they predate the window, were superseded or had no new development: GPT-6 Astra, Grok 4.7, DeepSeek-V4.1-Flash, Qwen3.8-Flash-Next, Qwen3.8-Max (open weights), Muse personal agent, Gemini Spark, GitHub Copilot (unified), Agentforce long-horizon runtime, Temporal, AIR, OpenAI Agent Plugins, Muse Code, Cloudflare Wallets & cloudflare.pay, Cloudflare Kitesurf.
