Top line

  • 55.093 million tonnes of maize, a record, 27% above the year before, with spot prices below the support price.
  • 13,179 tonnes of maize would supply the world's whole hyaluronic acid output: 0.024% of India's crop.
Updated 9 November 2026. The 19% discount to the support price is the May 2026 figure, and the support price for the 2025-26 crop is Rs 2,400 a quintal; Rs 2,410 is the 2026-27 MSP approved on 13 May 2026. The gap has since narrowed: Agmarknet’s national modal price for maize reached Rs 2,342 a quintal by 11 August (this blog’s RIN, RED and CBIO piece), and an aggregator of APMC data puts it at Rs 2,291 on 13 October, about 5% under Rs 2,410. The argument does not change: grain is still about 3% of what cosmetic hyaluronic acid sells for.

Updated 15 November 2026: rewritten as charts; figures unchanged.

Distilleries offered 1,776 crore litres of ethanol against a need of about 1,050

What it means: The spare asset in the maize chain is fermenter volume and starch-to-glucose plant, not the grain itself.

What to do: Read the surplus as spare fermenter and distillery capacity, not spare grain, before planning around it.

What it means: Cosmetic ingredients are worth a great deal per tonne and consume almost no grain, so they are not a maize outlet.

What to do: Treat cosmetic ingredients as import-substitution targets, and fund purification and fermentation plants rather than grain supply.

Citric acid, not hyaluronic acid, is the volume that maize could feed

What it means: Citric acid is the volume story, still under half a per cent of the crop.

What to do: Judge any maize-based plant by its tonnage and its competitor's capacity, not by the value of its product.

What would fix this

Give hyaluronic acid its own eight-digit customs code. It currently shares HS 391390 with xanthan gum, alginates and laboratory reagents, so neither the volume nor the average price India pays can be read from the trade data. A separate code costs nothing and would tell a prospective investor the size of the market.

Target the fermenter, not the feedstock. Every incentive aimed at “value addition to maize” will be captured by ethanol, which is where the tonnage is. If the aim is cosmetic ingredients, the binding constraint is sterile fermentation capacity, downstream purification and endotoxin-grade quality systems — which is what the BioE3 biomanufacturing hubs are for, and what they should be measured on.

Add hyaluronic acid, and the cosmetic polysaccharides generally, to the published bio-based chemicals shortlist, or say why they were left off. The policy already funds squalene and rhamnolipids for the same end market.

Put the idle distillery capacity to the test before building anything new. About 726 crore litres of ethanol capacity had no buyer this year. Some of that plant is glucose-ready and could run contract fermentation for lactic acid, xanthan or citric acid, where India imports at known volumes and prices. That is a smaller claim than “cosmetics from the maize surplus”, and a testable one.

These are proposals from this blog, not recommendations any agency has adopted.

What this piece could not establish

The conversion yields are from published laboratory and process literature, not from any operating Indian plant, and the recovered hyaluronic acid yield of 0.15 g per g of glucose, the 0.65 tonnes of glucose per tonne of maize, and the Rs 40 a kilogram glucose price are this blog’s assumptions. Change them and the rupee-per-tonne figures move, though not by enough to alter the ranking. India’s hyaluronic acid imports could not be separated from the rest of HS 391390, so the $86.75/kg price comes from two individual shipment records in March 2024 rather than an annual average, and the quantity imported is unknown. The global HA tonnage is a consultancy forecast, not a measured figure, and the grade split behind it dates from 2021. No Indian commercial HA fermentation plant was found, but absence of evidence here is not proof; the companies named are at pilot or early commercial stage and their capacities were not published. Prices for alkyl polyglucosides and 1,3-propanediol are international reference levels, not Indian landed costs. The 2025-26 maize production figure is an advance estimate, not a final one, and the ethanol diversion figure is a projection.
