One free project replays de-identified ICU vital signs into a multi-bed dashboard tied to a single patient record; the other screens capsule-endoscopy video for suspected bleeding and lesions and writes a report. Both are research and learning prototypes run on demo or synthetic data, and both say plainly that they are not medical devices.

Top line

  • 24.2% less frame review in a synthetic capsule study, leaving 455 of 600 frames to read.
  • 3.4 s to analyse 600 frames on the guide's Mac; the ICU demo replays seven beds.
  • 27 events in one linked patient record, with every name and ID invented.

Updated 15 November 2026: rewritten as charts; figures unchanged.

Screening cut the review workload by 24.2%, from 600 frames to 455

What it means: On one synthetic study, frame triage cut the frames to review by 24.2%, the reduction the guide reports.

What to do: Re-check the 24.2% cut on labelled real recordings before quoting it as typical, since the guide warns it comes from one synthetic study.

The synthetic study flagged two bleeding and two lesion episodes

What it means: Each flag type produced two episodes, which shows the plumbing works on known synthetic ground truth, not accuracy on patients.

What to do: Tune the thresholds on labelled real data such as Kvasir-Capsule before reading any flag as a finding.

The ICU demo replays seven beds into one linked record of 27 events

  1. ReplaySeven beds, each backed by a VitalDB case
  2. Alarm checkEach reading tested against alarm rules
  3. StreamWebSocket feeds a React and Vite dashboard
  4. RecordOne linked record with 27 events, including the discharge summary

EndpointWhat the demo returned
GET /api/bedsSeven beds, Bed-A to Bed-F plus Bed-DEMO, each backed by a VitalDB case (Bed-A is case 4096). Bed-DEMO has a 40-second encounter and is marked demo=True.
WebSocket /ws/vitalsOne tick per bed with heart rate, oxygen saturation, respiratory rate and systolic blood pressure. At sim_t=8, Bed-A showed HR 76, SpO2 100, RR 19. Some values are None when a case has no reading.
GET /api/beds/Bed-A/identityA generated name and a synthetic ABHA-style number, age 53, sex F, General surgery, Cholecystectomy, with a disclaimer that the number is not real.
GET /api/beds/Bed-DEMO/insuranceAn invented insurer, an eligible status and an approved claim.
GET /api/beds/Bed-DEMO/recordAdmission, eligibility check, prescriptions, alerts, discharge, discharge summary and claim in one append-only timeline: 27 events.
GET /api/beds/Bed-DEMO/discharge-summaryVitals ranges (HR 71 to 82, SpO2 99 to 100), one alert (RR low), the approved claim and a disclaimer that it is not a real clinical document.

What it means: The demo links a replayed bedside feed to one patient record, but every name, health ID and insurer in it is invented.

What to do: Have clinical reviewers set the alarm thresholds in backend/app/alerts.py before any use, since they are illustrative defaults.
