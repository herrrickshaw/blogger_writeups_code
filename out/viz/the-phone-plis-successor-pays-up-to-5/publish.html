Smartphone PLI successor: up to 5% above 15% yearly growth, 0.20% for battery cells

Skip to article content
Text Size

Top line

  • 2.25–5% base incentive on eligible sales; the 2.75% headline applies only to a narrow band.
  • ₹62,500 crore five-year outlay in the Cabinet release; the guidelines print ₹62,300 crore.
  • 0.20% extra for a battery, paid only if the cell inside is made in India.
Updated 28 October 2026. Checked against the Gazette of India notification of the Mobile Phone Manufacturing Scheme (CG-DL-E-21082026-275648). The outlay question this piece left open is settled: the gazette gives ₹62,500 crore including administrative charges, fungible between the two target segments. The sales thresholds, the 15% moving baseline, the ₹10,000 crore entry level for new brands and the 2.75% band rate for the first two years also match the gazette text.

Updated 15 November 2026: rewritten as charts; figures unchanged.

Sales above the band earn 4–5%, so the 2.75% headline is a floor

What it means: On the post's reading of the band, most eligible sales of a growing brand earn the higher rate.

What to do: MeitY should publish a worked example of the 2.75% band, as the post asks.

Enclosures carry the largest component add-on, 0.50% of eligible sales

What it means: The five component add-ons total 1.50%, and each pays only pro rata to a localisation share that must reach 25%.

What to do: Brands should check each component against the 25% localisation minimum before committing to a line.

OLED display module imports grew fastest, up 71.4% in FY2025-26

What it means: The lithium-ion line covers every accumulator, so it overstates what phone makers import for their cells.

What to do: Track the FY2026-27 import lines for cells and OLED modules to see whether localisation responds.
