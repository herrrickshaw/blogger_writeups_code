Most of the 314 codes are confirmed under Ministry of Textiles, 53 are not

What it means: The Ministry of Textiles owns 261 of the 314 codes across 13 labels, or 83%, and Packtech, Geotech and Clothtech confirm all of theirs (100%).

What to do: Read each 'No' code as a ministry-ownership flag, not a verdict that the product is not a technical textile.
