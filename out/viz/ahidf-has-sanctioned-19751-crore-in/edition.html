The Animal Husbandry Infrastructure Development Fund is the government's main credit lever for private investment in dairy plants, meat processing, animal feed and veterinary-vaccine units — a 3%-subsidised, 90%-loan-to-cost scheme routed through SIDBI's Udyami Mitra portal. Its own live dashboard, checked on 10 August 2026, shows the fund has sanctioned ₹19,750.8 crore in term loans against 916 applications. It has actually disbursed ₹7,770.4 crore — 39% of what's been sanctioned, and just 358 applications. The gap between a scheme's headline sanction number and what's actually reached a builder's account is where most infrastructure-fund stories live, and AHIDF's own dashboard makes the gap unusually easy to see.

Top line

  • 358 of 11,201 filed applications have been disbursed, after 916 were sanctioned by a lender.
  • 39% of the ₹19,750.8 crore sanctioned loan value has been disbursed (₹7,770.4 crore).

What it means: The portal's counters show the pipeline narrowing at each stage, from 11,201 filed to 358 disbursed.

What to do: Publish average sanction-to-first-disbursement times so readers can separate normal construction lag from stuck applications.

Only 39% of sanctioned loan value has been disbursed

What it means: Most sanctioned value had not been disbursed at the snapshot date, and the dashboard cannot say how much of that is normal lag.

What to do: Report disbursed and undisbursed value by sanction year so the construction lag can be measured rather than assumed.

Maharashtra accounts for the largest share of applications filed

What it means: Maharashtra's 2,261 applications lead the state counts, but the dashboard is a live snapshot that can shift.

What to do: Re-run the state and district counts on a fixed date and keep a revision history, so changes can be tracked.

What would fix this

  • The Department of Animal Husbandry and Dairying should add average and median sanction-to-first-disbursement time to the AHIDF dashboard, by lender and project type, so the 61 per cent undisbursed balance can be split into normal construction-linked lag and genuinely stalled loans.
  • SIDBI, which runs the AHIDF portal, should publish a dated revision history and monthly archived snapshots of the dashboard, so state, district and bank counts that currently shift without trace can be tracked over time and cited reliably.
  • The Department of Animal Husbandry and Dairying should publish a reconciliation between ministry-approved projects in its parliamentary answers and lender-sanctioned applications on the dashboard, so the 402-project figure of August 2025 and the 916 sanctions of August 2026 can be read on one basis.
  • The Department of Animal Husbandry and Dairying should publish the reasons applications fail at each funnel stage, coded as ineligible, rejected by lender, collateral, compliance or withdrawn, so the drop from 11,201 filed to 1,614 eligible is explained rather than inferred.
  • NABSanrakshan Trustee should publish annually the number and value of AHIDF loans covered by the 25 per cent guarantee and claims paid from the ₹750 crore fund, so the guarantee’s actual use can be checked against the credit-rationing case made for it.

These are proposals from this blog, not recommendations any agency has adopted.
