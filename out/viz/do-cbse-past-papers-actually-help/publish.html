CBSE past papers: useful for Accountancy and Physics, not English Core

Top line

  • 49% of Accountancy's high-mark marks sit on a recurring topic, against 0% for English Core.
  • 10 of 10 official sample-paper sessions since 2015-16 carry Physics's electric-field topic.

What it means: Repetition is a property of the subject, so Accountancy and Physics papers recur most while English Core's unseen passages never repeat.

What to do: Drill Accountancy and Physics from past papers by topic, and prepare English Core by format and timing rather than content.

Physics's field topics and Economics's aggregate demand recur in all 10 sessions

What it means: A decade of official sample papers repeats these three topics in every session, a longer-run check on the real-paper result.

What to do: Start Physics revision with the electric and magnetic field chapters, since they recur in both real and sample papers.

Accountancy's balance sheet recurs in all 7 sample sessions; partner admission in 2

What it means: The partner-cycle topic that dominates Accountancy's real papers shows up in only 2 of 7 sample papers, because a sample paper is a smaller slice of the syllabus.

What to do: Practise partnership admission and retirement questions from real past papers, since sample papers will not reliably show them.

What would fix this

  • CBSE should publish Hindi-medium question papers with Unicode-encoded text rather than legacy embedded fonts, so that Hindi-medium papers, which currently extract as unreadable characters, can be analysed alongside the English-medium ~54% of questions this piece was able to use.
  • CBSE should restore its archive of administered Class XII question papers from before 2022 on cbse.gov.in, in downloadable form, so that recurrence claims can be tested on real papers over a decade rather than relying on one sample paper per session as a proxy.
  • CBSE should release a machine-readable file with each paper listing every question’s marks, section and syllabus unit, so that marks no longer have to be parsed heuristically from paper text and any repetition index can be computed exactly rather than to within a few percentage points.
  • CBSE should publish a subject-wise statement each year of how high-mark questions were distributed across syllabus units in the main and compartment sittings, so that students get evidence on which subjects reward past-paper practice instead of uniform coaching advice that fits Accountancy and not English Core.

These are proposals from this blog, not recommendations any agency has adopted.
