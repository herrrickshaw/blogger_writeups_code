Every central bank runs monetary policy through the same two levers: the rate (what money costs) and the window (how much money gets through, and against what collateral). Commentary obsesses over the first and mostly ignores the second. That is exactly backwards for understanding India in 2025-26 — because in the current cycle, the Reserve Bank of India's quantity lever did more work than its rate lever. And the world's other big central banks now pull these two levers in almost opposite proportions to the RBI.

Fresh-loan rates fell 105bp of the 125bp cut, high pass-through by Indian standards

What it means: The 125bp of cuts reached fresh borrowers as 105bp, which the post calls unusually high pass-through for India.

What to do: Judge the 7 October 2026 hike by the window operations that follow it, not by the repo move alone.

The RBI's corridor is only 50bp wide, so the overnight rate signal is precise

What it means: The corridor is only 50bp wide, and the Bank Rate moves automatically with the repo rather than as an independent lever.

What to do: Watch how hard the RBI uses its VRRR window, since that decides how much of the 7 October hike reaches borrowers.

What would fix this

  • The Reserve Bank of India should publish with each monetary policy statement one table of net liquidity added or absorbed since the last meeting, tool by tool — CRR, OMOs, FX swaps, VRR and VRRR auctions and FX intervention — so the roughly ₹7.3 lakh crore of window operations can be read beside the rate decision from a primary source.
  • The Reserve Bank of India should report each month the rupee-liquidity effect of its foreign-exchange intervention alongside the intervention amount, so how far dollar sales drain the liquidity released through CRR cuts and bond purchases is measured rather than flagged by analysts.
  • The Reserve Bank of India’s Monetary Policy Committee statements should state the range of average daily liquidity surplus the Bank is operating towards, so a ‘hold’ on the repo rate accompanied by a ₹70,000 crore daily surplus is not read as a neutral setting.

These are proposals from this blog, not recommendations any agency has adopted.
