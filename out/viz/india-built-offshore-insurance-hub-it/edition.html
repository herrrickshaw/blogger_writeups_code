GIFT City's IFSC is marketed as an offshore insurance and reinsurance hub. Its own quarterly regulatory tables show it built one of those two things: across FY2024-25 it wrote $195.45 million of reinsurance premium against just $12.42 million of direct insurance — a book that is 94% reinsurance.

GIFT City insurance hub: 94% reinsurance, $12.42 million direct cover in FY2024-25

Top line

  • 94% of GIFT IFSC's FY2024-25 insurance premium is reinsurance, not direct insurance.
  • $2.54M of direct life insurance premium was written across the whole year.
Updated 7 November 2026. Newer IFSCA bulletin figures, reported by Reinsurance News, show direct business picking up in FY2025-26: IFSC Insurance Offices wrote $15.38 million of direct gross premium in January–March 2026 alone (up from $9.25 million in the previous quarter), more than the whole of FY2024-25’s $12.42 million. Reinsurance premium in the same quarter was $186.93 million (from $148.13 million), so reinsurance was still about 92% of the quarter’s book. The number of insurance offices rose to 36 in March 2026, from 22 in September 2025 and 24 in December 2025, and intermediary offices to 34. A full-year FY2025-26 total of $648.7 million has been reported in a headline but could not be checked. The tables below remain the FY2024-25 snapshot, and the title’s “$12 million a year” is that year’s figure.

What would fix this

  • IFSCA should publish a revised Jan–Mar 2025 insurance table once the reinsurance data of the IIO “under examination” is settled, with the change noted, so the year's reinsurance total and the 94 per cent share are complete rather than understated by an unknown amount.
  • IFSCA should release its quarterly bulletin tables as downloadable CSV files alongside the PDF, marking which figures are unaudited and when audited figures replace them, so the series can be checked without extracting text from a 4.6 MB document.
  • IFSCA should publish a note whenever it reclassifies a reinsurance line of business, such as the split between “Health + Personal Accident” and “Health Insurance”, with restated earlier quarters, so a reclassification is not mistaken for a collapse in business.
  • IFSCA should report direct premium written by IFSC Insurance Offices split by life and non-life, retail and corporate, and policyholder residence, so whether GIFT City is becoming a direct-insurance hub, beyond its $12.42 million year, can be tracked quarter by quarter.

These are proposals from this blog, not recommendations any agency has adopted.
