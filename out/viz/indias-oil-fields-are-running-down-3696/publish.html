India's crude oil output: 36.96 to 28.70 MMT in the decade to 2024-25

Skip to article content
Text Size

Top line

  • −22% crude output since 2015-16, from 36.96 MMT to 28.70 MMT in 2024-25.
  • 43.45% of India's crude came from Mumbai Offshore in FY2024-25.
  • 11.8% of domestic petroleum product consumption was made from indigenous crude in FY2024-25.
Updated 7 November 2026. Crude output fell again in 2025-26, to about 28.0 million tonnes (provisional), the eleventh straight annual decline, and import dependence for crude reached a record 88.7%, per a government reply to the Rajya Sabha reported by ThePrint. Gas output also fell in 2025-26, to about 34–35 billion cubic metres; reports give 34,326 and 34,776 million cubic metres on different bases. The tables below remain DGH’s 2024-25 data.

What would fix this

  • The Directorate General of Hydrocarbons should publish, with each year’s production targets, the field-wise decline assumptions and new-field contributions they are built on, so that a target missed by every operator and regime — 91.8% for oil, 91.0% for gas — can be traced to the assumption that failed.
  • The Directorate General of Hydrocarbons should publish annual water cut and decline rates for the largest producing fields, starting with Mumbai Offshore, so that the “natural decline and rising water cut” cited for the fall from 36.96 to 28.70 MMT is shown field by field rather than stated in general.
  • ONGC and Oil India should publish the incremental oil attributable to each improved and enhanced oil recovery project against its cost, so that whether IOR/EOR is slowing decline in matured nomination fields can be judged from results rather than assumed.
  • The Directorate General of Hydrocarbons should publish a block-wise production forecast for the Krishna Godavari deepwater blocks, since PSC gas rising from 7.84 to 13.38 BCM rests on a small number of wells and a forecast would show how long they can offset decline elsewhere.

These are proposals from this blog, not recommendations any agency has adopted.
