Top line

  • 37.8 GW of solar-cell capacity a year is now enlisted in ALMM List-II, from 19 company names running 21 units.
  • 2.9x growth since the first list of 31 July 2025, when enlisted capacity was 13.1 GW.
  • 67% of enlisted capacity sits in Gujarat, Tamil Nadu and Telangana together.
Updated 7 November 2026. A 13th revision of List-II has been reported since this piece was written: Energetica India says it added 7,063 MW from two new entrants, GREW Energy and Ampin Solar One (a joint venture of Ampin and Jupiter International, 1,160 MW of mono-PERC cells at Bhubaneswar), taking enlisted cell capacity to 44,858 MW (Energetica India). That is a single trade report, not checked against the MNRE memorandum, and other October coverage still gives 37.8 GW; the tables below remain on the 12th revision of 8 October.

Updated 15 November 2026: rewritten as charts; figures unchanged.

Enlisted cell capacity has roughly tripled in fourteen months

What it means: The list grew in steps, with the biggest jump at the third revision on 15 December 2025.

What to do: Check the current List-II revision before ordering cells for any project that commissions after 31 December 2026.

Twelve company names hold 86% of enlisted capacity

What it means: Concentration is high, so a single plant's ramp-up or delay moves the national picture.

What to do: Ask suppliers which listed unit will make their cells and whether that unit is already at full output.

Gujarat and Tamil Nadu host most of the enlisted factories

What it means: A developer in a state with no enlisted cell plant must buy from a neighbouring state.

What to do: Price cell sourcing from Gujarat, Tamil Nadu or Telangana before committing to a project site outside them.

Mono PERC still leads, but TOPCon is now 42% of the list

What it means: The list records manufacturer-claimed efficiency ranges, not tested values, so technology share is a capacity view only.

What to do: Ask for tested efficiency data on any cell line a project relies on, since the list does not carry it.
