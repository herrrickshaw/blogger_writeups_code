Between August 1995 and July 2026 the Rajya Sabha was asked 2,606 questions about fertiliser. Read as one series, the corpus shows a Parliament that has asked the same two questions for three decades — what does the subsidy cost, and why can we not make it here — and one plant, Namrup, that has been the subject of revival questions in every one of those 31 years.

What would fix this

  • The Rajya Sabha Secretariat should restore machine-readable answer text in its question database for every answer from 2021 onwards, so that the 578 fertiliser answers now held only as PDFs — and every other ministry’s — can be searched in bulk like those from 1999 to 2019.
  • The Rajya Sabha Secretariat should add a subject classification field to each question record, so that analysts are not left with keyword filters that, as here, pulled in 183 unrelated questions because ‘urea’ matches ‘bureau’.
  • The Department of Fertilizers should publish plant-wise production and capacity utilisation each year for the revived Talcher, Ramagundam, Gorakhpur, Sindri and Barauni units, so that what the revival produced is on record without a member having to ask.
  • The Department of Fertilizers should publish a dated milestone schedule for the new ammonia-urea plant at Namrup, so that progress on a plant raised in parliamentary questions in every year from 1995 to 2026 can be checked against stated targets.

These are proposals from this blog, not recommendations any agency has adopted.

Sources and caveats

All question data is from the Rajya Sabha question database, queried through its public search backend at rsdoc.nic.in, which returns for each question the member, ministry, question type, answer date, session number, question text and — for years up to 2020 — the full answer text. Three title filters were run (fertili, urea, nano), the results deduplicated on the question serial number, and the combined set then filtered to require a genuine fertiliser token in the title. That last step removed 183 false positives caused by the substring urea matching bureau — questions about the Narcotics Control Bureau, the Press Information Bureau, aircraft accident investigation and others, spread across eight unrelated ministries. The cleaned corpus is 2,606 questions answered between 4 August 1995 and 28 July 2026, of which 280 are starred and 2,326 unstarred. Yearly counts, the three-period theme shares, the plant mention counts and the 319 revival-language questions are all computed here from that corpus. Theme classification is keyword-based and is this article’s own; themes overlap by design, so shares do not sum to 100, and the classification measures what was asked about rather than what was established. The three ministerial answers quoted — December 2005 on Gorakhpur and Sindri, August 2011 on Ramagundam, March 2020 on the five-plant revival — are quoted from the answer text held in the database and are reproduced as they appear there, including the 12.7 lakh metric tonne per annum figure. The 1995 start is the beginning of the available archive, not the beginning of parliamentary interest in fertiliser; the six plants all appear in the earliest year, so their true question spans are longer than 31 years. 1995 and 2026 are partial years. 2020 counts are depressed by pandemic session disruption. Answer-text coverage figures were checked record by record; the 2021–2026 result of zero machine-readable answers is exact, while the year-by-year percentages for earlier periods were computed before the bureau clean and are therefore approximate against the 2,606 total. A parallel Lok Sabha corpus of 1,034 questions was harvested from the sansad.in question API for cross-checking but is not used for any figure in this article. Nothing here is policy, agricultural or investment advice.
