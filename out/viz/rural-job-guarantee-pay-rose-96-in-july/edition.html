What to do: Check the state rate calculations against the gazette before relying on any single state's figure.

What would fix this

  • The Ministry of Rural Development should publish, with every wage notification, the method used to set each state’s rate: the price index, the months it covers and any floor or cap applied.
  • Wage rates under VB-G RAM G should be revised against the latest twelve months of each state’s CPI-AL, so that a state with 9–12% price growth does not receive a rise of under 2%.
  • The ministry should publish its national average wage with the weights used, so figures such as Rs 298.8 and Rs 327.4 can be reproduced from the state rates.

These are proposals from this blog, not recommendations any agency has adopted.

What this piece could not establish

The 2025-26 rates and the Act’s start date were read in the Gazette of India’s own text through the SansadSaar index of the archive.org Gazette of India collection (S.O. 1463(E), CG-DL-E-27032025-262013; S.O. 2382(E), CG-DL-E-11052026-272413). The July 2026 wage notification, S.O. 3518(E), is not in that index, so the new rates come from news lists of it, which disagree for a few states. The price comparison uses each state’s overall CPI-AL; it does not account for the extra 25 days of work the new law guarantees, which raises what a household can earn without changing the daily rate. Whether workers in each state actually received the higher rate from 1 July, and how many days they worked, is not covered.
