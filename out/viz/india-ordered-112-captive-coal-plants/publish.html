Top line

  • ₹7.3 per unit average day-ahead exchange price in September, up from ₹4.88 in August: about +50%.
  • 269 GW peak demand on 10 September, the day buy bids on the exchange rose 281% year-on-year.
Updated 7 November 2026. October eased the shortage this piece describes without ending it. The IEX day-ahead average fell to ₹6.66/kWh in October from ₹7.3 in September, still 149% above October 2025’s ₹2.67, which IEX partly put down to a low base; the real-time market averaged ₹5.78 (Mercom, reporting IEX’s monthly update). Coal at power plants held at about 18.2 MT: the Ministry of Coal said stocks fell only about 0.06 MT a day in the first ten days of October, against 0.24 MT a day in September, as supply rose to 2.49 MT a day (The Hans India). No count of how much the 112 captive plants actually offered to the exchanges has been published.

Updated 15 November 2026: rewritten as charts; figures unchanged.

What to do: Check IEX's October figures, due in early November, to see whether the 25 September order has cooled prices.
