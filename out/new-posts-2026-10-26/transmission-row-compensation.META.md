## Worked-example arithmetic (illustrative; assumptions flagged in post)
Market Rs 70 lakh/ha (= ministry Illustration 1 result), circle Rs 40 lakh/ha (hypothetical).
- Pipeline 10%: 0.10x70 = 7.00; x40 = 4.00 lakh/ha
- Corridor rural 30%: 21.00 / 12.00; 45%: 31.50 / 18.00; 60%: 42.00 / 24.00
- Tower base 200%: 140.00 / 80.00 lakh/ha
- 400 kV D/C km: corridor 46 m x 1000 m = 4.6 ha; towers = 1000/400 = 2.5; footprint assumed 12 m square +1 m each side = 14x14 = 196 m2 -> 490 m2 = 0.049 ha.
  Market: 4.6x0.30x70 = 96.60; 0.049x2x70 = 6.86; total 103.46 lakh. Circle: 4.6x0.30x40 = 55.20; 0.049x2x40 = 3.92; total 59.12 lakh. Difference 44.34 lakh = 75.0%.
- Headline ratio: 30/10 = 3, 45/10 = 4.5, 60/10 = 6.
