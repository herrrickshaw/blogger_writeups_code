## CORRECTION to the commission-queue entry
The queue says projects "commissioned on or after 18 Jun 2026". The scanned OM of 18 Jul 2026 (viewed as an image, almm2.pdf) says **1 June 2026** ("1st June 2026"; OCR misread "1st" as "18"). 18 July is the OM date. The post uses 1 June. The 25 May OM (283/63/2025) and 6 Jul OM (283/34/2026) also say 1 June.
