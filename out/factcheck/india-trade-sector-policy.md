**INTERPRETATION ERRORS:**
- *"The migration away from production-linked design"* — The claim that PLI schemes are "migrating" from output-linked to design/employment incentives is **temporally and causally misaligned**. While newer schemes (e.g., **PLI for IT Hardware, Pharma, Food Processing**) do include design/employment components, the **core PLI model (output-linked incentives)** remains dominant. The "migration" narrative ignores that:
  1. **Output-linked incentives** still account for **~80% of total PLI outlays** (e.g., ₹50,000 cr for Pharma, ₹30,000 cr for Auto).
  2. Employment-linked incentives (ELI) are **separate schemes** (e.g., ₹1.5 lakh cr for ELI under MSTC) and not a direct replacement for PLI’s output focus.
  The article conflates **scheme diversification** with a systemic shift in PLI’s design. [confidence: high]
