- "Actual CBG sold to oil marketing companies in FY2024-25 was about 42,800 tonnes (roughly 79,200 tonnes cumulative since the scheme began in September 2019), against a rated capacity across the 160 operational plants implying about 323,000 tonnes/year — a utilisation rate of only around 13%..." — This calculation uses an apples-to-oranges comparison to measure capacity utilisation. The Perform, Achieve and Trade (PAT) scheme was a mandatory, regulatory energy-efficiency compliance market for large, energy-intensive heavy industries (such as thermal power, cement, and steel). Citing PAT's historical savings as a "scale marker" for what the Carbon Credit Trading Scheme's Offset Mechanism can achieve for CBG projects conflates a mandatory industrial compliance framework with a voluntary, project-based offset mechanism.
