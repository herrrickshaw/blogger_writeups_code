**INTERPRETATION ERRORS:**
- *"The winning pattern is proven: consumables went from import-dependent to net-export within a decade. The same playbook now needs to climb the value ladder..."* — **The "playbook" analogy conflates causality and scalability.** While consumables (e.g., gloves, sutures) achieved net-export status due to **low R&D barriers, labor-cost advantages, and regulatory tailoring**, high-value segments (e.g., lab instruments, IVDs) face **fundamental constraints**:
  1. **Knowledge gaps**: India’s domestic R&D in analytical chemistry or molecular diagnostics lags behind global leaders (e.g., Thermo Fisher, Roche).
  2. **Supply-chain lock-in**: Even if PLI funds assembly, **core components (e.g., X-ray tubes, MRI magnets) remain imported**, as noted in the article itself.
  3. **Demand elasticity**: Consumables have **inelastic, high-volume markets**; high-end devices require **niche expertise and patient affordability**, which India lacks.
  The claim overstates the **direct applicability of the consumables model** to complex, capital-intensive segments. [confidence: high]

- *"PLI for Medical Devices (₹3,420 cr, 2020) — four target segments; see the companion post for full coverage-vs-gap detail."* — **The ₹3,420 cr outlay is misleadingly presented as a "four-segment" allocation without transparency.** The article later notes that **SMDI’s components sub-scheme (opened June 2026) is the vehicle for Tier-3 parts**, implying the original PLI may have **underfunded or misaligned incentives** for critical gaps (e.g., lab instruments). The claim assumes the PLI’s structure is optimal without addressing:
  - Whether the **₹3,420 cr was evenly distributed** across the four segments (radiology, implants, consumables, diagnostics) or skewed toward easier-to-localize items.
  - How much of the budget was **actually disbursed vs. committed**, given past PLI delays (e.g., ₹6,000 cr PLI for semiconductors had ~₹1,000 cr disbursed by FY26).
  The article’s own data shows **lab instruments (₹2.19 bn deficit) and IVDs (₹1.05 bn) received no dedicated PLI focus**, yet these are the largest gaps. [confidence: high]
