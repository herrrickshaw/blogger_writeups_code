# 03 — ARUL Act (RoU) gazette notices, Jan 2025 – Oct 2026: which pipelines are being laid

Research date: 2026-10-23. Scope: Ministry of Petroleum and Natural Gas (MoPNG) notices under the Petroleum and Minerals Pipelines (Acquisition of Right of User in Land) Act, 1962 (ARUL Act), covering s.3(1) declarations of intent and s.6(1) declarations. The window is Jan 2025 – Oct 2026, plus late 2024 where a later notice cites it.

## Method and limits (read first)

- **I could not open any primary source.** egazette.gov.in and all *.gov.in sites are blocked here, and I did not try to get around that. WebFetch could not resolve the aggregators either (advocategandhi.com and gazettetracker.com both returned `ENOTFOUND`). Every item below therefore comes from **WebSearch result summaries** (29 of the 30-search budget used, plus 2 failed fetches). Those summaries are machine-written paraphrases of egazette PDFs and blog posts. They are not the documents themselves.
- **Tags:**
  - **[primary-via-summary]**: the search summary was paraphrasing an egazette.gov.in PDF or a gazettetracker entry.
  - **[press]**: news, company disclosures, or the advocategandhi.com / lawchronicle legal-commentary blogs. Several advocategandhi posts carry "AI-assisted" disclaimers, and their dates conflict with each other, so treat them as weak.
- **S.O. numbers:** these are given only where a summary stated them. Blank means none was given. I flag any number that appears with two different pipelines, or that breaks the date sequence.
- **What the ARUL stages mean:**
  - **s.3(1):** the Centre declares an intention to acquire RoU, followed by a 21-day objection window and survey. A new route is being sought, so laying work is coming.
  - **s.6(1):** the Competent Authority reports and the Centre declares the RoU acquired. Under s.6(4) the RoU can vest in the company instead of the Centre. Acquisition is finalised and laying is imminent or under way.
  - **Caution:** on several large lines, notices lag construction. The 2025 s.6(1) notices on Nagpur–Jabalpur were issued while GAIL reported the section mechanically complete (Sept 2025). Many 2025–26 notices are mop-up parcels, reroutes, or "supplementary" schedules on lines that are already mostly built. They are not all greenfield work.
- **Sanity check on S.O. numbering.** MoPNG Extraordinary S.O. numbers run in one series per year across all ministries, so they should rise with the date. The numbers collected here mostly do:
  - **2025:** 1532(E) Mar → 1713(E) Apr → 2504(E) 29 May → 2967(E) 30 Jun → 3574(E) 31 Jul → 4224(E) 17 Sep → 5079(E) 7 Nov → 5085(E) 11 Nov → 5490(E) 28 Nov → 5703(E) 4 Dec → 5737(E) 10 Dec → 6008(E)/6034(E) 23–24 Dec → 6109(E) 29 Dec.
  - **2026:** 120(E) 7 Jan → 167(E) 12 Jan → 471(E) 29 Jan → 1303(E) 11 Mar → 2247(E) 5 May → 2993(E) 11 Jun → 3026(E) 12 Jun → 3223(E) 18 Jun → 3667(E) 6 Jul.
  - Two numbers break the pattern. They are listed under Gaps and conflicts.

- **Nagpur–Jabalpur section (Madhya Pradesh):**
  - s.3(1) S.O. 3755(E), 27.08.2024 (published 03.09.2024). [primary-via-summary]
  - A 2025 egazette notice then moves these lands to s.6(1) and vests the RoU in GAIL. Its own S.O. number was not shown. [primary-via-summary]

    - Pandhurna district (Sausar tehsil)
    - Villages including Beesapur Kalan, Jamuniya Badi, Bothiya, Mohpani Mal and Rangari Thoka
  - One summary calls this schedule an "intends to acquire" notice (s.3(1)) and another calls it part of the s.6 step, so its section is unclear. [primary-via-summary]
- **Nagpur–Jharsuguda section (Maharashtra):**
  - A July 2025 egazette schedule lists Bhandara district, Tumsar taluka.
  - Its section was not visible in the summary. [primary-via-summary]
- **Chhattisgarh:**
  - A blog reports a notification dated **19 Dec 2025** under s.3(1) starting RoU acquisition in "several districts" of Chhattisgarh. No S.O. number was given, and the districts were not named. [press]
  - A May 2026 notice appointed a Chhattisgarh Joint Collector as Competent Authority for GAIL's gas pipeline projects in the state for two years. [primary-via-summary]
- **Construction status:**
  - GAIL's Reg-30 disclosure (24 Sept 2025) says the Nagpur–Jharsuguda part covering Odisha and Chhattisgarh (489 km) was mechanically complete.

  - So the Dec 2025 Chhattisgarh s.3(1) notice is most likely a reroute, spur or mop-up, not new trunk work.
