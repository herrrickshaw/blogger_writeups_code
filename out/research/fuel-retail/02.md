## 4. Retail price and E10 availability
- No evidence of an E20-linked retail price cut; E20 sold at same price as previous E10 in most cities (Business Today, 11 Jul 2026; 2+). NITI Aayog 2021 had suggested lower pricing (single).
- E10 not generally available: SC dismissed PIL for E0/E10 option 1 Sep 2025 (LiveLaw, Bar & Bench, Moneylife; 2+). Reports say from 1 Apr 2026 E20 is the only petrol type at pumps (single, Mint-based). Ministry Q&A 10 Jul 2026 ruled out E0/E10/E20 together; Mint reports talks on E10 in 95-octane premium (XP95, Speed, Power95), early stage, no decision; Business Today explainer 27 Aug 2026. Ethanol-free Octane 100 sells at about Rs 150-170/l (single).
