Method: 25 WebSearch calls (all "standard"). Every item below comes from a SEARCH SUMMARY only; no page was opened (WebFetch to dataful.in failed: DNS). Confidence: "2+" = two or more sources, "single" = one source, "unverified" = inferred or garbled. Nothing was read from ppac.gov.in or pib.gov.in.

Three margins kept apart: DEALER = commission per kL (petrol/diesel) or per kg (CNG) from the company; OMC/CGD = company margin; ETHANOL = supplier price. Most private/CNG figures below are company-level, not dealer-level.

## 0. Headline for the premise test (2026 context from this slice)
- 2026 is NOT a clean "margins improved" year. Sources describe a Middle East / Strait of Hormuz supply shock, crude about USD 108/bbl, PSU pump prices "unchanged since May" after ~Rs 7.50/litre of May hikes, and private retailers losing volume. (PSUWatch, PTI via DTNext/ThePrint; single-to-2 sources, summaries only.)
- Reliance Q1 FY27 (results around 17-19 Jul 2026): fuel retailing "reported under-recoveries"; SAED (special additional excise duty) reintroduced on diesel, petrol, ATF "adversely impacted" domestic margins (brokerage summary; single). So post-E20 margin gains, if any, are swamped in 2026 by crude/duty effects. Other agents should confirm with PSU Q1 FY27 results.

## 2. CNG stations
- Count: 7,395 (PNGRB, 30 Nov 2024, Lok Sabha annex https://eparlib.nic.in/bitstream/123456789/2974242/1/AU1097.pdf; summary); 6,907 (1 May 2024); minister quote ">8,150" undated (tribuneindia.com/news/business/india-to-double-cng-stations-to-18000-by-2030...). PPAC state-wise table as on 28 Feb 2026 exists (https://dataful.in/datasets/17467/, only Andhra Pradesh = 213 seen). National total for 2025-26: NOT FOUND. MWP target 18,336 by 2032.
- Throughput: MGL 3.496 mmscmd CNG, 519 stations, Q1 FY27 = ~6,700 scmd/station (my division; single source multibagg.ai/market-pulse/articles/mgl-q1fy27-96949). IGL CNG ~7.2 mmscmd (656.8 mn scm in quarter) but IGL station count not found, so no per-station figure.
- CGD company margins (not dealer margins): IGL EBITDA ~Rs 3.4/scm in Q1 FY27 vs Rs 6.2 a year earlier (guidance Rs 7); gas cost ~Rs 43.3/scm vs realisation ~Rs 52.1/scm. MGL EBITDA ~Rs 7.9/scm vs Rs 12.35 (guidance Rs 8-9); gas cost ~Rs 39.85/scm. (plindia.com IGL-15-8-26-PL.pdf; barodaetrade.com MGL note; summaries; 2 sources per company.) Per scm, not per kg.
- DEALER commission per kg: NOT FOUND for IGL, MGL or ATGL in 2026. Fragments only: PTI (year not shown) "dealers earn Rs 1.70/kg, federation wants Rs 2.50"; IGL pays dealers electricity at Re 0.79/kg (2001 study, old parliamentary reply, https://eparlib.nic.in/bitstream/123456789/565215/1/64350.pdf) - a cost reimbursement. Gujarat (IRM Energy-supplied pumps) dealer strike, ~65 pumps shut 19 Aug 2026: dealers say commission unchanged since 2020 while gas price rose from Rs 50-52 to ~Rs 89/kg (https://deshgujarat.com/2026/08/19/over-60-cng-pumps-shut-in-2-gujarat-districts-as-dealers-go-on-strike/; single). Read as: fixed Rs/kg commission, so price rises do not lift it.
- 2026 CNG prices (Rs/kg): Delhi 77.09 (start of year) -> ~80.09 (mid-May) -> 83.09 (26 May) -> 86.98 (29 Aug), IGL, Hormuz/LNG cost cited; Mumbai ~Rs 84-86 after +Rs 2 in late May; Ahmedabad (ATGL) Rs 94.02 after +Rs 4 on 1 Aug, cumulative +Rs 12 in 2026 (outlookbusiness, thehitavada.com/Encyc/2026/5/18/cng-prices-hiked-by-re-1-.html, businesstoday.in 2026-05-26, bajajbroking.in, sahi.com; 2+ sources for Delhi). Sources conflict on exact intermediate Delhi steps (Rs 6 in four instalments vs the Rs 3.89 August step).
- Station effect: CGD margins fell (above); dealer commission fixed; volume risk from price rises (Ahmedabad auto-rickshaw strike 5 Aug 2026). No source quantifies station margin effect.

## 4. Licensing rules and new outlets
- 2019 policy (resolution 8 Nov 2019; announced Oct 2019): non-oil entities need net worth Rs 250 crore (Rs 500 crore for both retail and bulk) instead of the earlier Rs 2,000 crore investment; commit to 100 retail outlets, 5% of them in notified remote areas within 5 years; alternative-fuel infrastructure within 3 years; Rs 25 lakh fee, Rs 5 crore bank guarantee; petrol and diesel only (madhyamam, itln.in, psuwatch.com, rsdebate.nic.in summaries; 2+). Note: the "5%" is remote-area outlets; I found NO "10% land" rule and no land-percentage criterion - the brief's "5 or 10 percent land and net-worth" wording is unconfirmed.
- Enacted since: 2021 authorisations to Reliance-bp Mobility, IMC group and 5 others (businesstoday.in, Jul 2021).
- Proposed/under review: Aug 2025 four-member expert committee (chair Sukhmal Jain, ex-BPCL) to re-examine 2019 norms; 14-day public comment from 6 Aug 2025 (etvbharat.com/en/!business/government-to-review-petrol-pump-licensing-norms-enn25081001144). Any 2026 outcome: NOT FOUND.
- New outlets: PSU OMCs advertised 49,964 dealership locations in Jun-Jul 2023; LoIs >23,000; >5,200 operational at 1 Jul 2025; PSU network 88,526 outlets, 3,095 temporarily non-operational (Lok Sabha reply via kashmirlife.net; single). Annual commissioning figure for FY26: NOT FOUND. Nayara adds ~365/yr (see above).
