# Filings check 01: Indian Oil Corporation (IOC)

Date of check: 16 Oct 2026. Method: WebSearch only (23 of 25 searches used). Company sites, BSE and NSE were not opened. What follows is what search summaries SAY the filings say. Where a result quoted the company's own release text (iocl.com NewsDetails pages), it is marked "company release text seen in snippet". Many Q1 FY27 write-ups are from auto-generated aggregators (sahi.com, multibagg, askfuzz, univest, psuconnect), so they are treated as weak.

## 2. Marketing margin: what IOC itself says

- IOC's Q1 FY27 release and results coverage blame high crude prices (West Asia conflict) that were not passed on to consumers (frozen retail prices). No IOC-stated per-litre marketing margin was found for FY24, FY25, FY26 or Q1 FY27. The company releases for FY24 said profit rose "mainly on account of higher marketing margins". The FY25 release attributed the fall to weaker refining and marketing margins.
- Per-litre figures found are all analyst estimates, not filings. They are NOT IOC-specific:
  - Emkay Research (reported 7 Oct 2026, newkerala.com): Q1 FY27 petrol about -Rs 12 per litre, diesel about -Rs 32 per litre. Q2 FY27 petrol +Rs 2.9, diesel -Rs 16.7.
  - JM Financial (23 Sep 2026): combined petrol and diesel margin about Rs 2.4 per litre in Q1.
  - Tribune/IANS (BPCL and HPCL write-up): negative margins of Rs 25.2 per litre on diesel and Rs 7.7 per litre on petrol.
  - Motilal Oswal pre-Q4 FY26 forecast: gross marketing margin about Rs 2 per litre. This is a forecast.
- Under-recoveries and policy (news and brokers, not IOC filings): excise cut of Rs 10 per litre on petrol and diesel (March timing not confirmed); retail price rises on 15, 19, 23 and 25 May 2026 (Kotak Neo). Kotak Neo says petrol under-recovery fell to about Rs 3 per litre from Rs 24 on 1 Apr 2026, and diesel to Rs 27 from Rs 105. Prabhudas Lilladher (Q4 FY26): "stable gross marketing margins in Jan and Feb 2026". Management did not disclose Q4 or FY26 GRM, citing West Asia volatility.
- SAED effect: IOC GRM of $15.59 per bbl is net of SAED. Single-source commentary says excluding SAED it would be about $36 per bbl. Unverified.
- Q2 FY27: not yet reported. Expect late Oct to Nov 2026.

## 4. Network, E20, ethanol, dealer commission, compensation

- Retail outlets: 40,221 ROs at end FY25, with 2,823 added in the year (annual report 2024-25, iocl.com/uploads/Annual_Report-2024-25/chairmans-perspective.html). FY26: 2,635 new ROs added and 909 on national highways, per the investor presentation and a broker note. The total of "42818" appeared next to "31-Mar-26" in a garbled PDF snippet and is unverified.
- Sales per retail outlet and throughput per outlet: not found.
- E20 outlets for IOC: not found. The annual report says E20 was rolled out nationally in Dec 2024 and 17.2% blending was achieved in 2024-25. Older IOC releases say about 12,000 outlets and about 7,000 outlets (different dates).
- Ethanol purchases: public sector OMCs spent Rs 73,996 crore in ESY 2024-25 (Rs 48,757 crore in ESY 2023-24), per the government's reply in Parliament via press. This matches the draft's "about Rs 74,000 crore" but it is a combined PSU OMC figure from a government source, not an IOC filing. IOC's own share, litres and average price were not found. OMC intake was about 10.3 billion litres in ESY 2024-25 (qcintel, summary only).
- Dealer commission: IOC announced on 29 Oct 2024, effective 30 Oct 2024, an increase of Rs 0.65 per litre on petrol and Rs 0.44 per litre on diesel, with no retail price change (outlookbusiness.com and psuwatch.com). Base dealer pay was Rs 1,868.14 per kl plus 0.875% on petrol and Rs 1,389.35 per kl plus 0.28% on diesel. No Rs crore cost found. Press called it the first revision in nearly eight years, and a Rajya Sabha reply refers to a prior revision in Aug 2017. So "revised once since 2016" holds only if 2017 is ignored. Safer wording: "revised in Aug 2017 and again on 30 Oct 2024".
- Government compensation: the Cabinet approved Rs 30,000 crore for IOC, BPCL and HPCL on 8 Aug 2025 for domestic LPG losses. A Rs 14,486 crore figure (12 monthly instalments from Nov 2025) also appears. IOC's own share was not found. Q1 FY27 recognised Rs 3,621.51 crore. A brokerage estimate puts three-OMC Q1 FY27 LPG under-recoveries at about Rs 13,700 crore after Rs 7,500 crore compensation (askfuzz.ai, single, weak).

## Gaps and cautions

- No filing PDF or transcript was opened. The Q1 FY27 earnings call transcript, investor presentation and annual-report tables (sales per RO, E20 outlets, ethanol litres and price) were not reached.
- IOC states no per-litre marketing margin. The draft's Rs 6-12 per litre and the negative Q1 FY27 margins rest on analysts. The estimates conflict across brokers (Emkay -12 and -32 vs BPCL/HPCL figures -7.7 and -25.2).
- Standalone versus consolidated is a recurring trap: Rs 2,661 crore (standalone), Rs 1,141 crore or Rs 1,631 crore (consolidated).
- Aggregator sites carry auto-generated numbers that differ by Rs 1 crore and more.
- Syndicated spam domains duplicated the Kotak Neo Q4 FY26 story and were ignored.
