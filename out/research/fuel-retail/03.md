# 03. E20 at the retail outlet (research notes, 16 Oct 2026)

Method: 25 WebSearch calls (all "standard"). Every URL below was seen only as a search-result link plus its summary. No page was opened (WebFetch not used). So "confidence" is for the search summary, not a read of the primary document. No primary PPAC/PIB/Sansad page was seen.

Who earns what (kept apart): DEALER = fixed commission per kL set by the OMC. OMC = marketing margin. Ethanol supplier = ethanol price. Nothing found below links E20 to any change in the dealer commission.

## 2. Dealer-side costs and complaints (all claims, little hard data)

- Dealers say E20 absorbs moisture; above about 0.5% water ethanol separates; underground tanks built for petrol, coastal and monsoon risk (indiaipo.in/news/detail/petrol-pump-owners-claim-ethanols-moisture-absorbing-nature-is-contaminating-fuel; outlookindia.com/national/e20-fuel-petrol-pump-owners-moisture-contamination-concerns-e20-ethanol). Dealers asked for non-ethanol petrol at least in monsoon (cartoq.com demand piece). Dealers complain OMCs give no specialised tanks. These are complaints, not costed.
- Telangana dealers' association circular (Aug 2025): water in a vehicle tank is not the pump's responsibility (cartoq.com).
- OMCs and FADA reject unique contamination; OMCs say 100+ refinery samples had chloride at or below 1 ppm vs limit 3 ppm; water-ingress tests 8 to 12 times a day at 87,000+ outlets (autocarindia.com/industry-amp/omcs-reject-e20-fuel-contamination-claims-after-nationwide-testing-440441; etvbharat.com/en/technology/omcs-monitor-e20-fuel-quality-regularly-tests-conducted-up-to-12-times-per-day-govt-enn26080405005).
- SIAM letter of 28 Jul 2026 on injector, fuel-pump, EGR failures (SIAM later said some figures needed verification). Ministry sources: petrol sales stopped at 2,573 outlets as of 25 Aug 2026 (IOC 1,257, HPCL 915, BPCL 401), unnamed sources, not an official release (autocarindia.com/industry-amp/govt-cracks-down-on-e20-contamination-petrol-sales-stopped-at-2573-pumps-440594). That is about 2.6% of 99,281 outlets (my arithmetic); sales suspension is an income hit to those dealers, size not found.
- Dealers want government/OMCs to pay for mandated E20 testing; say the water test ruins fuel and causes daily loss per pump (newsbytesapp.com/news/auto/fuel-dealers-in-india-seek-payment-for-mandated-e20-testing/tldr). Single source.
- Odisha Petroleum Dealers Association (6 Jul 2026): asked to reconsider E20 (fallback E10), customer blame; says OMCs promised in 2024 six-monthly margin revisions and "dealer margin has not increased" since 2024 (zeenews.india.com/mobility/petrol-dealers-in-odisha-seek-review-e-20-rollout-cite-vehicle-issues-and-pending-margin-revision-3059649.html; rushlane.com/petrol-dealers-association-urges-govt-to-reconsider-e20-rollout-12550537.html). 3 summaries, one ANI-sourced interview; claim not independently confirmed.
- Not found: any OMC compensation scheme for dealers; any quantified tank-cleaning, filter, dispenser replacement or shelf-life/shrinkage cost; any dealer commission tied to ethanol. (Nepal 2017 shrinkage material is irrelevant.)

## 3. Dealer commission: the premise test

- Last known revision: effective 30 Oct 2024, IOC; about +Rs 0.65/litre petrol and +Rs 0.44/litre diesel (about Rs 650 and Rs 440 per kL, my conversion), first since 5 Oct 2016, after litigation was resolved. The 2016 base: petrol Rs 1,868.14/kL + 0.875% of billable price; diesel Rs 1,389.35/kL + 0.28% (outlookbusiness.com/news/oil-companies-hike-dealer-commission-no-change-in-petrol-diesel-price; deccanherald.com/business/oil-companies-hike-commission-paid-to-petrol-pump-dealers-no-change-in-fuel-prices-3254051). 2+ sources. The revised per-kL rate was not stated in any summary.
- That revision is 18 months after the Feb 2023 E20 launch and was a litigation settlement, not an ethanol benefit. No 2025-26 revision found (a 2026 Indian revision search returned only Pakistan). So dealer margins did NOT visibly improve because of E20; the premise fails for the dealer. Any OMC-margin story belongs to another block.

## 4. Throughput, E10 and premium

- Industry petrol: Apr to Aug FY26 17.78 MT, +6.44% on 16.71 MT; Aug 2025 3.53 MT, +5.05% (knnindia.co.in, citing PPAC). IOC 9M FY26 domestic sales +4.3% vs industry +4.1% (iocl.com/NewsDetails/59419); IOC FY26 domestic sales 88.97 MMT (all products). Full-year FY26 petrol and per-outlet throughput: not found.
- Mileage penalty: government says 1 to 6% (3 to 5% typical, older vehicles) and rejects "30%" claims; LocalCircles (50,000+ owners) found about half of pre-2023 petrol owners report lower mileage (businesstoday.in, 26 May 2026). A 3 to 5% penalty could lift litres only if it were fully offset; no evidence isolates a mileage-driven volume gain from the underlying ~5 to 6% growth. Not shown.
- July 2026 FADA: petrol car sales dipped on E20 hesitation, diesel share of new cars up from 16.32% to 17.73% (business-standard.com/industry/auto/e20-effect-petrol-car-market-share-dips-cng-leads-alternative-fuel-charge-126080602126_1.html). Single source; a forward risk to petrol volumes.
- Premium grades: not found: XP95/Speed/Power share or margin per litre. XP95 priced Rs 101.89/litre after a Rs 2 rise in Mar 2026 (outlookbusiness.com). Premium 95-octane grades also contain E20 (Deccan Herald summary); an RTI-based Aaj Tak item says XP95 is E20 and XP100 is 0% but notes conflicting statements: unverified.

## 5. E85, E100, E27, flex fuel

- E85: launched 5 Jun 2026 at an IOC Delhi outlet, 48 outlets at start; targets 500 by Dec 2026 and 5,000 by Dec 2027 (swarajyamag.com/amp/story/infrastructure/india-launches-e85-fuel-at-48-outlets-plans-expansion-to-5000-stations-by-december-2027; indianinfrastructure.com/2026/07/03/mopng-launches-e85-fuel/). Targets are proposals.
- E100: March 2024 launch at 183 IOC outlets in 5 states; "over 400 outlets" (Puri, undated); plan of 5,000 E100 stations within two years (biofuelsdigest.com/india-launches-e100/; en.meroauto.com). Current count not found; sources conflict.
- E27: no retail pilot found; only the excise exemption.

## 6. Brazil (brief)

E30 mandatory from 1 Aug 2025 (from 27.5%). MME projected pump gasoline down up to R$0.13/litre; market expected 1 to 2 centavos. ANP: average resale (station) margin on gasoline up 97% Jan 2021 to Jun 2025; MME asked CADE and ANP to probe margins. No pre/post-E30 station margin series found, so no E30 effect on station margins can be attributed (agenciainfra.com/blog/mme-sustenta-projecao-de-queda-na-gasolina-com-e30-e-fala-em-aumento-de-r-02-no-diesel-com-b15-ante-fim-de-julho/; spglobal.com refined-products 080125 piece). Resale margin rise predates E30.
