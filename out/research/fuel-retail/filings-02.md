Specific checks asked for:
- BPCL EBITDA loss about Rs 41 bn and net loss about Rs 40 bn: CONFIRMED as STANDALONE (EBITDA -Rs 40.8 bn, PAT -Rs 39.6 bn per PL; 2+ sources). Not the consolidated figure.
- HPCL EBITDA loss Rs 161 bn: NOT too large. It is consistent with the pre-tax loss of Rs 17,446 crore (Rs 174 bn) once depreciation and interest are added back. PL and Baroda both give Rs 161 bn. Nomura's pre-results guess was Rs 13,900 crore, so reported was worse for HPCL than that guess.
- Duplication: headline figures were NOT duplicated between BPCL and HPCL (different profit, EBITDA, GRM, volumes). But one aggregator article mixes HPCL and BPCL figures, and Nuvama's per-litre "diesel -Rs 25.2, petrol -Rs 7.7" is attributed to BPCL by Tribune/Outlook and to HPCL by another summary; Jefferies (via Reuters) gives petrol -Rs 10.6 and diesel -Rs 18.4. Treat per-product per-litre numbers as unattributed analyst estimates. The draft's "negative in Q1 FY27" holds, but company-stated per-litre margins were not found.
- Company did not quantify fuel-sales losses in Rs per litre in what I saw; it said loss was due to "suppressed marketing margin on certain petroleum products", partly offset by refining.
- IOC Rs 2,661 crore loss also appears in Outlook Business comparison (consistent with draft; not my slice).

## 3. Policy and costs
- Dealer commission: effective 30 Oct 2024, +65 paise/litre petrol, +44 paise/litre diesel; first revision since 2016 (Rs 1,868.14/kL + 0.875% petrol; Rs 1,389.35/kL + 0.28% diesel). No company-disclosed annual Rs crore cost found. (outlookbusiness, theweek; 2+)
- March 2026 excise cut: SAED on petrol cut from Rs 13 to Rs 3/litre, diesel Rs 10 to nil; notification 26 Mar 2026; export duties raised (diesel Rs 21.5, ATF Rs 29.5/litre). Quoted under-recoveries then: about Rs 26/litre petrol, Rs 81.90/litre diesel. Diesel duty detail is disputed in one source. (moneylife, drishtiias, outlookindia)
- May 2026: four price rises, petrol +Rs 7.38/litre, diesel +Rs 7.52/litre in total; started 15 May (Outlook Business; 2 sources). Companies' own quantification of the excise/price-rise effect: not found.
- E20: BPCL 4,279 E20 outlets (18% of its network) around Sep 2024 (bioenergytimes); PSU-wide 16,059 of 82,617 on 1 Sep 2024; BPCL blending 16.35% in FY25. Current E20 outlet counts and ethanol purchase Rs crore from company filings: not found.
- Ethanol: ESY 2024-25 CHM ex-mill price Rs 57.97/litre (from 56.58); OMC contracts 997 crore litres vs 673 crore litres supplied ESY 2023-24 (Triveni annual report, via search); blending 18.36% at 31 Mar 2025. Rs 74,000 crore NOT confirmed by any filing; 997 crore litres x about Rs 58-72 is a rough check only, not found stated.
