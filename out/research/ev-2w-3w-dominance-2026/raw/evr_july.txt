Note: Low speed e-2W sales data not included. Electric vehicle sales in India registered mixed category-wise trends in July 2026 compared with June. Electric two-wheeler (E2W) sales increased to 204,201 units from 194,951, while E3W L5 Passenger sales rose to 40,198 from 34,593. E-rickshaw sales also increased to 34,716 from 32,119, and E3W L5 Cargo/Goods registrations grew to 4,860 from 4,405. E-Goods Carrier sales climbed to 2,977 from 2,614, while the Others category increased to 171 from 56. On the other hand, E4W sales declined slightly to 32,609 from 33,334, E-bus sales fell to 548 from 732, and E-cart registrations remained largely stable, easing marginally to 7,277 from 7,303.
