# 03 - ESY 2025-26 OMC ethanol tender: key terms (read from the document)

Source: tender ID 22376, ref 1000442332 (C-1), dated 23.09.2025, 85 pages; copy uploaded by the user (file name "OMCs-invite-bids-for-around-1050-crore-litres-of-ethanol-for-ESY-2025-26", as attached to ChiniMandi's report https://www.chinimandi.com/esy-2025-26-omcs-invite-bids-for-around-1050-crore-litres-of-ethanol/).

- Quantity ~1,050 cr L; requirement Q1-Nov 100, Q1-Dec/Jan 200, Q2 280, Q3 250, Q4 220. Pre-bid 26.09.2025; due 07.10.2025; bids valid to 31-07-2026.
- Feedstocks: cane juice/sugar/syrup, B-heavy, C-heavy, damaged food grain, FCI surplus rice, maize.
- Rates (Rs/KL basic): juice 65,610; B-heavy 60,730; C-heavy 57,970 (ESY 2024-25 rates continued until revised); DFG 64,000; FCI rice 60,320; maize 66,070 + incentive Rs 5.79/L.
- FCI rice: reserve price Rs 2,320/qtl ex-godown; 52 LMT 1 Nov 2025 - 30 Jun 2026; ~234 cr L max; excess cut pro rata from Q3 backwards; no rice in Q4. Bid validation: FCI rice >= 40% of each bidder's grain offer in Q1-Q3.
- Allocation (Annex III): zones (NE states; Punjab/Chandigarh; TN/Puducherry; Gujarat/DNH-DD; J&K/Ladakh; others standalone). Deficit zones: all offers accepted. Surplus zones: first preference to NFCSF cooperative sugar mills and LTOA quantity of commissioned DEPs (best endeavour, prorated quarterly); balance by feedstock priority sugar/syrup/juice -> B-heavy & C-heavy -> all other feedstocks; then surplus-zone balance offers fill deficit zones pro rata. UP separate. Optimizer (Accenture, in IEMS, LP): min 100 KL per vendor-location, min 3 vendors per cluster, max 40% of cluster per vendor, min 2 clusters per vendor. Location-wise allocation published only for Q1 periods.
- Capacity: total offer within licensed capacity / IEMS-declared max KL/day. Min offer 100 KL.
- Transport: 0-75 km Rs 306/KL; Rs 2.24/KL/km beyond; >1200 km Rs 3.54/KL/km; diesel benchmark Rs 90.51/L (10.09.2025); +10% for J&K, HP, NE states.
- SD 3% of basic LOA value incl. incentive; PRC 3% on shortfall below 95%; <50% in a quarter = non-performance; JRC reply in 7 days, max 3 rejections; payment 21 days; additional qty 10% (min 500 KL).
