# 01 - ESY 2026-27 OMC ethanol tender: key terms (read from the document)

Source: BPCL e-tender 26985, CRFQ/ref 1000466894 (C-1), dated 16.10.2026, https://bpcltenders.eproc.in/Product/LiveDoc/BPCLDocs/TenderDocuments/26985.pdf

- Quantity: around 1,100 crore litres; quarter-wise 300 / 300 / 250 / 250 (Nov-Jan, Feb-Apr, May-Jul, Aug-Oct). Pre-bid 21.10.2026; due 29.10.2026 11:00; bid validity to 31.10.2027.
- Annex I para 7: GoI directed sugar diversion for ethanol reduced to zero for ESY 2026-27; no offers sought from sugarcane juice / sugar / sugar syrup / B-heavy molasses. New category "Other feedstock(s) as per NPB" (sugar beet, sweet sorghum, cassava, rotten potatoes, etc.).
- Prices (basic, Rs/KL): C-heavy 57,970; damaged food grains 64,000; surplus FCI rice 60,320; maize 66,070 + incentive Rs 2.79/L; other NPB feedstocks 57,970. Transport and GST 5% extra.
- FCI rice: MoPNG directive, 72 LMT (324 cr L) to 30.06.2027; allocation capped at 108 cr L per quarter in Q1-Q3; no rice offers in Q4.
- Allocation (Annex III): preference to (i) cooperative sugar mills registered with NFCSF and (ii) DEPs with valid LTOAs up to Clause 6.2 annual offtake (lowest ex-mill price first, commissioning certificate in IEMS required); balance in ascending ex-mill price order, with EOI-1 DEPs (CRFQ 1000374174, 28.09.2021) preferred on "best endeavour" basis up to EOI design capacity, rest pro rata. UP allocated separately. Optimiser: min 40 KL per vendor-location, min 3 bidders per cluster, max 40% of a cluster to one vendor, min 2 clusters per vendor.
- Offer caps: CTO capacity x days (Q1 83, Q2 81, Q3 83, Q4 83); min 120 KL per feedstock per quarter.
- Declarations: CTO capacity, feedstock-group CTO, FCI rice end-use, own production / non-trading, EOI registration extension to 31.10.2027, integrity pact, holiday listing, land-border restriction.
- Security deposit 3% of basic LOA value (BG or invoice retention; BGs from ESY 24-25 roll over; validity 30.11.2027, claim 31.05.2028). Payment within 21 days. Diversion between locations/OMCs at OMC discretion. Additional quantity up to 10% (min 500 KL). Feedstock conversion paid at lower price. PRC 3% of ex-mill price on shortfall; no PRC in a month with >=95% of mandatory supply; <50% in a quarter = non-performance.
- Transport: slabs from Rs 311/KL (0-75 km); benchmark diesel RSP Rs 98.08/L (four-metro average, 30.09.2026); quarterly revision if diesel moves >=2%.
- Disclaimer: all allocations provisional, subject to WP 109133 of 2025 (GM-RES), SLP(C) 22411 of 2026 (lead) and others, transferred cases from Bombay HC Nagpur bench (WP 2298/2026) and Patna HC (CWJC 3296/2026), the interim arrangement approved 04.08.2026, and proceedings on Common Quantity Bid Tender 22376 dated 23.09.2025.
- Quality: IS 15464:2022; total chloride < 3 mg/kg. Feedstock certification per DFPD OM of 21.12.2023; self-declaration mandatory for C-heavy and other NPB feedstocks.
