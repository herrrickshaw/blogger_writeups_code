## Outcome of ESY 2025-26 tender

- **Offers: 1,776.49 crore litres.** 1,304.86 came from grain and 471.63 from sugarcane feedstocks. BPCL separately cited 1,759 as cumulative offers. Sources: iamrenew https://www.iamrenew.com/green-energy/omcs-get-offer-for-1776-cr-litres-ethanol-while-require-just-1050-cr-esy-25-26/ and bioenergytimes https://bioenergytimes.com/esy-2025-26-omcs-receive-1776-crore-litres-offer-for-ethanol-supply-against-requirement-of-1050-crore-litres/ (Oct 2025, press).
- **Allocation: about 1,048 crore litres to 378 bidders.** BPCL in court said about 1,050. Source: bioenergytimes / ChiniMandi, https://bioenergytimes.com/ethanol-supply-year-2025-26-omcs-allocate-around-1048-crore-litres-for-ethanol-supply/ (late Oct to Dec 2025, press).
- **By feedstock** (ChiniMandi, as relayed by bioenergytimes):

  | Feedstock | Share | Crore litres |
  |---|---|---|
  | Maize | 45.68% | 478.9 |
  | FCI rice | 22.25% | 233.3 |
  | Cane juice | 15.82% | 165.9 |
  | B-heavy molasses | 10.54% | 110.5 |
  | Damaged food grain | 4.54% | 47.6 |
  | C-heavy molasses | 1.16% | 12.2 |
- **By sector.** Sugar routes got 289 crore litres (about 27.5–28%) and grain got 760 crore litres (72%), according to ISMA and the Lok Sabha reply. ISMA says sugar-based distillery capacity is 838 crore litres and that the sector could supply more than 500 crore litres. Sources: tribuneindia https://www.tribuneindia.com/news/business/isma-warns-of-payment-delays-to-farmers-as-ethanol-cuts-frozen-sugar-prices-threaten-industry-stability ; https://bioenergytimes.com/isma-seeks-clarity-on-beyond-e-20-roadmap-amid-lower-ethanol-allocation/ (Oct–Nov 2025).
- **Cooperative share in litres: not found.**
- **Controversies:**
  - ISMA complained that grain took most of the allocation, and that juice and B-heavy prices stayed frozen while cane FRP rose 16.5% since 2022-23, leaving a gap of about Rs 5/L. ISMA puts juice cost at Rs 70.70/L against the Rs 65.61 price.
  - DEPs objected to the cooperative-first priority. Bharat Plus Ethanol complained to the IOCL chairman. In Bihar, quotas were reportedly cut about in half and some plants shut in Dec 2025 (Mongabay, Feb 2026).
  - VINP Distilleries (a DEP) won a Karnataka HC order on 16 Jun 2026 for a higher LTOA-based allocation. On 30 Jun 2026 the Supreme Court ordered status quo on BPCL's appeal (https://www.barandbench.com/amp/story/news/litigation/supreme-court-orders-status-quo-on-karnataka-hc-direction-to-revisit-ethanol-allocation-after-bpcl-says-it-will-affect-e20-fuel-programme). Separately, an ISMA statement in BS (URL dated 12 Mar 2026) says the SC set aside an earlier Karnataka HC preference order. The two court events are not reconciled.
  - Plants commissioned under EOI-2 got no orders because no Cycle 2 tender was floated (blog archive).
- **Q4 top-up.** On 4 Aug 2026 the SC allowed OMCs to award an extra **149 crore litres** to Q4 bidders. That was about 55.5 crore litres to 80 DEPs and about 93.3 to 122 other bidders, bringing the total to about 1,197 crore litres. Sources: BS https://www.business-standard.com/industry/news/supreme-court-allows-omcs-additional-ethanol-procurement-esy-2025-26-126080500241_1.html ; LiveLaw https://www.livelawbiz.com/top-stories/oil-marketing-companies-propose-additional-149-crore-litres-ethanol-allocation-for-fourth-quarter-before-supreme-court-546047 ; blog archive. **No separate second-cycle tender was found.**
- **Deliveries:**
  - About 680 crore litres by 18 Jun 2026 (BPCL in SC).
  - 718 crore litres by 30 Jun 2026, 83% of Q1–Q3 contracted volume (blog archive / ChiniMandi).
  - 810.67 crore litres from Nov 2025 to Jul 2026, 68% of it grain.
  - 895 crore litres by end-Aug 2026, including maize 345 and FCI rice 214 (AIDA, https://www.autocarpro.in/news/india-ethanol-supplies-reach-895-crore-litres-by-august-end-134667).
  - Surplus FCI rice supplied for ethanol: 44.2 LMT by 30 Jun 2026 (Lok Sabha via press).

## Reported plans for ESY 2026-27

- **FCI rice for distilleries.** Fixed at **Rs 2,390/qtl for Nov 2026 to Jun 2027** under OMSS(D), up from Rs 2,320 (Rajya Sabha reply, BS 29 Jul 2026, URL above). Press search did not find the matching ethanol price for FCI rice in ESY 2026-27.
- **Cane-based ethanol prices.** In May 2026, sugar bodies petitioned for higher juice, syrup and B-heavy prices and a higher sugar MSP for ESY 2026-27. **No revision was found as of the search date.** Sources: https://cropgpt.ai/indian-sugar-industry-calls-for-comprehensive-revision-in-ethanol-procurement-prices ; https://bioenergytimes.com/government-considering-increasing-domestic-ethanol-prices-media-report/ (a "considering" report, not a decision).
- **Sugar context.**
  - Cane FRP for 2026-27 is Rs 365/qtl.
  - 2025-26 output was revised to 30.6 Mt from 34.3 Mt.
  - Retail sugar rose from Rs 48.18 to Rs 55.70/kg between Jul and Aug 2026.
  - The government imposed a dealer stock cap and allowed 1 Mt of duty-free raw imports. It says diversion fell to about 9% of sugar in 2025-26.
  - Mills may divert less in ESY 2026-27.
  - Sources: https://www.deccanherald.com/india/govt-rejects-ethanol-diversion-claims-as-sugar-prices-surge-4119645 ; https://bioenergytimes.com/rising-sugar-prices-may-reduce-ethanol-production-as-mills-favour-sugar-sales/
- **Blending roadmap.**
  - E21 is under consideration for 2027 and E25 possibly by 2029, with a possible 25% cap (Business Today, 7 Jul 2026, https://www.businesstoday.in/amp/auto/story/amid-e20-debate-centre-evaluating-phased-rollout-of-e25-petrol-by-2029-541326-2026-07-07). Not a decision.
  - Petrol with E22–E30 blends is exempted from excise duty if BIS-compliant, and E22/E25/E27/E30 standards were reportedly notified in May 2026 (https://www.autocarindia.com/car-news-amp/e22-e25-e27-and-e30-fuels-exempted-from-central-excise-duty-439939).
- **Cooperative priority, sugar diversion cap and ISMA's 50% reservation for ESY 2026-27.** No reported government decision found. A higher ethanol quota for sugar mills was "discussed" (28 May 2026) and has not been notified (blog archive).
- **The 1,100 crore litre ESY 2026-27 tender itself.** No press coverage surfaced in search.
