# Update check, 12 October 2026: the five amino-acid, sweetener, bagasse and thermocol pieces

Method: each article was read for dated claims, then each topic was re-searched for newer information.
Sources are search summaries and trade press, not primary documents, unless stated. Inferences are marked.

## Thermocol / limonene (live) -- CORRECTION (major)
- **Wrong line:** "The European demonstration of CreaSolv did not reach its target of 3,000 tonnes a year." Not supported.
- **What the reports say:** Terneuzen plant (PolyStyreneLoop) first commissioned June 2021; operator insolvent March 2022; bought by
  German EPS Converters (Karl Bachl, Rygol, Brohlburg), restarted June 2022; regular operation reported June 2023, initial capacity
  3,000 t a year, 8,000 t a year the target; about EUR 17 million invested (Plasteurope, EUWID). Insolvency year inferred from the dates
  of those reports. Current throughput: not found.
- **No 2026 TEA, LCA or pilot study** of limonene or p-cymene foam recycling found. Main finding stands.

## Bagasse (live) -- update (minor)
- ISMA third advance estimate (Feb 2026): gross about 324 lakh t, about 31 lakh t to ethanol, net about 293 lakh t (+12% on last season's net).
  Mid-Feb 2026: 456 mills crushing vs 460 a year earlier.
- **Inference:** the 80 Mt wet-bagasse figure (WMW, older) is probably higher this season; not recomputed (no cane-crushed total found).
- New 2026 lab papers: bagasse polyol as geopolymer plasticiser (Composites Part B, Mar 2026, 10.4 MPa, >40% over baseline); nanobubble pretreatment
  (Physchem 2026); HTL bio-oil multi-step extraction (BMC Chemistry, Mar 2026, 280-340 C). Xie 2015 hydroxyl value 426-505 mg KOH/g.
- Still no TEA/LCA/pilot.

## Sucralose and sweeteners (live) -- update (minor)
- FSSAI single-phase front-of-pack proposal, 23 Sep 2026 (press): "contains non-caloric sweetener" to the front of pack; about 4 months to finalise draft,
  60 days comment, 365-day voluntary period; Supreme Court reserved verdict (about 28 Sep). A proposal, not a rule.
- Diabetes: IDF 2024 = 89.8 m adults 20-79 (10.5% age-standardised), 156.7 m by 2050, 43% undiagnosed; Lancet Global Health (Feb 2026, 45+, fieldwork 2017-19):
  about 1 in 5, 50.4 m. The article's 101 m (ICMR-INDIAB) is an extrapolation; the two differ in method and age range.
- ISMA first advance estimate 2025-26: domestic requirement 28.5 Mt.
- Not rechecked this round: Indian sucralose producers, customs duty rate.

## Lysine (live) -- update (minor)
- Price: aggregator quotes China FOB $870/t July 2026 (June $930), Germany $1,827/t. Indicative, unverified.
- EU: definitive duties from 12 Jul 2025 (47.7% Meihua, 58.2% Eppen, 53.1% others in one summary; other summaries say 58.3% -- rates differ);
  re-opened inquiry; Commission registering Chinese lysine imports from 29 Jul 2026 (MLex), after a March request from Eurolysine.
  Exporters reported shipping to non-EU markets ahead of duties; Fufeng Kazakhstan base described as a bypass.
- **Inferences (flagged in the article):** at $870-930/t, India's $106.1 m of FY26 imports is roughly 115,000-120,000 t if the FY26 price was similar (unknown);
  about 9% of Meihua's 1.3 Mt. A 25,000 t plant sells about $22 m a year at $870/t (vs INR 50-1,500+ crore build cost).
