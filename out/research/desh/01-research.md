# DESH Bill — research notes (27 Oct 2026)

Primary pages (drishtiias.com, *.gov.in, sezindia.gov.in, pib.gov.in) are blocked from the research
machine; facts below come from search-index text of those pages and from news reports, as marked.

## The Bill
- Announced in the Union Budget, 1 Feb 2022, to replace the SEZ Act, 2005. Draft "Development of
  Enterprise and Service Hubs (DESH) Bill, 2022" circulated mid-2022 (explainers: Drishti IAS, Business
  Standard podcast 27 Jul 2022, The Federal, IASGyan, Civilsdaily).
- Draft contents (as reported): SEZs renamed development hubs (enterprise hubs, service hubs); no
  net-foreign-exchange (NFE) condition; domestic-market sales with duty on imported inputs, not the final
  product; possible equalisation levy on domestic supplies; online single window with time-bound
  approvals; larger role for states, which could send proposals directly to a central board; direct-tax
  incentives dropped to be WTO-compatible; a concessional 15% corporate tax for hub units to 2032
  (Business Standard, Oct 2022).
- Finance Ministry rejected the 15% rate and the tax incentives (Business Standard 5 Oct 2022;
  BW Businessworld 5 Dec 2022); also objected to duty-forgone domestic sales (reports). NITI Aayog raised
  concerns in Dec 2022 about partial de-notification and removing NFE (reports).
- Business Standard, 6 Nov 2023: government could junk DESH and amend the SEZ Act instead.
  Policy Circle: an "SEZ Amendment Bill 2023" planned in its place.
- Business Standard, 12 Apr 2024: SEZ amendment bill may figure in the next government's 100-day agenda.
- Business Standard, 13 Jul 2025: commerce department set to seek Cabinet approval for a bill to replace
  the SEZ Act in the monsoon session; proposals: domestic sales on duty forgone on raw materials, reverse
  job work, rupee payment for services to DTA units.
- PRS bill tracker search: no SEZ bill after the SEZ (Amendment) Bill, 2019 (passed June 2019). No report
  found of a DESH or SEZ bill introduced in 2024–2026.

## Reform by rule and notification
- SEZ (Amendment) Rules, 2025, G.S.R. 364(E), 3 Jun 2025: minimum area for semiconductor / electronic
  component SEZs cut to 10 ha (most sources: from 50 ha); free-of-cost goods count toward NFE for
  semiconductor units; Board of Approval may relax the encumbrance-free land condition; domestic sales
  on payment of duty for these units (Business Standard; PIB via seepz.gov.in; Taxguru).
- Budget 2026-27: one-time relief for SEZ manufacturing units to sell in the DTA at concessional duty
  (PIB explainer). Customs Notification 11/2026-Customs, 31 Mar 2026 (PIB PRID 2247628 and 2247993):
  1 Apr 2026 – 31 Mar 2027; cap 30% of the highest annual FOB export value of the three preceding years;
  units in production by 31 Mar 2025; FTWZ units excluded; minimum 20% value addition; no duty drawback;
  Development Commissioner certificate; faceless assessment. Rates: 7.5→6.5; 10→9; 12.5/15→10; 20→12.5;
  20–30→15; 30–40→20 (CBIC table as reported).
- SEZ (Third Amendment) Rules, 2026, G.S.R. 893(E), 15 Oct 2026: goods rejected by DTA buyers may return
  within 30 days; separate inventory; resale is a fresh DTA sale; refund of duty under Rule 47(5)
  (Business Standard 22 Oct 2026; A2Z Taxcorp). BS opinion 25 Oct 2026: 30 days is short.
- SEZ (Fourth Amendment) Rules, 2026, around 21 Oct 2026: eases requirements for fund and aircraft/ship
  leasing units in IFSCs (report).

## SEZ 2.0 committee
- March 2026: 17-member inter-ministerial committee (Commerce, Customs/CBIC, NITI Aayog, DPIIT), chaired by
  the Additional Secretary (SEZ) per the order (one report says Commerce Secretary); six months to prepare
  a concept paper and roadmap for SEZ 2.0; mandate includes reviewing the SEZ Act and harmonising SEZs
  with EOUs, MOOWR, Advance Authorisation, EPCG and DFIA (A2Z Taxcorp, KNN, Angel One).
- Heard 100+ developers, units and advisers, 30 Jun 2026 (report). Business Standard 19 Jul 2026: report
  "soon"; topics include rupee payment for services to DTA and job work without export linkage.
- No report public as of 27 Oct 2026 (no news of submission found).

## Numbers
- Lok Sabha written reply, as of 31 Jul 2026 (PSUWatch; Torbit; Kashmir Life): 436 approved, 372
  notified, 277 operational, 95 non-operational (Haryana 14, Telangana 12, Karnataka 11, Tamil Nadu 9,
  Uttar Pradesh 9), 110 de-notified; reasons: slow statutory/state approvals, adverse global climate,
  changes in fiscal incentives. Employment 26.96 lakh (2021-22) to 32.61 lakh (2025-26). Exports
  Rs 14.63 lakh crore (2024-25) to Rs 16.4 lakh crore (2025-26).
- SEZ fact sheet as on 31.12.2025 (search text): 417 formal approvals, 370 notified, 276 operational;
  exports Apr–Dec 2025 Rs 11,65,711 crore (US$133.45 bn), +31.48% on the same period of 2024-25;
  FY2024-25 US$173.07 bn; investment Rs 7,82,192 crore and employment 31.78 lakh (both 31 Mar 2025).
- SEZ India export-performance page: 2024-25 Rs 14,56,871 crore, US$172.27 bn, +7.37%.
- India total exports 2024-25: US$824.9 bn (RBI revision). SEZ share about 21%.
- Reports that "SEZ exports fell to $133.45 bn in FY26 from $172.07 bn" (Whalesbook, also in some
  SEZ 2.0 coverage) compare the Apr–Dec figure with a full year.
- EPCES Jan–Mar 2026 newsletter tables (labels unclear): 69,004 → 70,762 and 107,623 → 112,286

## WTO
- DS541 (India — Export Related Measures), US complaint. Panel report circulated 31 Oct 2019: MEIS, EOU,
  SEZ, EPCG and DFIA measures are prohibited export subsidies; withdrawal within 90–180 days. India appealed
  19 Nov 2019; the Appellate Body has had no quorum since Dec 2019, so the appeal is unresolved
  ("appeal into the void"). MEIS replaced by RoDTEP in 2021.
- SEZ income-tax holidays: developer benefit for SEZs notified by 31 Mar 2017; unit benefit for units
  starting by 31 Mar 2020 (extended to 30 Sep 2020) — from this blog's textile-SEZ piece.
