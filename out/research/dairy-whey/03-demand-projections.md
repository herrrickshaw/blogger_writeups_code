## 2. Why WPC spiked in 2025–26, and whether it lasts

- **Drivers reported:** high-protein product launches, and GLP-1 users told to eat more protein. Vesper cites a 2025 RAND survey: 11.8% of US adults have used a GLP-1, and Circana says about a quarter of US households have a user. Whey processing capacity has not kept up. A Rabobank figure, reported secondhand, has WPC80 up 120% between Oct 2025 and Jul 2026. AP/Ever.Ag (Jun 2026) report US WPC80 above USD 13/lb, up 250% y/y, and an EU record average of EUR 26,450/t in late May 2026. Sources: https://www.business-standard.com/health/there-s-not-enough-whey-for-the-world-s-high-protein-product-demand-126061400124_1.html ; https://vespertool.com/blog/how-the-rise-of-glp-1-is-reshaping-the-dairy-protein-industry/ ; https://dairynews.today/global/news/wpc80_prices_have_nearly_sextupled_as_supply_lags_demand_9286450.html
- **Cheese output does not ease it:** Rabobank's Q1 2026 summary reports strong cheese output (December production +6.7% y/y) and falling cheese prices, while whey kept strengthening (https://ahdb.org.uk/news/tighter-margins-slower-production-growth-and-a-cautious-price-recovery-rabobank-q1-summary). In other words, the constraint is fractionation capacity and protein demand, not liquid whey volume.
- **China:** No credible figure found for China's whey demand in 2025–26.
- **Persistence:** No analyst gives a dated price path. Signs of easing:
  - In mid-July 2026, USDA Dairy Market News said US interest had "softened" and loads were easier to find (https://www.clal.it/upload/2026-07-17-ams_1053.pdf).
  - Ever.Ag (Wolfley) is unsure whether relief comes within a year, and notes that demand destruction is possible.
  - New capacity arrives late: Glanbia's New Mexico isolate expansion in 2027, Agropur's US and Canadian expansions by 2029, and FrieslandCampina's >EUR 90 m protein investment (May 2026, https://www.foodmanufacture.co.uk/Article/2026/05/05/frieslandcampina-to-invest-over-90-million-into-protein-ingredients/).

  Rabobank's Q3 2026 view is that slowing milk supply and growing protein demand are tightening the market. So tightness through 2026 and into 2027 is well supported. Any statement about 2028–30 prices is speculation.

## 5. Reliability notes

- **Paid reports disagree by roughly 2.6x on the base and by 3.3x on growth.** For India whey protein in 2024–25, bases range from USD 102 m (TechSci, MarkNtel) to USD 178–186 m (Mordor, IMARC) to USD 267 m (unattributed). CAGRs range from 3.3% to 9.9%. Most were written before the 2026 price spike, so their value figures mix volume with price, and none reflect the threefold rise in landed cost.
- **The low CAGRs (3–5%) for whey protein sit badly against 13% for sports nutrition (Ken).** The two cannot both describe volume. Ken's two sports-nutrition reports also disagree with each other (USD 679 m vs USD 1,159 m for 2025).
- **India cheese:** CAGRs fall into two groups, about 7–10% in USD (Ken, Credence) and about 21% in INR (R&M, TechSci). The INR group probably includes price inflation and has a smaller base. The TechSci "USD bn" label is plainly a unit error.
- **Institutional coverage of whey is thin.** OECD-FAO does not model whey. No USDEC or IFCN whey forecast was found. Rabobank's whey comments reach us through trade-press summaries.
- **Commercial stake:** Vesper and supplement-brand blogs have one, so they are treated as context only.

## 6. Best defensible range

What can be said:
- **India whey protein market:** Paid estimates put it at roughly USD 100–190 m in 2024–25, with forecasts of 3–6% a year to about USD 125–220 m by 2030–31. The 9.9% outlier is unattributed.
- **Wider protein-supplement and sports-nutrition category:** Forecasts are 6–7% CAGR (IMARC, Ken protein, blogs), with one Ken series at about 13%.
- **Infant formula and clinical nutrition:** These add demand growing at roughly 5–9% a year.
- **Cheese:** Paid forecasts put India's cheese market growth at roughly 7–10% a year in USD terms, or about 20% in INR terms. That growth sets the ceiling on how fast domestic sweet whey supply can grow.
- **Global whey ingredients:** Paid forecasts cluster at 6–7% CAGR to 2030 (GIA, BCC).
- **Prices:** The 2026 tightness is real and well documented. New Western capacity mostly arrives in 2027–29, so tightness through 2026 and into 2027 is well supported.

What cannot be said:
- India's whey protein demand in tonnes for any year to 2030.
- India's total domestic WPC/WPI capacity, or the size of the demand–supply gap.
- An official multi-year import series for HS 3502.20 or 1702.11. The gov TradeStat portal was not reached, and no workaround was attempted.
- India's pharma lactose demand.
- Any analyst forecast that WPC prices will return to 2024 levels, or a date for it.

For the blog, frame the whey-protein value forecasts as "single-digit growth per paid estimates, pre-dating the 2026 price shock". Present the import share and price data (already known) as the stronger evidence.
