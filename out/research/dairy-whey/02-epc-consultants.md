### Small-scale and effluent

- **Small whey beverage lines.** NDDB says a whey drink needs only pasteurisation and packaging, so ordinary small-dairy fabricators can build these lines. IndiaMART shows mini-dairy and paneer-equipment makers, but none of them advertises a whey RO/UF skid. https://m.indiamart.com/impcat/mini-dairy-plant.html
- **Small RO/UF skids.** These are best sourced through membrane integrators such as Ion Exchange or Hydranautics channel partners. No dedicated small whey-skid vendor was found.
- **Biogas.** No Thermax, Praj or Ion Exchange whey-to-biogas project was found. Related Indian examples:

## 2. Indicative costs and lead times (sparse)

- **WPC80 benchmark (not India).** A line of about 1,000 kg/h needs total capital investment of about USD 21.0m (Romanian academic study, vol. 21, about 2021). This is an order-of-magnitude figure only. https://managementjournal.usamv.ro/pdf/vol.21_1/Art7.pdf
- **UF plant in India (proxy).** Milky Mist's UF yoghurt plant cost about Rs 40 crore for 150 TPD (Sep 2026). It is a UF dairy plant, not whey.
- **Lead time.** Milky Mist's WPC plant was expected to take 15–18 months to build.
- **Tetra Pak.** It says its pre-engineered UF System P is cheaper than custom systems and delivers faster. No price was published.
- **Not found:** Indian rupee capex for small whey RO/UF skids, spray dryers or a hub-scale WPC34–80 plant. Getting these needs vendor quotes or a paid DPR from IMARC or NIIR, whose prices were not shown.

## 3. Takeaways for the blog

- **Large dairies integrating WPC on site.** The proven route is a global line supplier for UF/DF, evaporation and drying (GEA, Tetra Pak, SPX Flow), with membranes from Hydranautics, Ion Exchange or the line supplier, and NDDB or a private consultant for the DPR and PMC.
- **Cluster "whey hubs".** No Indian shared-hub precedent was found. The likely team is NDDB Engineering or IDMC as PMC/EPC, an Indian evaporation and drying vendor such as SSP, and a membrane integrator such as Ion Exchange. Whey-specific references still need confirming.
- **Small paneer and cheese units.** Use NDRI or NDDB whey-drink know-how with standard pasteurisation and filling equipment. Membrane skids are optional.
