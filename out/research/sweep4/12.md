### 10. indias-subsidised-imports-fertilisers (LOW)
- Claim in post: "12% safeguard duty (stepping to 11%)" (iron and steel, HS 72)
- Current (as of 18 Oct 2026):
  - **The rate had already stepped down before this post (31 Jul 2026).** The Finance Ministry's safeguard notification of 30 December 2025 sets the duty on specified flat steel at:
    - 12% in year 1, from 21 April 2025
    - 11.5% from 21 April 2026 to 20 April 2027
    - 11% in year 3
  - **So the rate in force is 11.5%, not 12%.** Imports priced above CIF floors (for example $675/t for HR coil) are exempt.
  - **Known since-events checked.** This post's FY2025-26 fuel-subsidy estimate is retrospective and does not contradict the known since-events: the 26 March 2026 special additional excise duty cut and the 15 May 2026 pump-price rise.
  - Seen: summaries only.
