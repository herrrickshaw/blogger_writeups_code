### 1. refiners-won-exemption-fight-biofuels (MEDIUM)
- Claim in post: "EPA's remand decisions on the 2016–2024 exemption petitions after Sinclair and Alon, and how many RINs come back to the market." This is listed under "What to watch". Elsewhere the post says "It also reallocates 70% of the gallons exempted for 2023–25: roughly 0.99 billion RINs in 2026 and 1.04 billion in 2027." The headline card reads "70%Share of 2023–25 exempted gallons EPA reallocated to other refiners".
- Current (as of 18 Oct 2026):
  - **EPA has acted on exemptions several times since the cases the post describes. The post (3 Oct 2026) mentions none of these actions.**
    - **22 Aug 2025:** EPA decided 175 petitions from 38 refineries for compliance years 2016–2024. It granted 63 in full and 77 in part (50%), denied 28 and found 7 ineligible. EPA's technical report puts the total exempted at 5,340 million RINs. One summary (ALL4) says EPA ruled on 16 more petitions by 7 Nov 2025.
    - **3 Aug 2026:** EPA decided at least one 2024 petition, which it reissued on 31 Aug.
    - **31 Aug 2026:** EPA decided 34 petitions for the 2025 compliance year: 18 full exemptions, 11 partial, 3 denied, 2 ineligible. That is about 1.76 billion RINs across 29 refineries. EPA also moved the 2025 RVO compliance date by 30 days to 1 Oct 2026.
  - **Reallocation is moving beyond 70%.** EPA said it would propose, before the end of October 2026, reallocating 100% of the difference between projected and actual 2025 exempted volumes into the 2026 and 2027 RVOs. OPIS describes this as a "proposed 100% reallocation into 2026, 2027 RVOs".
  - No result reported a D.C. Circuit ruling on the Set 2 petitions.
  - Seen: summaries only.

  - https://www.all4inc.com/4-the-record-articles/u-s-epa-processes-renewable-fuel-standard-small-refinery-exemption-backlog/
  - https://www.epa.gov/renewable-fuel-standard/august-31-2026-decisions-petitions-rfs-small-refinery-exemptions

### 3. sugar-at-5570-kilo-why-cane-acreage (MEDIUM)
- Claim in post: "Separately, reports suggest India may need to import sugar for the first time in roughly a decade if the current stock trajectory holds." The post also says: "industry estimates put closing stock as of 30 September 2026 at just 3–3.5 million tonnes".
- Current (as of 18 Oct 2026):
  - **The import decision had already been taken when the post went up (21 Aug 2026).** By a DGFT notification of 20 August 2026, the government allowed duty-free imports of 10 lakh tonnes of raw sugar under a tariff-rate quota, valid until 31 October 2026. The conditions reported:
    - only companies with in-house refining capacity may import;
    - the refined sugar must be sold in India by 31 October.
  - Coverage (Crisil, Business Today, News on AIR, Rural Voice) describes this as the first such imports for domestic use in about a decade.
  - In late August the industry said around 8 lakh tonnes of the quota had been allocated.
  - **The stock estimate is unchanged in substance.**
    - In late August ISMA projected closing stock at about 35 lakh tonnes at end-September. That is in line with the post's 3–3.5 million tonnes; one Crisil summary gives 3.9 MMT.
    - ISMA and NFCSF put 2025-26 net production at about 279 lakh tonnes after about 30 lakh tonnes was diverted to ethanol. Consumption is estimated at 280–285 lakh tonnes.
  - The existing 12 Oct note covers the price reversal and refers to "stock limits and other supply measures". It does not mention the import decision.
