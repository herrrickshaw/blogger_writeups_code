### 1. where-worlds-oil-sits-when-it-isnt (HIGH)
- Claim in post: in the pipeline table, under the column "Status, mid-Aug 2026", the Kirkuk–Ceyhan row reads: "Reopened 18 Mar 2026 after a 12-year shutdown (halted 2014); bilateral Iraq-Turkey agreement expired and is under a 12-month extension negotiation as of Aug 2026". The Scale column reads "Restarted ~250,000 bpd, targeting up to 770,000 bpd".
- Current (as of 18 Oct 2026):
  - **The deal was signed before the post was published (14 Aug 2026).** Turkey's BOTAS and Iraq's SOMO and North Oil Company signed a one-year interim transit agreement in Ankara on Saturday 1 August 2026. It sets transit capacity at 750,000 bpd. The old bilateral agreement had lapsed the Monday before (due 27 July).
  - **Both sides call it a bridge** while talks continue on a long-term or broader framework agreement.
  - **Flows:** Turkish data quoted in the same coverage put current flows at about 170,000 bpd, below the post's "~250,000 bpd".
  - **Capacity discrepancy:** Peregraf notes that 750,000 bpd is below the up to 1 million bpd Iraq's prime minister had offered at a 28 July summit.
  - **Not an existing note:** the post's 26 Sep 2026 update note says Section 7's pipeline table was not re-checked, so this is not already corrected.
  - Seen: summaries only.

  - https://english.aawsat.com/node/5302371
  - https://www.enerdata.net/publications/daily-energy-news/turkiye-and-iraq-extend-kirkuk-ceyhan-oil-pipeline-agreement-one-year.html
  - https://pipeline-journal.net/news/iraq-turkiye-sign-one-year-interim-deal-maintain-northern-crude-pipeline-operations
  - https://peregraf.com/en/news/12157
- Suggested wording: "Updated [date]. The Kirkuk–Ceyhan row described the Iraq–Turkey pipeline agreement as still under negotiation. Press reports say Turkey's BOTAS and Iraq's SOMO and North Oil Company had already signed a one-year interim transit agreement on 1 August 2026, with 750,000 barrels a day of transit capacity, while talks on a longer-term agreement continue. Flows were reported at about 170,000 barrels a day at the time. The table's point, that the route gives northern Iraqi crude an outlet that avoids Hormuz, is unchanged."
- Confidence:
  - **The 1 Aug 2026 signing and the 750,000 bpd capacity:** confirmed by several independent outlets (Al Jazeera, Asharq Al-Awsat, Enerdata, Pipeline Journal, Peregraf), all seen as summaries.
  - **The ~170,000 bpd flow:** a single figure attributed to Turkish data.
  - **Severity:** HIGH because the post presents as pending an agreement that had been signed two weeks before publication. The post's wider argument is not affected.
  - **Possible separate error:** one summary describes the pipeline as "largely idle since 2023" after an arbitration ruling, which may conflict with the post's "halted 2014". This is unverified, and it is history rather than staleness.

  - **Nothing enacted yet.** No enacted provision or official SEBI or MCA document was found, so the post's "not a shipped feature" still holds.
  - Seen: summaries only.

  - https://www.sansalegal.com/post/sebi-seeks-inclusion-of-fractional-shares-in-companies-amendment-bill-2026-what-it-means-for-invest
