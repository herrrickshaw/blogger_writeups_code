### 1. india-mineral-oil-hsn-ch27-trade (HIGH)
- Claim in post: "On the export leg: RoDTEP/drawback, and watch export-duty/SAED windfall levies on MS/HSD/ATF (imposed 2022, rescinded Dec-2024 — can return)." This is in section 6, "Commodity-import pricing checklist (India, current rules)", rendered from the page's data script. The segment table also lists "Export duty/SAED can be re-imposed on windfall margins" as a future risk for diesel.
- Current (as of 18 Oct 2026): The levy has already returned. Search summaries say the government imposed an export duty (SAED) on diesel and ATF on 26 March 2026 (some reports say 27 March), during the West Asia crisis, so that fuel stayed available at home. It added petrol exports on 16 May 2026. The Finance Ministry has changed the rates every fortnight since then. Reported diesel rates: Rs 14/L from 16 June, Rs 8.5/L from 1 July, Rs 15.5/L from 16 July, Rs 24/L from 15 August and Rs 25/L (SAED plus cess) from 1 September. Reported ATF rate: Rs 19/L from 1 September. Exports by state-run companies to Nepal, Bhutan, Bangladesh and Sri Lanka were reported exempt, later also Mauritius and the Maldives. Reliance's SEZ refinery was reported exempt. I found no revision after mid-September, and the sources conflict on some figures, including one outlier of Rs 55.5/L in April. The levy was already in force when the dashboard was published on 30 July 2026, so the "current rules" checklist was out of date at publication. Seen: summaries only.
