### 1. ethanol-blending-omc-books-e20-today-vs (HIGH)
- Claim in post: "Excise / VAT. Central excise ₹19.9/L; state VAT ~₹18/L national average (RR Table 8.17)." The exchequer table puts "Centre petrol excise foregone" at "−21,500" (E20) and "−32,250" (E30). "What would fix this" repeats "a cost this piece models at about ₹21,500 crore at E20". The SGST note says it is "funded from the ~₹15/L ethanol-vs-petrol fiscal space".
- Current (as of 18 Oct 2026):
  - **₹19.9/L was already out of date twice when the post went up (24 Jul 2026).**
    - It is the pre-April-2025 petrol excise. Tribune/ANI and Business Today report that excise was raised by ₹2/L from 8 April 2025, taking total petrol excise from ₹19.90 to ₹21.90/L. The rise was in the special additional excise duty (SAED), which went to ₹13/L.
    - On 26 March 2026 petrol SAED was cut from ₹13 to ₹3 (this blog's established list of since-events). That leaves total petrol excise at about ₹11.90/L, by this reviewer's arithmetic (21.90 − 10).
  - **Sources disagree on the March 2026 rate.** One Outlook India summary describes the 26 March 2026 notification as setting petrol SAED to "Nil", which would give about ₹8.90/L. Later 2026 SAED notifications summarised by Taxguru and World Trade Scanner relate mainly to petrol exports. Either way, the domestic rate is far below ₹19.9/L.
  - **Effect on the model (reviewer's arithmetic, using the post's own volumes):** at ₹11.90/L, central excise foregone is about ₹12,850 crore at E20 (10.8 bn L) and about ₹19,300 crore at E30 (16.2 bn L). The post has ₹21,500 crore and ₹32,250 crore. Total exchequer foregone at E20 falls from about ₹39,400 crore to about ₹30,750 crore.
  - **The "~₹15/L fiscal space"** for the proposed SGST also shrinks. The post does not say how it was derived, so the revised figure is unverified.
  - The OMC-margin and export lines do not depend on excise and are unaffected.
  - Seen: summaries only.
  - https://www.tribuneindia.com/news/business/government-increases-excise-duty-on-petrol-diesel-by-rs-2-per-litre
  - https://www.businesstoday.in/india/story/government-hikes-excise-duty-on-petrol-and-diesel-by-rs-2-per-litre-470997-2025-04-07
  - https://www.outlookindia.com/amp/story/national/government-slashes-additional-excise-duty-on-petrol-and-diesel-to-provide-relief
- Suggested wording: "Updated [date]. This model assumes central excise of ₹19.9 a litre on petrol. That rate was raised to about ₹21.90 in April 2025, and the special additional excise duty was then cut by ₹10 on 26 March 2026, leaving roughly ₹11.90. At that rate the Centre's excise forgone on ethanol-displaced petrol is about ₹12,900 crore at E20 and ₹19,300 crore at E30, not ₹21,500 and ₹32,250 crore. The OMC and export figures are unchanged, and the net national picture becomes more favourable to blending."
- Confidence: the April 2025 totals (₹19.90 to ₹21.90) and the SAED of ₹13 agree across Tribune/ANI and Business Today summaries. The March 2026 cut comes from the blog's established list. One summary (Outlook) says petrol SAED went to nil rather than ₹3, so confirm the exact post-cut total against the notification before posting. The recomputed crore figures are this reviewer's arithmetic, not a published number. Severity is HIGH because the post presents an outdated tax rate as today's ("Current · E20") and its headline exchequer cost changes by about ₹8,600 crore a year.
