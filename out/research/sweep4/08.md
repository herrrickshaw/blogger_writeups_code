### 1. fuel-pricing-to-crude-cost (HIGH)
- Claim in post: "Petrol's stack (₹21.90/l: 1.40+13.00+5.00+2.50) is heavier than diesel's (₹17.80/l: 1.80+10.00+2.00+4.00)". The method note says: "Excise duty, dealer commission and the effective VAT rate are held at their 01.10.2025 PPAC levels — i.e. no policy offset." The Section 3 table row "25 Sep 2026 (current)" puts Delhi petrol at ₹115.42/l and diesel at ₹107.96/l. Section 4 shows "PPAC published: ₹94.77/l" for petrol and "PPAC published: ₹87.67/l" for diesel. The text also says government "has repeatedly cut excise duty when crude spiked", but only as history.
- Current (as of 18 Oct 2026):
  - **The excise stack changed before this post was published.** A notification dated 26 March 2026, under Section 5A of the Central Excise Act, cut Special Additional Excise Duty (SAED) as follows:
    - petrol: from ₹13 to ₹3 per litre
    - diesel: from ₹10 to nil
    - Source: Moneylife, with Outlook India and askfuzz describing the same notification.
  - **Pump prices did not move at the time of the cut.** The cut went to the oil marketing companies' under-recoveries. Business Today and Outlook Business reported losses still at about ₹18/l on petrol and ₹35/l on diesel in mid-April 2026. Export duties were also imposed on diesel and ATF.
  - **The total stack is now lower.** By this reviewer's arithmetic, with the other three components unchanged, total central excise is about ₹11.90/l on petrol and ₹7.80/l on diesel. The post uses ₹21.90 and ₹17.80.
  - **Delhi pump prices have since risen.** Hans India's daily listings for 1–15 October 2026 give Delhi petrol at ₹102.12/l and diesel at ₹95.20/l. That is up from the ₹94.77 and ₹87.67 shown in the post. The date of the increase was not found.
  - **Status unverified:** whether the March SAED rates are still in force in October. No October 2026 Indian-basket figure was found either.
  - Seen: summaries only.
  - https://moneylife.in/article/petrol-excise-duty-down-to-3-diesel-nil-no-change-in-retail-prices-as-govt-aims-to-cut-omc-losses/80059.html
  - https://www.outlookindia.com/amp/story/national/government-slashes-additional-excise-duty-on-petrol-and-diesel-to-provide-relief
  - https://www.businesstoday.in/amp/india/story/fuel-price-freeze-spells-trouble-for-oil-companies-loss-on-petrol-rises-to-rs-18-per-litre-diesel-to-rs-35-per-litre-525578-2026-04-14
  - https://www.thehansindia.com/business/market-compass/petrol-and-diesel-prices-today-in-hyderabad-delhi-chennai-and-mumbai-on-15-october-2026-1132618
- Suggested wording: "Updated [date]. The excise figures below are the 1 October 2025 levels and are no longer current. Per press reports of a 26 March 2026 notification, Special Additional Excise Duty was cut from ₹13 to ₹3 a litre on petrol and from ₹10 to nil on diesel. That cut was absorbed by the oil companies rather than passed to the pump. Separately, daily price listings put Delhi petrol at about ₹102.12 and diesel at about ₹95.20 a litre in mid-October 2026. The '25 Sep 2026 (current)' row therefore shows what the 2025 tax structure would imply, not the actual pump price. It also illustrates the excise-cushion effect this piece describes."
- Confidence:
  - SAED cut: several summaries agree on the date and direction, and one gives the ₹13→₹3 and ₹10→nil figures. Sunday Guardian described it as a "₹10 per litre" cut, which is consistent.
  - October pump prices: a single source (Hans India listings).
  - Whether the cut is still in force: unverified. Check the CBIC notification and PPAC's current price build-up before posting.

### 2. india-let-oil-companies-put-solar-on (HIGH)
- Claim in post: "The Green Energy Corridor programme's next phase, GEC Phase 3, was proposed in the FY26 Budget at an estimated cost of roughly ₹56,000 crore (the Centre covering about 40%, the rest from state governments and other stakeholders)". It goes on: "It isn't due to commission until around 2029-30." The fixes section says "The Ministry of Power should prioritise GEC Phase 3's Gujarat and Rajasthan legs specifically" against "a 2029-30 commissioning date".
- Current (as of 18 Oct 2026):
  - **The scheme was approved two days after this post (28 September 2026), in a very different form.** On 30 September 2026 the Union Cabinet approved GEC Phase III with:
    - an outlay of ₹1,86,405 crore: ₹1,36,378 crore for intra-state transmission and ₹50,000 crore for 50 GWh of battery storage
    - central support of ₹54,082 crore, about 29%, not 40%
    - evacuation of up to 135 GW
    - a target of FY2032-33, not 2029-30
  - **It is intra-state, not an HVDC backbone.** State transmission utilities implement it, with TBCB for greenfield lines. The post describes an HVDC backbone instead.
  - This blog's own 9 October 2026 post (green-energy-corridor-iii-is-15-times) reports the same figures.
  - Seen: summaries only.
  - https://renewablewatch.in/2026/10/01/cabinet-approves-green-energy-corridor-phase-iii-scheme/
  - https://indianinfrastructure.com/2026/10/01/cabinet-approves-rs-1-86-trillion-green-energy-corridor-phase-iii-scheme/
  - https://www.uniindia.com/national/cabinet-green-energy-corridor/198041
  - https://www.tndindia.com/cabinet-approves-green-energy-corridor-phase-iii-insts-scheme-tbcb-route-mooted-for-greenfield-projects/amp/
- Suggested wording: "Updated [date]. Two days after this piece was published, on 30 September 2026, the Union Cabinet approved Green Energy Corridor Phase III. As reported, it is ₹1,86,405 crore for intra-state lines plus 50 GWh of battery storage, with ₹54,082 crore (about 29%) of central support, to evacuate up to 135 GW by 2032-33. That supersedes the ~₹56,000 crore, ~40% and 2029-30 figures below. The approved phase covers lines inside states, not an inter-state HVDC backbone. See this blog's GEC-III piece."
- Confidence: four trade-press summaries agree with each other and with this blog's later post. One summary noted that the components sum to ₹1,86,378 crore; the blog's GEC-III post attributes the remaining ₹27 crore to programme management. Whether the FY26 Budget figure of ₹56,000 crore referred to the same scheme is unverified.

  - **The 7% real figure may not match.** One summary gives the earlier advance estimate as 7.4% real and 8.0% nominal, not the post's "7% real".
