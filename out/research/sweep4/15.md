### 1. a-slice-of-apple-costs-about-1700-slice (MEDIUM)
- Claim in post: "The Company Law Committee recommended in 2022 that the Companies Act be amended to permit issuance and transfer of fractional shares in dematerialised form — that amendment has not been made." Related lines: "needs a Companies Act amendment that hasn't happened yet". The fix list says: "The Ministry of Corporate Affairs should publish a draft amendment, with a consultation deadline".
- Current (as of 18 Oct 2026):
  - **The bill exists, without fractional shares.** Per search summaries, a Companies Amendment Bill was introduced in the Lok Sabha on 23 March 2026, before this post (11 Aug 2026), and leaves out any provision for fractional shares. A government source quoted in the coverage says the omission was deliberate: fractional shares without voting rights could weaken protections for minority shareholders.
  - **SEBI's request.** Reports from August 2026 say SEBI has asked the Ministry of Corporate Affairs to add fractional-share issuance and holding to the bill.
  - **No change found.** No report says the bill has passed, that fractional shares were added, or that the Xaults sandbox has graduated. So "has not been made" is still true.
  - **What the post misses.** The live legislative vehicle and the open SEBI–MCA disagreement. The post's ask for a "draft amendment" now points at a bill that is already in Parliament.
  - Seen: summaries only.
  - https://www.5paisa.com/news/sebi-seeks-legal-recognition-for-fractional-shares-under-companies-amendment-bill
  - https://caalley.com/news-updates/indian-news/sebi-seeks-inclusion-of-fractional-shares-in-companies-amendment-bill
  - https://www.sansalegal.com/post/sebi-seeks-inclusion-of-fractional-shares-in-companies-amendment-bill-2026-what-it-means-for-invest
  - https://ksandk.com/news/surbhi-kapoor-fractional-shares-companies-bill-india/
- Suggested wording: "Updated [date]. The Companies Act change is still not made, but there is now a bill to watch. Press reports say a Companies Amendment Bill introduced in the Lok Sabha on 23 March 2026 left out fractional shares, reportedly over minority-shareholder concerns. In August 2026 SEBI asked the Ministry of Corporate Affairs to add them. We found no report that this has happened."
- Confidence: Several outlets (5paisa, CA Alley, two law-firm notes) agree on SEBI's request and the omission. The 23 March 2026 introduction date comes from one summary and is otherwise unverified. The exact date of the SEBI request within August 2026 is unverified, so it may be after this post.
