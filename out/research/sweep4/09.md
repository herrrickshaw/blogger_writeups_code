### 1. upi-fees-are-now-legal-petrol-pumps (HIGH)
- Claim in post: "The subsidy that currently keeps UPI free for merchants is ₹437 crore a year." The post also says "the budget line that compensated banks for carrying free UPI was cut from ₹2,000 crore to ₹437 crore this fiscal year". The headline chart compares the fee pool with "India's ₹437 cr RuPay incentive (FY26)" and gives multiples of "205×", "246×" and "820×".
- Current (as of 18 Oct 2026):
  - **₹437 crore was the FY2025-26 budget estimate, not the current figure.** Inc42 read the Union Budget 2026-27 documents (Feb 2026) as allocating ₹2,000 crore for FY2026-27 to the incentive scheme for RuPay debit cards and low-value BHIM-UPI transactions. That allocation came before this post (8 Aug 2026).
  - **FY2025-26 itself ended higher.** The same report gives a revised estimate of ₹2,196 crore for FY2025-26.
  - **The multiples shrink.** By this reviewer's arithmetic, the post's own fee pools against ₹2,000 crore give about 45× at 0.25%, 54× at 0.30% and 179× at 1.00%. The fee pool still dwarfs the subsidy.
  - Seen: summaries only.
  - https://inc42.com/?p=547197
- Suggested wording: "Updated [date]. The ₹437 crore figure below was the budget estimate for FY2025-26. Press reading of the Union Budget 2026-27 puts the FY2026-27 allocation for the RuPay/BHIM-UPI incentive at ₹2,000 crore, with FY2025-26 revised to about ₹2,196 crore. Against ₹2,000 crore, the fee pools in Exhibit 1 are roughly 45 to 180 times the subsidy rather than 205 to 820 times. The comparison's point stands."
- Confidence: single source, a trade-press reading of the budget documents. Check the Demand for Grants (Department of Financial Services) before posting.

  - **A lower bid came in before this post was published.** The Numaligarh Refinery (NRL) green hydrogen tender closed in February 2026 at a record low of ₹279/kg, about $3.08/kg, excluding taxes.     - So the post compares a tax-inclusive price with a plant-gate target.

  - https://bioenergytimes.com/ocior-energy-wins-bid-to-supply-green-hydrogen-to-hpcls-visakhapatnam-refinery/
