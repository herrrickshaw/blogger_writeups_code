### 1. indias-textile-import-export-strategy (HIGH)
- Claim in post: "BIS Mandatory Standards: All imported chemicals/petrochemicals now require BIS compliance (effective FY24-25)". The slide goes on: "Impact on textiles: Dyes, pigments, polymers must meet quality standards → reduces dumping of low-grade imports (3-5% effective tariff)" and "Policy Opportunity: Quality standards create competitive space for domestic high-quality feedstock production. Domestic units meeting BIS gain 3-5% price premium vs low-quality imports."
- Current (as of 18 Oct 2026):
  - **The quality control orders the slide relies on were withdrawn for the main textile feedstocks before the post went up (27 Jul 2026).** The Ministry of Chemicals and Fertilizers rescinded several BIS quality control orders (QCOs) by a Gazette notification dated 12 November 2025, effective immediately.
  - The withdrawn orders cover terephthalic acid (PTA) and ethylene glycol (MEG), whose QCOs dated from December 2021. Reports also list PSF, FDY, POY, PP, PVC, EVA and ABS. Reports describe 14 items in all.
  - The withdrawal followed a review by a NITI Aayog committee chaired by Rajiv Gauba. A NITI Aayog finding cited in coverage said the QCOs raised raw-material prices 10–30% above global benchmarks.
  - Separately, the Ministry of Textiles withdrew the viscose staple fibre QCO by a Gazette notification of 18 November 2025 (Texprocil circular).
  - The exact product list differs slightly between outlets.
  - Seen: summaries only.
  - https://visionias.in/current-affairs/upsc-daily-news-summary/article/2025-11-14/business-standard/polity-and-governance/no-bis-certification-required-qcos-for-plastics-synthetic-fibres-scrapped
  - https://superkalam.com/current-affairs/14-11-2025/govt-revokes-14-qcos-on-goods-affecting-textile-value-chain-pg25-2e6dfa90-a76c-4451-83bc-4e942497ece1
  - https://www.alchempro.com/news/textile-news/india-withdraws-polyester-qcos-easing-industry-burden-306427-newsdetails.htm
  - https://texprocil.org/images/circulars/1763790729-Eserve-204_of_2025.pdf
- Suggested wording: "Updated [date]. The BIS quality-control requirement described on this slide no longer applies to the main polyester and polymer feedstocks. On 12 November 2025 the government rescinded the QCOs for PTA, ethylene glycol, polyester fibre and yarn, and several polymers including PP and PVC, after a NITI Aayog-led review, as reported in the trade press. The 'quality standards as protection' argument should therefore be read as historical."
- Confidence: several independent summaries agree on the 12 Nov 2025 date and on PTA and MEG. The full product list was not checked against the Gazette text. Severity is HIGH because the slide presents the requirement as current ("now require") and builds a policy opportunity on it.

Energy-led pressure persisted in August, and US consumer inflation was higher in September than in June. This is inference and is not stated by any source.

  - No confirmed generic launch dates in those markets were found.
