### 1. announced-vs-delivered-three-indian (HIGH)
- Claim in post: the 2025-26 row of the Karnataka excise table reads "~₹45,000 crore full-year target" with outcome "On a growth trajectory, final-year outcome not yet closed at time of writing". The 2026-27 row reads "₹40,000 crore stated target (some reporting cites ₹30,000 crore as an earlier ministerial marker)". The verdict text says: "The pattern across the three years we could fully close out is consistent: Karnataka sets an ambitious excise target each budget, misses or downgrades it mid-year, but still posts real year-on-year revenue growth"
- Current (as of 18 Oct 2026):
  - **The targets look swapped.** Two searches returned summaries saying the Karnataka Budget 2025-26 set the excise target at ₹40,000 crore, up from a 2024-25 revised estimate of ₹36,500 crore. The ₹45,000 crore figure is the FY2026-27 target, announced with the 2026 liquor-tax reforms (ABV-based duty from April 2026).
  - **Mid-year collections ran ahead of target.** Deccan Herald reported ₹16,358 crore collected in April–August 2025 against a target of ₹16,290 crore.
  - **The year-end figure is from a single secondary source.** A June 2026 Deccan Chronicle report, citing a Tamil Nadu government white paper, put Karnataka's 2025-26 excise collection at about ₹41,000 crore. That would meet the ₹40,000 crore target. No official Karnataka year-end figure was found.
  - Seen: summaries only.

  - https://www.deccanchronicle.com/amp/southern-states/tamil-nadu/tamil-nadus-excise-revenue-lags-behind-karnataka-maharashtra-govt-1964147
- Suggested wording: "Updated [date].
