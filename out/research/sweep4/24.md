## Findings
### 1. who-collects-indias-gst-and-who (HIGH)
- Claim in post: "Tax devolution — the states' constitutionally mandated share of the Centre's divisible tax pool (41% under the 15th Finance Commission) — is distributed by a formula weighted on population, income distance (favouring lower-income states), area, forest cover and demographic performance." Also: "UP's 17.62% devolution share in this instalment is close to its formula-fixed 17.93–17.96% share under the 15th Finance Commission's 2021-26 award" and "currently 41%, set by the 15th Finance Commission for 2021-26".
- Current (as of 18 Oct 2026): The 15th Finance Commission award ended with FY2025-26. The 1 August 2026 instalment the post analyses falls under the 16th Finance Commission award (2026-31). Summaries of that award (Feb 2026) say it kept the states' share of the divisible pool at 41%. It set Uttar Pradesh's share at 17.619% and Bihar's at 9.948%. It also added a new "contribution to GDP" criterion with a 10% weight, next to income distance (42.5%), population (17.5%), demographic performance, forest and ecology, and area (10% each). The weights differ slightly between summaries. UP's 17.62% in the instalment is therefore its 16th FC share, not a deviation from a 15th FC share. The post's list of formula criteria also leaves out the new GDP-contribution criterion. Seen: summaries only.
  - https://www.policycircle.org/?p=40882
  - https://barodaetrade.com/Reports/BOBEconomics-Summaryof16thFC17Feb26-Research.pdf
  - https://c4scourses.in/blog/16th-finance-commission-on-centre-state-fiscal-relations-2026-31-2/
- Suggested wording: "Updated 18 October 2026. From April 2026 devolution follows the 16th Finance Commission's award for 2026-31, not the 15th's. According to published summaries of that award, it kept the states' share at 41%, set Uttar Pradesh's share at about 17.62% (the figure in the August 2026 instalment) and added a 'contribution to GDP' criterion with a 10% weight. The redistributive pattern described below is unchanged."
- Confidence: Several independent summaries agree on 41% and on UP's 17.619%. The criterion weights vary slightly between summaries, so check them against the 16th FC report before quoting decimals.
