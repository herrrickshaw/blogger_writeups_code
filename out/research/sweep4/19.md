### 1. indias-gas-pipelines-run-at-half (HIGH)
- Claim in post: "the company has approved raising the line's capacity from 1.72 to 5.5 million tonnes a year" (with "Oil India's Numaligarh–Siliguri crude line at 91.46% for FY2024-25, both near or above the level regulators consider healthy")

The reported cost was about Rs 750 crore against an approved Rs 860 crore. The post describes the expansion as approved but not yet done. The 91.46% figure was measured against the old 1.72 MMTPA rating. Against roughly tripled capacity, the line's utilisation will be much lower until the Numaligarh refinery's expansion from 3 to 9 MMTPA fills it. No FY2025-26 NSPL throughput or utilisation figure was found. Sources give the pre-upgrade rating as both 1.72 and 1.77 MMTPA. Also, the lede and stat card call NSPL a "crude line", but the body and these sources describe it as a product pipeline. Seen: summaries only.

The Numaligarh–Siliguri product pipeline's expansion to 5.5 million tonnes a year described below as approved was inaugurated on 13 March 2026, per press reports.
