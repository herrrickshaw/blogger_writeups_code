## What the follow-up needs, in order of importance

1. **Did the auction go ahead?** That means at least two technically qualified bidders for each block (Sewell Rise-01, West Sewell Ridge-01). If fewer qualified, the auction for that block is annulled under the amended rule 9(10).
2. **Who bid?** Names of technically qualified bidders, if the ministry publishes them, and whether they are PSUs (e.g. NMDC, Coal India, KABIL, IREL) or private firms.
3. **Winning bid and bid parameter.** The premium bid (as a percentage, if that is the parameter), the reserve price, and the preferred bidder for each block.
4. **Any changes after 9 Oct 2026.** Corrigenda to the tender, deadline extensions (the first tranche was extended twice), changes to the terms, and the pre-bid conference record (what bidders asked).
5. **Context updates.**
   - Whether the ISA nodule contract (expiring 24 March 2027) has been extended again.
   - The Deep Ocean Mission's crewed dive schedule (last reported as December 2027).
   - Any MoEFCC environmental baseline or EIA rules for seabed mining.
   - Kerala's position, in case sand blocks are re-offered.
6. **Proposals check.** Re-read the five "What would fix this" proposals in the first piece and say whether any has moved: GSI data before auction, MoES technology sharing, MoEFCC rules, fisheries consultation, auction-outcome disclosure.
