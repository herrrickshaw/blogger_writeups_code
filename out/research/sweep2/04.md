### 1. ten-cubic-metres-hour-indias-first (HIGH)
- Claim in post: "International shipping has its own binding decarbonisation timetable. The International Maritime Organization's Net-Zero Framework sets a \"base target\" requiring a 4 per cent cut in ships' GHG Fuel Intensity (GFI) by 2028, rising to 30 per cent by 2035 ... A pricing mechanism attached to the framework applies to a share of international shipping emissions starting in 2028, with an initial carbon price of $100 per tonne of CO2." - Current (as of 13 Oct 2026): The IMO Net-Zero Framework was approved at MEPC 83 (April 2025) but its adoption was adjourned for 12 months at the October 2025 extraordinary session (vote reported as 57 to 49). At MEPC 84 (Apr-May 2026) 55 countries spoke in support and 51 favoured reopening or weakening it. The intersessional ISWG-GHG 22 met 1-4 Sept 2026 and ended without a final text; the next intersessional is 23-27 Nov, MEPC 85 is 30 Nov-3 Dec, and the resumed extraordinary session is provisionally set for 4 Dec 2026 "subject to discussions at MEPC 85". I found no source confirming adoption. So the 2028 targets and $100/t price are a proposal still awaiting a vote, not a binding timetable. Sources (search summaries only, not opened): https://www.dnv.com/news/2025/decision-on-the-imo-net-zero-framework-delayed-for-one-year/ ; https://shipandbunker.com/news/world/596439-iswg-ghg-22-working-group-ends-with-net-zero-framework-text-expected-at-mepc-85 ; https://datamarnews.com/noticias/imo-net-zero-framework-survives-assault-but-adoption-remains-uncertain/ ; https://www.hellenicshippingnews.com/imo-net-zero-shipping-talks-to-resume-in-2026/
