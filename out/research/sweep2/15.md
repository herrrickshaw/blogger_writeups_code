### 1. indias-79-lakh-crore-rural-water (MEDIUM)

- Current (as of 13 Oct 2026): a newer Lok Sabha reply (30 July 2026) reportedly puts coverage at 82.09%, about 15.89 crore of 19.36 crore rural households, as of 18 July 2026. Source: Kashmir Life, https://kashmirlife.net/jammu-kashmir-has-3-6-lakh-rural-households-yet-to-get-tap-water-connection-under-jal-jeevan-mission-445608/ (search summary only, not opened). The target is tap connections to all 19.36 crore rural households by December 2028, and the Cabinet note put coverage at about 15.80 crore (81.61%) at that time. Sources: PIB-derived items at https://www.pmindia.gov.in/en/news_updates/cabinet-approves-extension-of-jal-jeevan-mission-jjm-period-up-to-december-2028-with-enhanced-outlay-and-restructured-implementation-focusing-on-structural-reforms-in-rural-drinking-water-supply-sec and https://www.drishtiias.com/daily-updates/daily-news-analysis/cabinet-extends-jal-jeevan-mission-till-2028/print_manually (both search summaries only, not opened). Because the extension was approved before the post's 29 Aug 2026 publication, its omission is context rather than a stale claim. The post's Rs 7.9 lakh crore "remaining spend" figure is the portal's B15 estimated cost of upcoming schemes, a different measure from the Rs 8.69 lakh crore Cabinet outlay, so a one-line pointer would help readers.
- Suggested wording: "Update, 13 October 2026: a Lok Sabha reply of 30 July 2026 reportedly puts national rural tap-connection coverage at about 82.1% (15.89 crore of 19.36 crore households, as of 18 July 2026), up from the 81.6% cited here from the February 2026 answer. The conclusion is unchanged. JJM was also extended as JJM 2.0 to December 2028 (Cabinet, 10 March 2026, outlay Rs 8.69 lakh crore)." Hedge the 82.09% figure as "reportedly" until the Lok Sabha record is checked.
- Confidence: tap-rate update is a single source (unverified against the primary record); JJM 2.0 extension is supported by 2+ sources (PIB-derived and Drishti IAS summaries). The B15 scheme counts (5,32,205 upcoming, 3,445 not started) are live-dashboard numbers; I could not check the portal, so whether they have moved since August is unverified.

Savills reports about 1.8 GW IT after 258 MW added. Vestian gives 1.4–1.6 GW. Sources: https://www.constructionworld.in/amp/latest-construction-technology/india---s-data-centre-capacity-reaches-1-789-mw-in-h1-2026/97528 and https://indianinfrastructure.com/2026/07/22/indias-data-centre-capacity-additions-grow-59-per-cent-in-h1-2026-says-industry-report/ (search summaries only). The 1.2–1.5 GW range is now at or below the low end of the newer counts. Definitions differ (top-7 markets vs all-India; IT load vs total).
  - (b) The "mid-2026" date has passed. I found no report that an 80 MW Google/Adani site went live, and no figure for it at all; the search returned nothing on it. Google's $15bn Visakhapatnam hub had a foundation ceremony reported for 28 April 2026 (https://www.businesstoday.in/technology/story/googles-big-india-bet-15-billion-vizag-ai-data-centre-foundation-to-be-laid-on-april-28-527498-2026-04-27, search summary only). An earlier Google subsidiary proposal gave July 2028 for operations (https://www.datacenterdynamics.com/en/news/google-subsidiary-proposes-1gw-data-center-in-visakhapatnam-india/, search summary only). I found no report of a 2026 go-live, but absence from search results is not evidence it has not happened.
  - (c) The Jamnagar project that surfaced in searches is a 168 MW built-to-suit facility for Meta, due within two years (reported June 2026), per https://www.nbmcw.com/news/infra-development/reliance-to-build-indias-first-built-to-suit-ai-data-centre-for-meta-in-jamnagar.html (search summary only). The post does not mention Meta. Whether this relates to the post's ">120 MW phase 1" is unverified. No commissioning reported.
  - The $210bn Adani + Reliance figure is repeated in a mid-2026 report (45% of announced investment) but that is the same announced-commitment basis the post already caveats; no change needed.
- Suggested wording: for (a) "Operational capacity is roughly 1.4-1.8 GW depending on source and scope (Cushman & Wakefield counted 1,789 MW across the top seven markets at the end of H1 2026)". For (b) change "due mid-2026" to "originally due mid-2026; commissioning not confirmed as of 13 October 2026". For (c) optionally add "Reliance has also reported a 168 MW built-to-suit Jamnagar facility for Meta".
- Confidence: (a) supported by 2+ summaries, consistent with each other; (b) and (c) single source or unverified.
