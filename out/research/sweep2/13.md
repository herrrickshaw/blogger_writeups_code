### 1. one-nation-one-gst-rate-what-true-flat (HIGH)
- Claim in post: "tobacco products stay on the old rate-plus-cess regime for now, specifically because the Centre's compensation-cess-backed borrowing has to be repaid before that transition can complete." Also in the sources note: "the deferred tobacco/pan-masala transition tied to compensation-cess loan repayment". The post says "Compiled via web research on 29 September 2026", so this was already stale when written.
- Current (as of 13 Oct 2026): The GST compensation cess on tobacco and pan masala ended on 1 February 2026 (government notification, loan obligations discharged). From that date pan masala, cigarettes and tobacco products are taxed at 40% GST, with biris at 18%. The old cess is replaced by an additional central excise duty on tobacco (Central Excise (Amendment) Act, 2025) and a "Health Security se National Security Cess" on pan masala, designed to keep the tax incidence about the same. Sources (search summaries only, not opened): https://taxo.online/latest-news/01-01-2026-gst-compensation-cess-on-tobacco-to-end-on-feb-1-orders-govt-new-laws-notified/ ; https://www.outlookbusiness.com/news/additional-excise-duty-on-tobacco-health-cess-on-pan-masala-to-be-effective-feb-1 ; https://swarajyamag.com/amp/story/economy/higher-taxes-on-tobacco-and-pan-masala-from-1-february-2026-as-centre-brings-new-excise-duty-and-health-cess-regime
- Suggested wording: "Update, 13 October 2026: the compensation cess on tobacco and pan masala ended on 1 February 2026. Pan masala, cigarettes and other tobacco products now carry 40% GST (biris 18%), plus an additional central excise duty on tobacco and a separate health and national-security cess on pan masala. The 'deferred transition' described above has therefore taken place, though these goods still sit outside a single GST rate." Also adjust the phrase "40 per cent rate GST 2.0 actually adopted for sin and luxury goods" if it implies tobacco was excluded (not checked in detail).
- Confidence: confirmed by 2+ sources (several outlets agree on the 1 Feb 2026 end date and the 40% rate); not opened, so exact rates for tobacco products should be checked against the CBIC notification.
