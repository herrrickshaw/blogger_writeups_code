- Current (as of 13 Oct 2026):
  - The 123 banks / 30 countries / 156 SRVAs / 26 banks count appears to be a February 2025 figure (Rajya Sabha written reply), not February 2026. A search summary quotes "A Rajya Sabha written reply said RBI has permitted 123 Correspondent banks from 30 trading partner countries for opening of total 156 SRVAs with 26 AD banks" and calls February 2025 "the most recent parliamentary count I found". The Business Standard article "RBI allows 156 vostro accounts with 26 banks for rupee trade settlement" carries a URL ID (…125020701044) consistent with 7 Feb 2025. Source: https://www.business-standard.com/finance/news/rbi-allows-156-vostro-accounts-with-26-banks-for-rupee-trade-settlement-125020701044_1.html (summary only). The "(Feb 2026)" label in the stat box is therefore probably a year mislabel, and the "most recent reporting" framing is stale by a year.
  - New: on 17 July 2026 the RBI issued a consolidated SRVA circular replacing five earlier circulars (2022-2025), effective immediately; AD Category-I banks can open SRVAs for overseas banks without prior RBI approval. Sources: https://www.business-standard.com/finance/news/rbi-issues-consolidated-norms-for-special-rupee-vostro-accounts-and-trade-126071701368_1.html (summary), https://ksandk.com/newsletter/special-rupee-vostro-accounts/ (summary), https://teamleaseregtech.com/updates/article/57900/rbi-issued-a-circular-regarding-the-special-rupee-vostro-accounts-srvas/ (summary). The post does not mention this circular.
  - The "₹14,000 crore in February 2026" figure could not be confirmed or refuted; no 2026 Parliament or RBI figure in crore was found.
  - Russia: a May 2026 report says India and Russia are exploring a dynamic rupee-rouble rate, which matches the post's wording. See https://indiaseatradenews.com/india-russia-explore-dynamic-rupee-rouble-rate-to-overcome-trade-issues/ (summary). Claims of 90%+ (one says 96%) of bilateral trade in national currencies are official statements, unverified.
- Suggested wording: "Correction, 13 October 2026: the count of 123 correspondent banks and 156 Special Rupee Vostro Accounts with 26 Indian banks dates from a parliamentary reply of February 2025, not February 2026. On 17 July 2026 the RBI consolidated its SRVA rules into a single circular. A current account count and the ₹14,000 crore February 2026 figure have not been re-verified."
