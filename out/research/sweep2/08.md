### 1. india-has-132-compressed-biogas-plants (HIGH)
- Claim in post: "India has commissioned 132 as of January 2026, producing roughly 920 tonnes a day." and "the regulator's own September 2026 count says only 17 are actually connected to a city gas network by dedicated pipeline". The post quotes PNGRB: "connectivity of 17 CBG plants through dedicated CBG pipelines was achieved" for "around 130 functional CBG plants", with "around 20 ... under various stages of construction".
- Current (as of 13 Oct 2026): Search results suggest 17 is an older figure and the newer PNGRB count is higher.
  - A PNGRB October 2025 deck lists "Already Connected 17" (102 TPD offtake): https://www.pngrb.gov.in/pdf/CBG/20251010_CBG_CGD.pdf (seen as search result title/summary only).
  - A February 2026 PNGRB status report lists 24 connected, 15 in progress, 20 with proposals initiated; a PNGRB "FY 2025-26 Connectivity and Performance Report" (file name 20260806_CBG_pipeline.pdf) is summarised as 24 of 207 functional plants already connected: https://www.pngrb.gov.in/pdf/CBG/20260806_CBG_pipeline.pdf and the secondary write-up https://bioenergytimes.com/405-cbg-plants-lack-planned-pipeline-connectivity-amid-higher-blending-mandate/ (both search summary only; the report itself not opened).
  - Plant count: a Drishti IAS summary of the Gobardhan portal says 210 CBG plants commissioned, 324 under construction, 1,261 registered, as of June 2026: https://www.drishtiias.com/daily-updates/daily-news-analysis/india-on-track-to-achieve-3-compressed-biogas-blending-target/print_manually (search summary only). The same source puts blending at nearly 2% against a 3% FY27 target.
  - PNGRB approved CBG-injection guidelines on 11 Feb 2026 (https://pngrb.gov.in/pdf/press-note/20260211_PR.pdf, summary only).
- Suggested wording: "Update, 13 October 2026: this piece's figure of 17 pipeline-connected plants and 132 commissioned plants reflects earlier PNGRB and SATAT counts. Later PNGRB material (a connectivity report dated August 2026, as reported by BioEnergy Times) puts the number of already-connected plants at 24 of about 207 functional plants, and the Gobardhan portal showed about 210 commissioned plants in June 2026. We have not opened these documents; figures should be checked against the PNGRB CBG Cell page." Also reconsider the headline/title "Reaches 17 of Them" and the "September 2026 count" attribution: the post cites a September 2026 count of 17, while the only dated PNGRB reports found are Oct 2025 (17), Feb 2026 (24) and Aug 2026 (24 of 207). Worth checking whether the post's quote comes from an older document.
- Confidence: single source (PNGRB file names plus one trade write-up; plant count from one secondary summary). Unverified at primary level.
