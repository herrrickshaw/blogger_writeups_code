## Sector notes

### A. Export composition: the frame for the article
- **Size in India:** In the RBI survey, software and IT-enabled services exports were US$221.4bn in FY26. About 69% of that was computer services (IT services, software) and about 31% was ITES, mostly BPO at US$56bn. Engineering services were about 5% in FY25. Separately, the balance-of-payments category "business services", which includes most GCC, R&D, consulting and technical exports, reached US$124.2bn in FY26, up about 16% from US$107.2bn.
- **AI impact evidence (indirect):** The derived RBI split shows ITES growing only about 2% (66.6 → 68.0) and BPO almost flat (55.8 → 56.0) in FY26, while computer services grew about 11%. ITES's share fell from 32.6% to 30.7%. This fits AI pressure on voice and back-office work, but it is not proof of it: currency effects, pricing and survey revisions could explain part of it. Flag as derived.
- **Counter-evidence:** Business-services exports grew faster than software exports in FY26, and Baldwin's work (already in the archive) puts them above their pre-AI trend.
- **What is not known:** The FY25 and FY26 split of business services into R&D, professional and management consulting, and technical/trade-related was not found. It needs the RBI's detailed services-trade tables, which this agent could not reach.

### B. Exposure studies (which jobs are at risk)
- **IMF (Jan 2024):** About 26% of Indian employment is highly exposed, against 40% for emerging markets and 60% for advanced economies. India's share is low because most of its workforce is in farm and manual jobs. The exposed share sits in exactly the occupations that offshoring employs: professionals, clerical workers and technicians.
- **ILO WP140 (May 2025):** Clerical work remains the most exposed group: data entry, typists, accounting and bookkeeping clerks, administrative secretaries. These are core BPM/F&A task profiles. Exposure in digitised professional and technical jobs has risen since the 2023 index.
- **World Bank SADU (Oct 2025):** South Asia overall is only "moderately exposed", but postings for the most exposed occupations fell about 20% after late 2022, while AI-skill postings rose and pay a premium of about 30%.
- **NITI Aayog (Oct 2025):** The only official Indian estimate found. IT services and customer experience (BPM) are named as the "frontline". Under business-as-usual, the tech workforce falls from 7.5-8mn to about 6mn by 2031; up to about 4mn new roles if a National AI Talent Mission is pursued. Press summaries disagree: one gives about 1.5mn lost, another up to 2mn.
- **Stanford "Canaries" (US):** The evidence is concentrated on young workers. Ages 22-25 in AI-exposed jobs (software developers, customer service) lost 13-19% of employment relative to less-exposed peers, depending on the version. The effect shows up in jobs where AI automates rather than augments. This is US data, but it covers the same occupations offshored to India.
- **Anthropic Economic Index (May 2026):** India's Claude usage leans toward Computer & Mathematical tasks (26.2% vs 23.8% globally) and education, with overall usage per working-age person low (index 0.3). This describes observed usage only. It is not evidence of job effects.

### C. Entry-level hiring signals
- **Naukri JobSpeak, Sep 2026:** IT/software services postings −4% y/y, while AI/ML postings +20%. Fresher postings were only +1%. BPO/ITES postings +4%, which does not show a collapse in BPO hiring.
- **Campus hiring:** Infosys is keeping at least 20,000 freshers in FY27. Wipro set no fresher target for FY27, citing a "very volatile environment" (CHRO Saurabh Govil, Apr 2026). TCS and HCLTech FY27 numbers seen were ambiguous or undated, so no credible figure found.
- **Counter-evidence:** foundit says AI-led roles are now 4% of fresher hiring, up from about 1%, but half of these are AI operations work, not model building.

### D. Offset: GCCs and AI-skilled demand
- foundit projects about 510k GCC hires in 2026 (+12%) and says about 64% of new GCC roles need AI/data/automation skills. This is a vendor estimate (L-M). Naukri shows GCC postings +4% y/y in Sep 2026. TeamLease Digital estimates AI-workforce penetration in retail GCCs at 2.1% (2022) → 4.8% (2025) → 7.2% (2026 estimate).

### What is not known (cross-cutting)
- The FY26 NASSCOM headcount split between IT services, BPM and ER&D (the brief's item 2): no credible figure found.
- India-specific numbers on how many jobs have actually been displaced (as opposed to exposed): no credible figure found.
- An India version of the Stanford-style age/occupation payroll analysis: none found.
