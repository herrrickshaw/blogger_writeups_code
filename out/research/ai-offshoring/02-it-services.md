## Sector notes

### Application development & maintenance (ADM)
- **Size in India:** No NASSCOM breakdown by service line was found. Broker estimates put application services at 40-70% of revenue depending on the firm (Jefferies), with custom application maintenance alone at ~35% of a typical firm (HSBC, Oct 2025). This is the largest single pool of offshored work. HCLTech classes ~40% of the industry as "AI-disrupted services": app development support, infrastructure operations and customer support.
- **AI impact evidence:**
  - Coding-assistant code shares are rising fast. Cognizant went from ~20% to ~30% of internal code AI-generated during 2025, then reportedly "nearly 40%" AI-assisted in Q1 2026. Google reported ~75% of new code AI-generated and human-reviewed (Apr 2026). Microsoft's latest figure is 20-30% (Apr 2025).
  - Pricing pressure shows up in company statements: Persistent's CEO said clients want the same work for 25-30% less (Reuters, Aug 2026). Infosys trimmed its FY27 guidance, with a broker citing pricing pressure and softer volumes (Jul 2026). HFS reports renegotiations within 24 months of signing, hitting hardest on contracts above $50mn a year.
  - Gartner, as reported by Le Monde Informatique, forecasts that 60% of large IT services contracts will carry AI gain-share "recovery" clauses by 2027.
  - Deal structures are changing. HCLTech's E.ON deal reportedly pays nothing in year 1 and ties later fees to efficiency gains (Reuters via The Star, Aug 2026, "people familiar"). That makes it single-source.
- **Offsetting / counter-evidence:**
  - AI revenue lines are now material. TCS runs at ~$3.1bn annualised, reported as >10% of revenue (Oct 2026). Infosys is at 8.2% of revenue (Jul 2026). HCLTech booked $171mn in the quarter, +62% YoY (Jul 2026).
  - TCS has had three straight quarters of net headcount additions (Q2 FY27 +4,258). Pzena (Q2 2026) says TTM bookings are up 11%, with new work outpacing per-contract deflation.
  - An Oct 2025 broker note argued that clients reinvest GenAI savings in new work. It predates 2026 and is opinion.
- **What is not known:** ADM revenue or headcount by line for any big-five firm. No named mega-deal renewal at a disclosed lower price was found; HFS says it could not identify specific contracts. "AI revenue" definitions differ by firm and may relabel existing work.

### Software testing / QA
- **Size in India:** ~375,000 testing and QA/QC professionals, according to staffing firm Xpheno via PolicyCircle. The period is the year to 30 April; the year is not confirmed. Another source says ~450,000. Both are single-source and unverified.
- **AI impact evidence:** The pool grew <1% YoY, and Xpheno estimates ~40% do highly automatable work. Demand is shifting to SDET and automation roles. No company-level testing headcount disclosure was found.
- **Offsetting:** A vendor research firm (Market Research Future) projects India's outsourced testing market growing from $2.58bn in 2024 to $11.1bn by 2035. This is low credibility.
- **What is not known:** Testing headcount at any listed firm, and any official statistic.

### Infrastructure / IT helpdesk / service desk
- **Size in India:** No credible figure found for the service desk or infrastructure management workforce.
- **AI impact evidence:**
  - TCS chairman N Chandrasekaran (AGM, Jun 2026) said TCS is moving toward an equal number of employees and AI agents and "will hire less", but does not plan to downsize. This is the strongest primary-ish statement found, but it is not specific to the service desk.
  - Wipro's Q1 FY27 release (Jul 2026) lists end-user support and infrastructure renewals that cite AI and automation to raise engineer productivity, plus a vendor-consolidation deal with "structural cost optimization". Price terms are not disclosed.
  - Salesforce forecasts AI will resolve 50% of service cases in India by 2027, up from 30% today. This covers customer service, not IT, and is a vendor forecast.
- **What is not known:** Any audited L1/L2 headcount reduction at an Indian infrastructure managed-services provider.

### Legacy modernisation (mainframe/COBOL)
- **Size in India:** No credible figure found.
- **Evidence:** This is the main counter-argument for demand. IBM watsonx Code Assistant for Z and AWS Transform for mainframe automate COBOL-to-Java conversion, which could pull forward migration projects. EY GDS Chennai advertised a "Mainframe Modernization Gen AI" senior role (Jun 2026). Sonata Software won a $73mn five-year "AI-led modernisation" deal with an India centre, but it is not mainframe-specific and the date is unclear.
- **What is not known:** No 2026 COBOL or mainframe deal by an Indian firm was verified. The same tools that create migration projects also reduce the person-effort per project.
