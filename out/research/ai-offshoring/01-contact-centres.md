## Sector notes

### Size in India
- **No credible 2025-26 figure for India's CX/contact-centre headcount alone.** NASSCOM's last clear split I found is >1.4mn BPM staff, about one-third in customer-interaction work. It is c. 2021 and excludes domestic and in-house centres. Wisemonk's "1.4mn CX professionals" (May 2026) is a vendor number and may reuse the NASSCOM figure.
- Firm-level anchors: TP India 90,000+ (Aug 2026), Firstsource ~36,900 group-wide (Jun 2026, not all in India), Concentrix group ~450k (third-party estimate, not a filing; no India split found).

### AI impact evidence
1. **Global CX outsourcers are shrinking or flat, and they name AI.**
   - Concentrix cut FY26 guidance in Jun 2026 and its shares fell ~21%. On the Q3 call (~29 Sep 2026) the CFO said revenue "reflected an accelerated deployment of AI for clients, as well as client decisions to reduce support for certain customer groups." Management also cited cuts in support by hyperscaler clients, which it said were limited to a small subset of clients.
   - TTEC's customer-care segment (Engage) fell 7.5% YoY in Q1 2026 and 12.1% in Q2 2026. In 2025 TTEC took a US$193mn impairment on its digital managed-services unit, tied to the shift toward AI-led consulting (press summary).
   - TP's like-for-like revenue fell 1.7% in H1 2026. TP blamed Trust & Safety (content moderation) and the shift offshore, and did not explicitly blame AI.
2. **Offshore shift: India gains share of a smaller market.** This is the key nuance for India. TP (Q1 2026) says India revenue growth "continued to accelerate" because of demand for offshore BPO, and that this shift lowers group revenue but lifts margin. Concentrix also names the offshore mix as a revenue headwind. When clients cut costs, they move remaining human work to India and automate the rest. Indian seat counts can hold up even while global revenue falls.
3. **Client-side automation removing support work.**
   - Salesforce cut ~4,000 support roles, from 9,000 to 5,000 (Benioff, ~Sep 2025). The company said it "no longer need[s] to actively backfill support engineer roles." Salesforce runs large support operations in India, but I found no source that says how many of the cut roles were in India.
   - Klarna's claimed AI capacity is equivalent to 853 agents. Klarna's support was largely outsourced, so the cut work would have fallen on BPO vendors rather than Klarna staff, though I found no source naming vendors or countries.
4. **Indian mid-tier firms are split.**
   - Firstsource grew strongly: +19.7% in FY26, +22.9% YoY in Q1 FY27. It sells "UnBPO", an agent-first, outcome-priced model. Headcount stayed roughly flat, around 36-37k, through FY26-Q1 FY27, so revenue is decoupling from headcount.
   - HGS revenue fell 2% in FY26 and profit fell 73%. Management says clients are moving from exploring AI to deploying it.
5. **Accent AI keeps Indian voice seats competitive.** TP invested US$13mn in Sanas and became exclusive reseller of its accent-translation tool. It deployed the tool in Indian centres serving foreign clients (Bloomberg, Feb-Mar 2025). This strengthens India against the Philippines rather than removing jobs.
6. **Hiring signals.** The World Bank South Asia Update (Oct 2025, via Policy Circle) found job postings down ~20% in occupations most exposed to AI. Gulf News, citing Reuters, says India's BPM net headcount additions fell to "under 20,000" a year. That is a single source and I could not locate the Reuters original.

### Offsetting job creation / counter-evidence
- TP reportedly hires ~10,000 people a year in India for AI data-labelling, annotation and model-training work (NewsBytes; date unclear, single source).
- Gartner (Sep 2025): a fully agentless contact centre is "unlikely and undesirable." It also expects half of firms planning big AI-driven service cuts to drop those plans by 2027.
- Klarna partly reversed course in May 2025. Its CEO said cost-focus produced "lower quality," and Klarna rehired humans for some support.
- Salesforce says it moved "hundreds" of staff into sales, professional services and customer success.
- Concentrix: net new-logo sales involving AI rose 63% quarter on quarter, and management expects >US$6bn of "new business" revenue in 2027. New revenue lines do not necessarily mean new jobs.
- Philippines: still adding jobs (+~80k in 2025), but IBPAP has cut its 2028 target. Demand is shifting from contact-centre seats toward analytics, BI and work for global capability centres (GCCs).

### What is not known
- India-only CX headcount for FY25-26. NASSCOM has not published a split I could find.
- The India split of Concentrix, TTEC and Startek headcount. Nothing found for iEnergizer, Startek or Tech Mahindra BPS in 2026.
- How many of Salesforce's and Klarna's eliminated support roles were in India or at Indian vendors.
- Hard data on growth in domestic Indian-language CX, or on AI-trainer and escalation roles. **No credible figure found.**
- TP's current Sanas rollout scale. Nothing newer than Mar 2025 found.

## Claims seen but not usable
- **"Indian BPO employment could fall from 4mn to <1mn by 2030."** An analyst discussion relayed by Outsource Accelerator. It is a projection, and the 4mn base does not match NASSCOM's figures.
- **"Generative voice AI eliminating millions of entry-level call-centre roles"** (Outsource Accelerator via WION, May 2026). No data behind it.
- **"AI takes 20% of BPO roles but creates 40% more jobs."** Attributed to a former NASSCOM VP in Nearshore Americas. Opinion, not data.
- **TP plans to hire 50,000-60,000 in India.** The reports are undated and probably pre-date the 90,000 figure.
- **"Klarna cut 40% of its workforce through AI."** That figure is total headcount change including attrition, not AI layoffs.
- **iX Hero "~US$1.3bn revenue"** (Concentrix Q3 summary). This conflicts with the US$120mn software ARR figure, and I could not verify it.
- **TP AI savings of €150-170mn.** From a job-site aggregator (Simplify); other summaries give conflicting restructuring numbers.
- **India BPO market of US$49.9bn (2025) to US$139bn (2033)** (Astute Analytica via a vendor). A market-research projection.
- **"67% of Philippine BPOs have adopted AI"** (stats aggregator). No source given.
- **FusionCX "BPO growing faster than ever"** blog. A vendor's promotional piece.
