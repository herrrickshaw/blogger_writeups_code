## Possible angle

**"India's military quantum plan has four pillars. So far only one, secure communication, has hardware in service."**
- **Communication:** QKD has been bought (the Army's 2024 iDEX contract) and PQC is being rolled out.
- **Computing:** a 6-qubit lab processor.
- **Sensing:** prototype contracts.
- **Materials:** the cryogenics that quantum computers need are still imported; the first Indian dilution refrigerator prototype is due in Q4 2027.
- **Funding:** NQM's reported spending in FY2024-25 (&#8377;33 crore utilised against an average of about &#8377;750 crore a year) is the other thread; it needs checking against the Parliament answers first.

Possible "What would fix this" agencies:
- **MoD/HQ IDS:** publish an unclassified summary of the framework's milestones.
- **DST:** publish NQM year-wise releases and utilisation.
- **DRDO:** publish TDF quantum project status.
- **CERT-In/NSCS:** a PQC migration deadline for government networks.
