## 4. Recurring causes of delay and underuse

1. **Breakwaters and marine works.**
   - Dabhol: the Enron-era half-built breakwater was finished only in Apr 2025, so for years there were no monsoon cargoes.
   - Chhara: a cargo could not moor for over a week in 2024; the breakwater was still being completed after COD.
   - Jafrabad: monsoon damage in 2019, then cyclones.
   - Hazira: its expansion needs a new breakwater and 2.8 million m³ of dredging.
   - Ennore: channel dredging had to finish before the first cargo.
   - Dahej 3rd jetty: monsoon slippage.
2. **Evacuation pipeline not synchronised with the terminal.** This is the biggest cause of *underuse*.
   - Kochi: GAIL's Kochi–Mangaluru/Bengaluru line was late; land and compensation costs nearly doubled its cost (₹2,915 cr to over ₹5,750 cr); utilisation stayed at 5–20% for a decade.
   - Ennore: pipelines were "still under construction" at COD.
   - Chhara: the pipeline runs through the Gir ESZ.
   - ICF/PNGRB: "islanded" terminals; PNGRB now requires terminal registration to be synchronised with pipeline capacity booking. LNG India Summit 2025 report [secondary]
3. **Land, lease and concession disputes.**
   - Mundra: a promoter–Gujarat government dispute over lease and sub-concession kept it idle 15 months after the Oct 2018 inauguration.
   - Gopalpur: the plan was restructured from FSRU to land-based before FID.
4. **Environmental and forest clearance.**
   - Ennore: CRZ and MoEF clearance conditions (2012 reply).
   - Hazira: 10 MMTPA expansion stalled in 2021 over forest clearance; cleared only in Sep 2025.
   - Chhara: wildlife and ESZ clearance for its pipeline.
5. **FSRU charter and ownership problems.**
   - Jafrabad: the owned FSRU was leased to Botas, then sold ($399m); the terminal is still looking for a charter.
   - Jaigarh: delivery and charter re-signed (*Höegh Giant*, Dec 2020) after a Q4 2018 target.
   - Gopalpur: the FSRU charter was excluded from capex, then the FSRU plan was dropped for land-based.
6. **Partner and offtake risk.**
   - Mundra: equity partner search (IOC deal 2017).
   - Dhamra: IOC and GAIL signed an equity MoU in 2016 but never invested, while holding 20-year take-or-pay tolling contracts.
   - Merchant capacity without anchor users (Chhara, Dhamra, Ennore) runs at 2–22%.
7. **Exogenous shocks.** COVID-19 lockdowns (Dhamra, Jafrabad); India–Pakistan security tensions (Dahej 22.5, May 2025); Hormuz disruption and force majeure in Mar 2026 (Elara). The last is a utilisation risk, not a build delay.
