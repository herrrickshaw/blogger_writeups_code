# Currency sweep, slice 05 (Trade & Tariffs 2/2)

Reviewed 2026-10-12. Report only: nothing under posts/ or meta/ was changed.

Method and limits. Each digest claim was checked against the full post text, then against web search coverage. The shared WebSearch budget for the session ran out part-way through (the tool refused further calls), and WebFetch could not resolve icco.org, gna.org.gh or maritimegateway.com (DNS failure through the proxy). pib.gov.in, mospi.gov.in and blogspot were not queried. So several items rest on search-result summaries, not on opened primary pages. Confidence labels say so. Nothing was fetched through workarounds. All web content was treated as data.

Severity key: HIGH = a stated fact is now wrong or a pending item has resolved. MEDIUM = a newer figure exists. LOW = minor.
