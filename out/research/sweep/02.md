# Currency sweep, slice 02 (Energy & Fuels 2/3), run 2026-10-12

Scope: 32 posts in digest_02.json. About 20 were screened for checkable claims; 13 were searched or compared against post text. The shared web-search budget ran out part-way through (limit of 200 calls per turn shared across all reviewers), so a block of posts was NOT checked (listed at the end). Fetching individual news pages also failed (DNS errors), so some findings rest on search-result summaries only. Blocked domains (pib.gov.in, mospi.gov.in, blogspot) were not attempted; news coverage was used instead. Nothing under posts/ or meta/ was modified.

### 1. indias-biofuel-rules-are-being-written (HIGH, provisional)
- Claim: scorecard row "Aug 2026, Supreme Court, Modi Biotech v. VINP and others, Appeals; Q4 allocation, Pending, interim deal"; text: "Hearing is set for 23 September 2026."
- Current: a trade-press headline and summary indicate the Supreme Court has since set aside the Karnataka High Court orders that favoured a dedicated ethanol plant's allocation, holding that ethanol procurement is a matter of government policy. See [Supreme Court restores level playing field in ethanol procurement: ISMA (Agro Spectrum India)](https://agrospectrumindia.com/news/112/33599/supreme-court-restores-level-playing-field-in-ethanol-procurement-isma.html). The publication date could not be read. The search summary matches the VINP/Modi Biotech dispute, but the case name is not stated in the snippet.
- Suggested replacement (only after the judgment date and case name are confirmed from the judgment or a dated report): change "Pending" to "Decided [date]: Supreme Court set aside the Karnataka High Court orders; OMC procurement treated as government policy". Revise the "Zero-sum... Whatever the Supreme Court decides will reprice the offtake promise" paragraph to past tense.
- Confidence: single source, date unverified. An editor should open the report and confirm before changing anything.
- Also relevant to gail-and-sumitomo-both-turned-their (it mentions the Feb 2026 Karnataka HC TruAlt matter as unrelated; no change needed there unless TruAlt is a party).

### 2. upi-fees-are-now-legal-petrol-pumps (MEDIUM)
- Claim: "The Taxation and Other Laws (Amendment) Bill, introduced 4 August 2026, replaces that blanket ban"; elsewhere "On 4 August 2026 that shield came off".
- Current: Parliament passed the bill in August 2026. The Lok Sabha passed it first and the Rajya Sabha returned it by voice vote a few days later. Sources disagree on exact dates (about 6 and 10 August). The Finance Minister called it an enabling provision only. See [LiveLaw: Parliament passes Taxation Laws Amendment Bill](https://www.livelaw.in/news-updates/parliament-passes-taxation-laws-amendment-bill-paving-way-for-possible-upi-charges-545218) and [Outlook Money](https://www.outlookmoney.com/news/parliament-passes-taxation-amendment-bill-paving-way-for-possible-upi-charges). "Came off on 4 August" conflates introduction with enactment.
- Suggested wording: "The Taxation and Other Laws (Amendment) Bill, introduced on 4 August 2026 and passed by both Houses that month, replaces that blanket ban..." and replace "On 4 August 2026 that shield came off" with "From August 2026 that shield is gone".
- Confidence: confirmed by 2+ sources (exact dates disputed).

- Confidence: rates confirmed by 2+ sources; dealer boycott is single source.

See [Cars24 explainer](https://www.cars24.com/news/auto/excise-duty-on-petrol-and-diesel-reduced-what-changed-what-didnt-and-why/) and [AIR/newsonair, 27 Mar 2026](https://www.newsonair.gov.in/central-government-announces-duty-cut-on-fuel-export-taxes-to-ensure-domestic-supply). Reports also say retail prices stayed unchanged and the benefit went to OMCs; the post says the cut was "to hold pump prices down", which is compatible.
- Suggested wording: "On 26 March 2026 (announced 27 March)..." or keep the date and add "(effective 26 March per Notification 11/2026)".
- Confidence: confirmed by 2 sources on amounts; date minor.
