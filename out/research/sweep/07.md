### 1. indias-cotton-yield-fell-from-566-kg-to (MEDIUM)
Claim: "CAI has put 2025-26 cotton imports at a record ~60 lakh bales, revised up from 47 lakh, with mills front-loading purchases to use a temporary 11% import-duty exemption before it lapses; roughly 51 lakh bales had already landed by end-June." Current:
- CAI's 10 August 2026 crop committee raised the 2025-26 pressing estimate by 2 lakh bales to 339 lakh bales, raised the import estimate from 60 to 62 lakh bales, and reported about 54 lakh bales landed by 31 July. Exports held at 15 lakh bales; consumption held at 348 lakh bales. See [KNN India, 12 Aug 2026](https://knnindia.co.in/news/newsdetails/sectors/agri-food/cotton-association-of-india-raises-2025-26-cotton-pressing-estimate-to-339-lakh-bales) and [Investment Guru India](https://investmentguruindia.com/newsdetail/cai-raises-2025-26-cotton-pressing-estimate-to-339-lakh-bales-by-amit-gupta-kedia-advisory436404). This revision pre-dates the post's 27 September publication.
- Duty: the first duty-free window (from 19 Aug 2025) ended on 31 Dec 2025 and the 11% duty returned on 1 Jan 2026 ([Rural Voice](https://eng.ruralvoice.in/agribusiness/duty-free-cotton-import-window-closes-11-pc-duty-back-in-force-from-january-1.html)). A second waiver (BCD plus AIDC) was notified on 30 May 2026 for 1 June to 31 October 2026 ([Tribune](https://www.tribuneindia.com/news/business/centre-waives-customs-duty-on-cotton-imports-from-june-1-to-oct-31/); one outlet gives 30 Oct). So the "before it lapses" deadline is about 19 days away, and the post does not name the date or say which waiver it means.

Suggested wording: "CAI's 10 August estimate puts 2025-26 imports at a record 62 lakh bales (up from 60 lakh, and 47 lakh earlier), with about 54 lakh landed by 31 July; mills have been front-loading purchases ahead of the end of the second duty-free window, which runs to 31 October 2026. The crop estimate now stands at 339 lakh bales." Update the "~60 lakh bales" stat tile and the "337 lakh bales (July)" line to 339 (August).
Also LOW: the post credits the 348 lakh consumption figure to the Confederation of Indian Textile Industry; the August coverage attributes the 348 lakh figure to CAI. Check the attribution.
Confidence: duty dates confirmed by 2+ sources; CAI August numbers confirmed by 2 sources (both secondary).
Also note: the 2026-27 season opens 1 October, so a first 2026-27 CAI estimate may exist; not searched successfully.
