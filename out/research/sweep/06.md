## Findings

### 1. beyond-pump-what-upi-fee-would-cost (MEDIUM) - stale caveat now contradicts the post's own update box
- Claim, near the end under "What this article does not establish": "Whether an MDR will in fact be notified, at what rate, or with which category carve-outs - the 4 August 2026 amendment creates the power, not the fee."
- Current: the fee has been notified. 0.4% on person-to-merchant UPI payments above Rs 2,000, capped at Rs 300 from Rs 75,000, effective 15 October 2026 (three days from today, so still pending as a go-live). Flat Rs 5 for fuel, railways, telecom, insurance, agri-inputs and utility bills above Rs 2,000. Merchants receiving up to Rs 1 lakh a month through UPI QR codes stay at zero. Government says the fee reaches only about 4% of merchant transactions.
  - [SCC Online explainer, 16 Sep 2026](https://www.scconline.com/blog/post/2026/09/16/npci-released-upi-mdr-faqs-explained/)
  - [Business Today on the petrol-pump rate, 15 Sep 2026](https://www.businesstoday.in/personal-finance/story/paying-for-fuel-via-upi-heres-how-much-youll-be-charged-at-petrol-pumps-555740-2026-09-15)
  - [Inc42](https://inc42.com/buzz/npci-notifies-0-4-mdr-on-p2m-upi-transactions-over-%e2%82%b92000/)
  - PIB release PRID 2310586, seen in search results only, not opened.
- Suggested wording: replace the sentence with "Whether the notified schedule (0.4% above Rs 2,000, flat Rs 5 for fuel and utilities, 0.02% for capital-market payments) will change after the 15 October 2026 start, and what NPCI's first monthly collections show, this article does not establish." Add to the top update box: "About 96% of merchant transactions fall outside the fee, per the government."
- Related, for the author to consider: the exhibits model a flat 1% fee on a 3.87% fuel margin (25.8% leverage). The actual fuel rate is a flat Rs 5, so the headline "fuel dealer" leverage figure no longer describes the real regime. The update box says the exhibits model a uniform rate, which is honest, but the "25.8%" tile is still presented unqualified.
- Confidence: confirmed by 2+ sources (secondary press and explainers). The NPCI circular itself was not seen. One source set differs on whether the Rs 1 lakh exemption is monthly-receipt based; check NPCI's own text.
