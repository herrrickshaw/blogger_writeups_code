In early October 2026 MoEFCC's Expert Appraisal Committee recommended environmental and CRZ clearance; the final grant had not been reported as of 12 October 2026." - Related MEDIUM points for the same post, single source (search summary of press reports):

  - June 2026: thermal paper, BOPA film, antioxidants, decor paper and CRGO steel, per [TPM Consultants, June 2026 roundup](https://www.mondaq.com/india/international-trade-investment/1800312/tpm-consultants-june-2026-trade-remedial-actions) and a search summary of the 25 June 2026 Commerce Ministry release.

The expected Aug-Sep 2026 decision has not appeared; this blog could not locate the case on DGTR's portal. Treat the duty upside as unconfirmed." The dyes row is already retracted; add that no DGTR dye case was located.
- Please check the DGTR "Anti-Dumping Investigations in India" list directly (not reachable from here) and the case numbers for TPA and dyes before deciding whether to delete the rows.
