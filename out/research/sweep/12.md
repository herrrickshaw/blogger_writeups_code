Run date: 2026-10-12. Reviewer scope: digest_12.json. Report only; nothing under posts/ or meta/ was changed.

## Method limits (read first)

- The shared WebSearch budget ran out partway through this sweep (search tool reported its 200-call per-turn limit used up). About 14 searches were completed. The bulk-drug follow-ups (PLI progress release of August 2026, parks scheme extension) were NOT run. I did not try to get around the limit.

## Findings

### 1. indias-ev-localisation-deadline-just  (HIGH)

- Claim: "PM E-DRIVE ... was approved by the Cabinet on 11 September 2024 with a ₹10,900 crore outlay running from 1 October 2024 to 31 March 2026." The chart caption and the table intro repeat "Oct 2024 – Mar 2026".
- Current: The Ministry of Heavy Industries extended the scheme by two years, to 31 March 2028 (or until the ₹10,900 crore is exhausted), for electric buses, e-trucks and e-ambulances. The 2W and 3W (L5) incentives were kept to 31 March 2026, and the 3W target was met by December 2025. This extension dates from August 2025, so the post's end date was already out of date when published. It matters here because e-buses and e-trucks are the exact categories the post is about. Sources: [Outlook Business, PM E-DRIVE extended to 2028](https://www.outlookbusiness.com/industry/govt-extends-pm-e-drive-scheme-2028-e-buses-workforce-upskilling); [Inc42](https://inc42.com/buzz/govt-extends-pm-e-drive-scheme-till-2028); [Energetica India](https://www.energetica-india.net/news/govt-extends-pm-e-drive-scheme-by-2-years-till-march-2028-for-electric-buses-trucks-and-ambulances). A [Drishti IAS item dated 1 April 2026](https://www.drishtiias.com/state-pcs-current-affairs/government-extends-pm-e-drive-scheme-till-march-2028/print_manually) also reports a 2028 extension and says the e-2W subsidy was extended to 31 July 2026. I could not confirm the e-2W date elsewhere, so leave it out.
- Suggested wording: "...with a ₹10,900 crore outlay, originally running from 1 October 2024 to 31 March 2026. In August 2025 the Ministry extended the scheme to 31 March 2028 for e-buses, e-trucks and e-ambulances (or until funds are exhausted); e-2W and e-3W incentives were not extended beyond March 2026." Change "Oct 2024 – Mar 2026" in the chart title and caption to "Oct 2024 – Mar 2028 (e-bus, e-truck lines)", or add the extension note beneath it.
- Confidence: confirmed by 2+ sources (several outlets in the results; pages not opened).

### 2. five-years-and-25000-crore-later-china  (MEDIUM, two items)

2a. Scheme sunset wording
- Claim: "Accelerate common infrastructure at all three Bulk Drug Parks ... the scheme's FY2026-27 sunset is one year away", and in the park table "Scheme tenure runs FY2020-21 to FY2026-27 — with one year left on the clock".
- Current: The FY2026-27 end date matches the tenure reported for the Bulk Drug Parks scheme (to March 2027). But as of 12 October 2026 that is about five and a half months away, not one year. Separately, the PLI-Bulk Drugs scheme itself does not sunset in 2026-27. A Rajya Sabha reply of 2 December 2025 put it at FY2029-30, and a March 2026 reply reported by AIR said "till 2028-29", so the two replies conflict. Sources: [Medical Dialogues on the Rajya Sabha reply](https://medicaldialogues.in/news/industry/pharma/pli-schemes-reduce-api-import-dependence-govt-tells-parliament-161149); [All India Radio, 10 Mar 2026](https://www.newsonair.gov.in/govt-has-taken-major-steps-to-reduce-indias-dependence-on-imported-pharmaceutical-ingredients-union-minister-nadda). Whether the parks scheme has since been extended was not checked (search budget).
- Suggested wording: "...the Bulk Drug Parks scheme's tenure ends in March 2027, about six months away (the PLI-Bulk Drugs scheme itself runs longer, to 2028-29 or 2029-30 depending on the parliamentary reply)." Also make sure the sentence says which scheme it means.
- Confidence: single source for the parks tenure and the 2028-29 date; the 2029-30 date comes from a separate reply. Two parliamentary replies disagree with each other. Unverified on any extension.

2b. Newer PLI-Bulk Drugs progress figures
- Claim: latest snapshot "Dec 2025: 48 projects, investment 4,814, cumulative sales 2,720, imports avoided 2,192 (₹ crore)", and "38 projects covering 28 products have actually been commissioned".
- Current: Reports from March 2026 give about ₹5,210.74 crore invested and 39 commissioned projects covering 28 APIs. A Department of Pharmaceuticals press release dated 7 August 2026 on scheme progress is also reported (48 projects approved; ₹87.70 crore incentive disbursed to March 2026). I did not find the newer cumulative import-avoidance figure. Sources: [Plutus IAS summary of the 7 Aug 2026 release](https://plutusias.com/?p=233001); [PLI bulk drugs explainer, 2026](https://plutusias.com/pli-scheme-for-bulk-drugs-boosting-indias-pharma-self-reliance-in-2026/); [AIR, 10 Mar 2026](https://www.newsonair.gov.in/govt-has-taken-major-steps-to-reduce-indias-dependence-on-imported-pharmaceutical-ingredients-union-minister-nadda). These are secondary explainers; the primary release should be pulled from the Department's site before editing.
- Suggested wording: add a fourth row "Mar 2026: 48 projects; investment about ₹5,211 crore (reported); 39 projects commissioned, 28 APIs", and note that the Department of Pharmaceuticals issued a progress release on 7 August 2026. Keep ₹2,192 crore labelled "as of December 2025" until the newer imports-avoided figure is found.
- Confidence: single source (secondary).

Also from this post: the "Strategic API Reserve" proposal is described as unconfirmed. I found no Indian strategic API reserve announced in 2026 (the only hits were the US reserve from the August 2025 executive order). No change needed.

### 3. you-cannot-substitute-crude-oil-or-gold  (MEDIUM)

- Claim: "40 per cent of imports ... cannot be manufactured away"; target is "24 per cent of the import bill, 41 per cent of the imports manufacturing could plausibly touch".
- Current: Coverage of the Business Standard report (16 July 2026) says the government document assessed only 26 per cent of the import basket as realistically amenable to substitution, and that 46 per cent ($332 billion: crude oil, gold, coal and similar) cannot be substituted domestically. The blog's own DGCI&S-based split (fuel plus gems and gold = 40%) is a different cut and is not contradicted, but readers will meet the 26/46 split in the press and the post does not mention it. Coverage also reports a separate Commerce Ministry list of more than 100 products and a task force led by Shaktikanta Das preparing an import-substitution roadmap; the post does not mention either. Sources: [Shoonya summary](https://blog.shoonya.com/india-import-substitution/); [Policy Circle](https://www.policycircle.org/policy/indias-import-substitution-plan/); [ThePrint](https://theprint.in/world/modi-govt-pushes-to-cut-imports-to-shield-india-from-shocks/2987880/); [EY Economy Watch, Aug 2026](https://www.ey.com/en_in/insights/tax/economy-watch/strategizing-import-substitution-in-india).
- Suggested wording: add one sentence in "Sources and caveats": "The government document as reported puts the substitutable share of the import basket at about 26 per cent and the non-substitutable share (crude, gold, coal and similar) at 46 per cent, about $332 billion. This post's 40 per cent uses a narrower two-chapter definition (mineral fuels plus pearls, stones and gold)." Optionally note the separate Commerce Ministry 100-plus product list and the roadmap task force.
- Confidence: single source family (press reports of one document); the $189 billion, 1,272 products and $50 million threshold in the post are confirmed by 2+ sources.

### 4. the-balkan-relish-india-already-has  (MEDIUM)

- Claim: GST rate for vinegar-preserved vegetable relishes is "genuinely ambiguous ... conflicting GST rates (5 per cent cited in one source, 12 per cent in several others)"; the proposal asks for clarity "between the 5% and 12% rates that public sources currently give".
- Current: From my own background knowledge, not from this sweep's searches: the GST Council's 56th meeting (3 September 2025) moved GST to a two-rate structure of 5% and 18% from 22 September 2025, and the 12% slab was abolished, with most 12% goods moved to 5%. If that is right, the 12% figure in the post comes from older sources and the "conflict" is a date artefact, not a classification question. I did not get to search this and have no source link, so verify against the CBIC rate notification for heading 2001 before editing.
- Suggested wording (after verification): "Older sources give 12 per cent; that slab was removed in September 2025 and heading 2001 preparations are now taxed at [5 per cent, to be confirmed against the current CBIC schedule]. Sub-heading classification may still matter." Adjust the proposal to match.
- Confidence: unverified.

### 5. from-fuel-to-fibre-indias-textile  (MEDIUM)

- Claim: PM MITRA table "as of February 2026": Dhar "1,150 of 2,158 acres allotted; 91 companies; ₹24,175 Cr proposed"; Navsari, Kalaburagi and Lucknow at "PPP framework/PPPAC review stage"; Vibrant Polymers "first to break ground, May 2026".
- Current: A Free Press Journal report on a Madhya Pradesh Industrial Development Corporation review says basic infrastructure at Dhar will be complete by June 2027 and units are expected to start production by December 2027 (an earlier report had expected operations in late 2026). It cites 1,130 acres allotted of 1,326 acres industrial land, 38 units and over ₹21,500 crore; this is on a different land basis from the post's 2,158 acres and 91 companies, so I would not swap the numbers without the primary source. A secondary source says the Lucknow and Navsari parks cleared the PPPAC in June 2026 (unverified). Sources: [Free Press Journal, MPIDC review](https://www.freepressjournal.in/amp/indore/mp-news-mpidc-reviews-progress-of-2063-crore-pm-mitra-park-in-dhar); [Apparel Resources](https://apparelresources.com/business-news/manufacturing/mp-pm-mitra-park-enters-execution-phase-first-textile-units/); [Alchempro (Tamil Nadu park)](https://www.alchempro.com/news/textile-news/india-approves-221-25-mn-plan-for-pm-mitra-park-in-tamil-nadu-state-303695-newsdetails.htm).
- Suggested wording: after the Feb 2026 table add: "Update, October 2026: Madhya Pradesh's state development corporation now targets completion of basic infrastructure by June 2027 and first production by December 2027." Hold the PPPAC line until confirmed from a Textiles Ministry source.
- Confidence: single source; the post's Feb 2026 table is labelled as dated, which limits the harm.
- Also in this post: "The Ministry has not yet published an FY2025-26 update to its official 207-HSN technical-textiles trade table" (3 Aug 2026). Not checked; the claim is date-stamped.

### 6. india-polishes-worlds-diamonds-with  (LOW)

I found no report of an extension, early restoration or new licence rules, so the item is still pending. As of 12 October it is four weeks away, not six. - Confidence: confirmed by 2+ sources for the date; no information on what happens next.

### 9. half-of-indias-import-bill-is  (LOW)

- Claim: "Oil sets the floor (~$200 bn/yr...), but it actually fell $15 bn this year."
- Current: This is a FY2025-26 statement and still holds for that year. Monthly data since then shows imports rising again: August 2026 merchandise imports were reported at $70.67 billion, up 14.7% year on year, with energy costs cited as the driver. The "fell this year" framing may mislead for FY2026-27. September 2026 trade data is due around 15 October, so it is not out yet. Source: [Trading Economics, India balance of trade](https://tradingeconomics.com/india/balance-of-trade/news/583828) (search-result summary only).
- Suggested wording: "...but it fell $15 bn in FY2025-26 (April-March); imports have turned up again in FY2026-27 on energy prices."
- Confidence: single source.
