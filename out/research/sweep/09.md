### 1. fourteen-states-reimburse-investors (Haryana row and "three states mid-rewrite" paragraph)
- **Claim as stated:** Haryana's "Make in Haryana" Industrial Policy 2026 "was approved by cabinet on 18 May 2026 ... but its detailed SGST sub-scheme notifications were ... still pending"; table row says "terms below are secondary-sourced, not yet independently verified" and the share is "30-70%".
- **Current:** A Grant Thornton alert dated 12 June 2026 says the Haryana government notified the policy, effective 26 May 2026, replacing HEEP 2020, for five years. It states net SGST reimbursement of 30-70% (depending on area) over 7 / 10 / 12 years for large / mega / ultra-mega units when more than half of intra-state sales are B2C, and 20-60% when less than half are. Capital subsidy is 2.5-20% of eligible capex, and the A/B/C/D blocks are replaced by Core / Intermediate / Sub-Prime / Prime-Focus areas. Sources: [Grant Thornton India alert, 12 Jun 2026](https://www.grantthornton.in/globalassets/1.-member-firms/india/assets/pdfs/alerts/make-in-haryana-industrial-policy-2026.pdf); [Tribune on cabinet approval, May 2026](https://www.tribuneindia.com/news/haryana/haryana-cabinet-clears-10-new-industrial-policies-targets-rs-5-lakh-crore-investment-10-lakh-jobs/); [TaxTMI](https://www.taxtmi.com/news?id=72863).
- **Suggested wording:** "Haryana's HEEP 2020 expired at the end of 2025. The Make in Haryana Industrial Policy 2026, approved by cabinet on 18 May 2026, was notified with effect from 26 May 2026 for five years. Net SGST reimbursement is 30-70% (by area) over 7, 10 or 12 years for large, mega and ultra-mega units where more than half of intra-state sales are B2C, and 20-60% where fewer are (Grant Thornton alert, 12 June 2026)." Drop Haryana from the "mid-rewrite" list (leave Jharkhand and Himachal). The "What would fix this" Haryana proposal is the blog's own opinion and is not flagged, but its premise (that the SGST sub-scheme is un-notified) looks stale.
- **Severity:** HIGH (a pending item has resolved). **Confidence:** confirmed by 2+ sources for the 30-70% range and the replacement; the 26 May effective date and 20-60% tier come from one source (Grant Thornton); the gazette number was not seen.

### 3. india-ended-63-years-of-state-nuclear (SHANTI Act rules, fuel-cycle threshold, liability)
- **Claim as stated:** Fuel-cycle activities are open to private entities "up to a threshold value the government has yet to set"; the supplier-liability fix "is a separate reform still working its way through government". The post is dated 15 September 2026 and does not mention subordinate rules.
- **Current:** The Department of Atomic Energy published draft SHANTI Rules, 2026 on 14 August 2026 with comments due 4 September 2026. The draft provides a single composite licence (construction, ownership, operation, decommissioning), in-principle approval before the site or technology is final, a requirement that foreign designs be approved by their home regulator and already operating, and strict operator liability for nuclear damage without proof of negligence. One outlet headline says the government "notifies" the rules, so final status needs confirming. I found no enrichment threshold figure. Sources: [Vision IAS, 18 Aug 2026](https://visionias.in/current-affairs/news-today/2026-08-18/science-and-technology/draft-shanti-rules-2026-released-by-the-government); [The Week explainer, 16 Aug 2026](https://www.theweek.in/news/biz-tech/2026/08/16/explained-shanti-act-nuclear-energy.html); [Civilsdaily on the draft rules](https://www.civilsdaily.com/news/draft-rules-under-the-shanti-act-open-nuclear-power-to-captive-industrial-use-and-a-composite-licence/); [Outlook Business, "Govt notifies SHANTI Act rules"](https://www.outlookbusiness.com/economy-and-policy/govt-notifies-shanti-act-rules-lt-bhel-mtar-among-likely-beneficiaries).
- **Suggested wording:** Add after the licensing paragraph: "In August 2026 the Department of Atomic Energy published draft SHANTI Rules (comments closed 4 September), which set out a composite licence and operator-liability provisions; [if confirmed: final rules were notified on DATE]." Keep the threshold sentence only if the final rules still omit a figure. Re-test the "supplier-liability fix is a separate track" sentence against the draft rules' liability chapter.
- **Severity:** MEDIUM (newer development the post omits; the "separate liability track" claim may be partly overtaken). **Confidence:** confirmed by 2+ sources that draft rules exist; whether final rules are notified is unverified.
