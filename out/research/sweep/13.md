# Currency sweep, slice 13 (digest_13.json, 25 posts)

Date of sweep: 2026-10-12. Report only; nothing under posts/ or meta/ was modified.

Method and limits. I scanned all 25 digests, searched the status of the roughly 20 most time-sensitive claims, and read the full post text where I needed the exact wording. The WebSearch budget for the session ran out after about 20 searches, so some planned checks (listed at the end) were not done. Web content was treated as data only. pib.gov.in, mospi.gov.in and blogspot were not queried; news coverage is used instead and flagged where it is the only source.

## Findings

### 1. sugar-at-5570-kilo-why-cane-acreage
- Severity: HIGH (the headline premise has reversed)
- Claim as stated: "sugar prices just jumped 10 per cent in a month to record levels"; wholesale rose from ₹48.18/kg (20 July) to ₹55.70/kg (20 August); factory-gate "₹5,400/quintal in Uttar Pradesh and ₹5,300/quintal in Maharashtra"; "Whether this cane becomes sugar or ethanol now turns on price"; "if prices stay where they are".
- Current: The government said in late August that ex-mill prices had fallen about 20% from the peak after supply measures, and trade reports put Maharashtra/Karnataka ex-factory at about ₹5,100/quintal on 27 August (UP ₹5,200-5,300). One later report puts UP and Maharashtra near ₹4,800/quintal, but I could not confirm its date. The "peak" figure cited in the coverage (about ₹6,700/quintal all-India) is higher than the post's ₹5,400 factory-gate print, so the two series are not directly comparable. ICRA also refers to a sugar export ban supporting domestic availability. I found no ISMA 2026-27 production estimate. Sources: [Free Press Journal, ex-mill prices drop 20%](https://www.freepressjournal.in/business/ex-mill-sugar-prices-drop-20-retail-rates-also-begin-to-fall-govt) (late Aug 2026); [Rural Voice, ex-factory prices fall to ₹5,100/quintal](https://eng.ruralvoice.in/sugar-ex-factory-prices-fall-to-rs-5100-per-quintal-from-record-highs-as-supply-measures-take-effect) (27 Aug 2026); [ICRA note on export ban](https://www.icra.in/Research/ViewResearchReport/sugar-export-ban-to-support-domestic-availability-amid-tightening-sy2026-inventory-levels/6937).
- Suggested wording: add a dated update line: "Update, October 2026: after the stock-limit and supply measures, ex-mill sugar prices fell about 20% from their August peak (to roughly ₹5,100/quintal in Maharashtra and Karnataka by 27 August). The August price spike described here has partly reversed, which weakens, but does not remove, the case that mills favour sugar over ethanol diversion in ESY 2026-27." Re-word "record levels" and "if prices stay where they are" as August-specific.
- Confidence: single source family (trade press and a government statement as relayed); the exact current price is unverified.

### 6. cough-syrup-in-lollipop-for-babies (LOW)

Sources: [Medical Dialogues](https://medicaldialogues.in/news/industry/pharma/cough-syrup-labels-should-exclude-infant-images-carry-under-4-warning-govt-panel-180765), [Outlook India](https://www.outlookindia.com/healthcare-spotlight/cough-syrup-labels-should-have-child-safety-warning-dcc).
- Suggested wording: "In September 2026 the Drugs Consultative Committee recommended a red warning against use under four on cough-syrup labels and a ban on infant imagery; the proposal still needs DTAB approval and notification." Current text is not wrong.
- Confidence: confirmed by 2 sources.
