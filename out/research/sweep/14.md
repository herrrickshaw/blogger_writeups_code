### 3. rooftop-solar-is-working-household
- Claim: "by May 2026 the figure was above 12 GW across roughly 33 lakh installations"; "May 2026 was the strongest month on record at 3.16 lakh installations"; "time taken to add one lakh households has fallen from 118 days to under eight". - Current: Early-August 2026 reporting citing MNRE puts the scheme above 50.06 lakh households benefiting and 14.8 GW cumulative rooftop capacity. - Sources: [Deccan Herald, PM Surya Ghar crosses 50 lakh](https://www.deccanherald.com/india/pm-surya-ghar-scheme-crosses-50-lakh-household-milestone-in-rooftop-solar-drive-4098827), [Energetica India, 4 Aug 2026 (as cited in search summary)](https://www.energetica-india.net/keyword/pm-surya-ghar-target), [SolarQuarter, 75 lakh by Dec 2026 target, 5 Jun 2026](https://solarquarter.com/2026/06/05/pm-surya-ghar-scheme-targets-75-lakh-rooftop-solar-installations-by-december-2026-as-india-accelerates-residential-solar-adoption/).
- Suggested wording: "...by early August 2026 the scheme had passed 50 lakh households and about 14.8 GW of rooftop capacity (MNRE). July 2026 was the strongest month so far at 5.06 lakh installations, and a lakh households are now being added roughly every six days." Update the stat strip and the "roughly 8% of all solar capacity" ratio (14.8 GW against the post's 150.26 GW fleet is about 10%; recheck the denominator).
