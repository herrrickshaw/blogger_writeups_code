### 2. thermal-parity-is-dead-what-85-oil-and (MEDIUM)

- Claim in post: "PPAC's own August 2026 notification puts the actual domestic natural gas price at $7.86/MMBtu"; deepwater, ultra-deepwater and HPHT gas has "its own, separate ceiling, published by PPAC as $8.90/MMBtu for the period 1 April to 30 September 2026"; and "this article could not locate PPAC's precise nomination-field ceiling figure".
- Current: a new PPAC notification applies from 1 October 2026 to 31 March 2027. The deepwater/ultra-deepwater/HPHT ceiling rises from $8.90 to $9.89/MMBtu. Gas from ONGC and Oil India legacy nomination fields stays capped at $7.00/MMBtu (the formula figure for October is reported at $11.22, so the cap binds). New-well gas from those blocks gets a 10% premium on the capped price, about $7.70. This answers the post's open question: the nomination-field ceiling is $7. Sources: [Business Standard, 4 Oct 2026](https://www.business-standard.com/amp/economy/news/govt-raises-deepwater-gas-price-ceiling-to-9-89-per-mmbtu-apm-cap-at-7-126100400148_1.html), [Hans India](https://www.thehansindia.com/business/govt-raises-ceiling-price-of-gas-from-deepwater-fields-to-989mmbtu-1128713), [Daijiworld](https://daijiworld.com/news/newsDisplay?newsID=1328560), [Indiaipo](https://www.indiaipo.in/news/detail/govt-raises-deepwater-gas-price-ceiling-to-989-per-mmbtu-apm-gas-cap-at-7).
- Suggested replacement (add an update line after the paragraph): "Update, 12 October 2026: PPAC's notification for 1 October 2026 to 31 March 2027 raised the deepwater, ultra-deepwater and HPHT ceiling from $8.90 to $9.89/MMBtu and kept the cap on ONGC and Oil India legacy-field gas at $7.00/MMBtu, even though the 10%-of-crude formula output for October was reported at $11.22. The $7 cap is therefore the binding nomination-field ceiling. The $85 crude and $7.86 figures in the tables are as of 4 and 6 August 2026."
- Open point for the author: the press reports describe the legacy cap as $7.00 (about $7.70 for new wells), whereas the post reads $7.86 as the August price. I could not reconcile $7.86 with these figures without the PPAC notice; worth re-reading the August notice.
- Confidence: confirmed by 2+ press reports of the same notification; the PPAC notice itself was not seen.
