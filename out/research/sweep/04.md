### 1. indian-railways-98-to-earn-100-business
- **Claim in post:** "the Western Dedicated Freight Corridor (1,506 km, Dadri to Mumbai's port cluster) was reported at 93% complete as of late 2025, per PSA India's account of the project."
- **Current:** This pending item has resolved, or is close to it. Wikipedia's article on the corridor says DFCCIL announced completion on 31 March 2026, once the JNPT to New Saphale (Vaitarna) section came into operation ([Wikipedia: Western Dedicated Freight Corridor](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Western_Dedicated_Freight_Corridor), accessed via search summary 12 Oct 2026). India Sea Trade News reported a trial run on the final 102 km JNPT to New Saphale stretch, "marking the full completion of the corridor" ([India Sea Trade News, 4 June 2026](https://indiaseatradenews.com/western-dedicated-freight-corridor-fully-completed-trial-run-on-jnpt-new-saphale-section-signals-operational-readiness/)). Earlier 2026 reports (Maritime Gateway) had the section slipping repeatedly, with signalling and overhead electrification pending.
- **Caveat:** The sources disagree on whether the final section is in full commercial service. I found no DFCCIL release confirming a revenue-service date.
- **Suggested wording:** "The Eastern Dedicated Freight Corridor (1,337 km, Ludhiana to Dankuni) is operational, and the Western corridor (1,506 km, Dadri to JNPT) was reported complete in 2026, with a trial run on the final JNPT to New Saphale section in June 2026. Check DFCCIL for the date of full commercial operation."
- **Severity:** HIGH. **Confidence:** single source (two secondary reports that partly conflict). Verify against DFCCIL before editing.

- **Current:** Full-year FY2025-26 data now exist.
  - Services exports were about USD 421 billion (Taxsutra, 24 Jul 2026, link in item 2).
  - Merchandise exports were USD 441.78 billion, per the post's own PIB 15 April 2026 citation in a sibling post.

- **Suggested wording:** "India's goods and services exports reached about US$860 billion in FY2025-26 (services about US$421 billion, up roughly 9%; merchandise US$442 billion, up under 1%), against US$825.3 billion in FY25."
- **Severity:** MEDIUM. **Confidence:** single source for the services total (Taxsutra headline, plus NITI figures from search summaries). The combined total is derived.

### 5. indias-e-commerce-inventory-ban-just
- **Claim in post:** "The 19th [amendment], quietly notified on 2 September 2026, opens exactly one exception ... the first time this specific prohibition has moved at all."
- **Current:** The policy change was announced earlier. DPIIT issued Press Note 3 (2026 Series) on 23 July 2026, inserting Para 5.2.15.2.5 to allow inventory-based e-commerce exclusively for exports of India-made goods. The FEMA NDI (Fourth Amendment) Rules, 2026 (S.O. 4870(E)) then made it legally operative, effective on Gazette publication on 3 September 2026 ([Free Press Journal](https://www.freepressjournal.in/amp/business/centre-allows-fdi-in-inventory-based-e-commerce-model-exclusively-for-export-of-indian-made-goods); [Daily Pioneer](https://www.dailypioneer.com/news/centre-clears-fdi-to-boost-export-based-ecommerce); Fox Mandal and Nishith Desai write-ups). The post does not mention the Press Note.
- **Suggested wording:** "DPIIT announced the carve-out in Press Note 3 (2026 Series) on 23 July 2026. The 2 September FEMA notification (S.O. 4870(E), in the Gazette from 3 September) made it legally operative." Drop "quietly" or qualify it.
- **Severity:** MEDIUM. **Confidence:** single source family (law-firm and news summaries). The two dates (2 vs 3 September) should be checked against the Gazette text.

### 6. a-million-wine-market-behind-150-wall
- **Claim in post:** "headed down to 20-30% under the India-EU trade deal signed in January 2026" (summary). The body says "agreed", "political agreement" and "not yet signed".
- **Current:** The deal was concluded, not signed, on 27 January 2026. On 11 September 2026 the Commission formally asked the Council to authorise signature and conclusion. I found no Council decision as of the latest sources (coverage dated up to 12 September 2026). The Commission president said "We will sign ... by the end of the year", and entry into force is targeted for 2027 ([Cyril Amarchand Mangaldas, Sep 2026](https://corporate.cyrilamarchandblogs.com/2026/09/the-india-eu-free-trade-agreement-a-landmark-deal-moves-toward-finalisation/); [Times of Oman](https://timesofoman.com/article/176797-european-commission-presents-landmark-india-fta-to-council-for-signature); [Swarajya](https://swarajyamag.com/economy/india-eu-free-trade-agreement-moves-closer-to-signing-as-european-commission-sends-pact-to-eu-council-for-approval); [EUbusiness](https://www.eubusiness.com/trade/eu-india-free-trade-agreement-set-for-2027-entry-into-force/)). The post's 26 September update is consistent with this. The Council-signature step was not re-checked for October.
- **Suggested wording:** Replace "signed in January 2026" with "concluded on 27 January 2026 (signature still pending)". Also replace "a signed political agreement awaiting ratification" with "a concluded agreement awaiting signature and ratification".
- **Severity:** LOW (wording). **Confidence:** confirmed by 2+ sources on the status as of mid-September. October is unverified.

### 7. cocoas-blockchain-reckoning-one
- **Claim in post:** the EUDR "has already been delayed twice ... postponed again in December 2025 to 30 December 2026 for large and medium operators (small and micro operators get until 30 June 2027)", plus "a real deadline is four months out".

- **Suggested wording:** Add one sentence: "The Commission's May 2026 review confirmed no further postponement." As of 12 October 2026 the deadline is under 11 weeks away.
- **Severity:** LOW. **Confidence:** confirmed by 2+ sources (mostly secondary).
