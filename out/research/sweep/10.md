### 1. indias-labour-codes-took-six-years-to
- Severity: MEDIUM (a newer official count exists, and a pending deadline is now on record)
- Confidence: single source (a Business Standard report, with the same details repeated in search-result summaries; the article itself could not be opened)
- Claim in post: "~11 of 28 states with final rules notified, per the most recent single-date tracker found" (9 July 2026 snapshot). The post also says it "found no single, continuously updated government tracker".
- Current: At a PHDCCI/Grant Thornton seminar, the Labour Secretary said ten states have published rules, the remaining states and UTs have put out drafts, and the government expects all states and UTs to publish by 31 October 2026. See [Business Standard, 24 Sep 2026](https://www.business-standard.com/india-news/all-states-uts-may-notify-labour-code-rules-by-oct-31-labour-secretary-126092401119_1.html). Private trackers still disagree: a tracker updated 3 Oct 2026 shows 6 of 37 states/UTs with all four codes' rules notified, 4 with some, and 27 with none. See [Labour Code Rules: Central & State-wise Status 2026](https://employmentlaw.lkslaw.com/rules).
- Note: the official count (10) is lower than the post's 11 because the two use different definitions of "notified". The Secretary's statement is the first official count the post could not find, and it comes with a dated target.
- Suggested wording: "On 24 September 2026 the Labour Secretary said ten states had published their rules and the rest had issued drafts, with the government expecting all states and UTs to finish by 31 October 2026. Private trackers give different counts depending on whether a state has notified rules under all four codes or only some." Revisit after 31 October. Also change the "What would fix this" sentence about "no official count" only if the blog treats the Secretary's remark as an official count.
