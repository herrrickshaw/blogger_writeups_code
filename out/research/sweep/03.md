### 1. where-worlds-oil-sits-when-it-isnt

- Severity: MEDIUM
- Claim in post: the 26 Sep update says Iran presented a written road map via Qatari mediation proposing a ceasefire of up to 60 days and a phased Hormuz reopening "within seven days", and that "both sides described the contact positively, but neither confirmed a breakthrough".
- Current: in the 4 to 7 October coverage the tone has reversed. Iran's lead negotiator says the Strait stays closed until Washington accepts the seven-day plan, which would require lifting the US naval blockade, waiving oil sanctions and a ceasefire that includes Lebanon. President Trump rejected the proposal. A later US counter-proposal and new US demands (7 Oct) leave the sequencing dispute open. An Iran-Oman temporary shipping corridor has been reported but Iran says it cannot start until the blockade is lifted. The US blockade has been in place since 13 April 2026. The Strait is still closed to commercial shipping, so the post's core "unresolved" claim stands.
- Sources: [Al Jazeera, 4 Oct 2026](https://www.aljazeera.com/news/2026/10/4/iran-says-strait-of-hormuz-to-remain-closed-until-us-meets-conditions); [Al Jazeera, 7 Oct 2026](https://www.aljazeera.com/news/2026/10/7/us-sets-new-demands-for-iran-deal-what-are-they); [CBS live updates](https://www.cbsnews.com/live-updates/iran-war-us-strait-of-hormuz-sanctions/); [Yahoo News](https://www.yahoo.com/news/politics/articles/iran-suggested-deal-reopen-strait-133854959.html).
- Suggested wording: "Update, 12 October 2026: the late-September contacts have not produced a deal. Iran says the Strait stays closed until the US lifts its naval blockade and oil sanctions; the US has rejected that sequence and tabled further demands (reports of 4 to 7 October)."
- Also unverified: "Refill activity was slated to begin in November 2026 ... had not yet begun" (SPR) was not re-checked. The late-September Brent and WTI figures could not be refreshed because searches for October prices returned no dated October quotes. Leave as dated.
- Confidence: confirmed by 2+ sources (headline-level; page text not read).

### 4. india-sells-world-53-billion-of-fuel

- Severity: MEDIUM (newer development relevant to the topic)
- Claim in post: frames India's refined-fuel exports (FY2025-26 $52.97 bn, down 15.3%) and policy as of 8 Aug 2026. A text search of the post finds no mention of export duties.
