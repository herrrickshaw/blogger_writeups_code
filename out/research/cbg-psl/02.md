## 3. MSME classification (relevant to CBG developers and to the GOBARdhan credit guarantee)

Notification S.O. 1364(E) of 21 Mar 2025, effective 1 Apr 2025 (replaces S.O. 2119(E) of 26 Jun 2020). Composite test: investment AND turnover; cross either ceiling -> next category.

| Class | Investment (plant and machinery / equipment) | Turnover |
|---|---|---|
| Micro | up to Rs 2.5 crore | up to Rs 10 crore |
| Small | up to Rs 25 crore | up to Rs 100 crore |
| Medium | up to Rs 125 crore | up to Rs 500 crore |

Sources: https://www.taxmann.com/post/blog/revised-msme-classification ; Deccan Herald/PTI https://www.deccanherald.com/amp/story/business%2Fgovt-notifies-revisions-to-investment-turnover-criteria-for-msmes-to-take-effect-from-april-1-3459255 . Confidence 2+ (search summaries). Note: a CBG plant is mostly a manufacturing/energy unit; whether it is registered as an MSME (Udyam) depends on the developer. Not sourced. PSL treatment of MSME loans (manufacturing and services, no per-borrower cap): not retrieved.

## 4. PSLCs

- Four certificate types: agriculture, small and marginal farmers (SF/MF), micro enterprises, general. Traded on RBI's e-Kuber (CBS) portal. All PSLCs expire 31 March. (RBI FAQ, https://www.rbi.org.in/commonman/Upload/English/FAQs/PDFs/FAQPSL08052025.pdf ; annex https://www.website.rbi.org.in/documents/d/rbi/nt196ad19012026_an - search summaries; confidence single/2+.)
- March 2025 RBI circular extended PSLCs to the weaker-sections sub-target (taxmann summary).
- **Premium / price of any PSLC type (agri, general, RE): NOT FOUND.** Two searches returned nothing relevant (one returned unrelated US results). There is no renewable-energy-specific PSLC type in the four categories listed. So the question "does a renewable-energy PSLC trade at a premium" has the answer: there is no RE PSLC; agri PSLC premium not found.
- 2018 Business Standard item: 18% GST on PSLC fee (dated, unverified for 2026).

## 6. Other subsidy / subvention touching biogas loans

- **Agriculture Infrastructure Fund (AIF):** 3% p.a. interest subvention on loans up to Rs 2 crore principal (up to 7 years); CGTMSE cover on loans up to Rs 2 crore with fee paid by government; CBG plants listed as eligible (Indian Bank product page https://indianbank.bank.in/en/ind-krishi-infra-fund-agriculture-infra-fund-1 ; vikaspedia https://en.vikaspedia.in/schemesall/schemes-for-farmers/agriculture-infrastructure-fund). Scheme end date disputed: FY2032-33 (Drishti IAS, extended to 13 years) vs FY2032 (Vikaspedia) vs FY2029 (older). Confidence: terms 2+, CBG eligibility single (bank page, not scheme guideline), end date unverified.
- **Central Financial Assistance (MNRE) for CBG:** discontinued since April 2021 per a 2022 parliamentary panel (Business Standard Dec 2022 summary). Replaced by GOBARdhan capital assistance from Sep 2026 (if guidelines figures hold). Single.
- No interest subvention specific to CBG loans (other than AIF) found.

## Gaps and cautions
- Nothing read from the RBI page itself; the Directions' exact text on CBG (para/annex number), the renewable-energy line (whether biogas generation is named) and the start-up limit remain second-hand. Cap figures (Rs 100 crore agri infra; Rs 35 crore RE; Rs 10 lakh household) came via search summaries of RBI FAQ and press wires.
- 7 Aug and 11 Sep 2026 amendments to the PSL Directions: contents unknown. The RBI FAQ was updated 11 Sep 2026, so something changed; check before saying "no 2026 change".
- PSL sub-target percentages, PSLC premiums, challenge-fund size, guarantee-fund set-up and guarantor, 80% pipeline claim, NABARD/SIDBI terms, sector-wide bank credit: not found.
- Blog claims to correct or soften: launch date (1 Oct vs 1 Sep effective / 15 Sep guidelines); Rs 2 crore/TPD (guidelines say Rs 1.25 crore/TPD, cap Rs 30 crore); 80% pipeline cost (unsupported); per-kg price equivalent (Rs 98 vs Rs 105).
- IREDA rates came from one summary of the June 2026 PDF; the SBI "repo + 2.65%" is single-source and its date uncertain.
- Cap cliff: the Rs 100 crore agri-infra limit is system-wide aggregate exposure per borrower, and exceeding it removes PSL status for the whole exposure per RBI FAQ summary. Relevant to large multi-plant developers.
