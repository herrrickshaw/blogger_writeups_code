# CCUS pipeline length check, 12 October 2026

Question: does CEEW's CO2 pipeline study use 32,000 km (sweep suggestion) or 18,000 km (current post)?

Answer: both. Search summaries of CEEW's report ("CO2 Pipeline Network for Carbon Capture and Storage in India: Evaluating a Parallel Network Along Existing Pipelines", Chordia and Mallya, 2026) give ~32,000 km of existing (or existing or planned) gas pipeline right-of-way as the headline base and ~18,000 km of existing NG trunk lines for the design linking 765 sources. The sweep's proposed replacement of 18,000 with 32,000 would have been wrong.

PNGRB (data bank table 31 Mar 2026, via search summary): authorised 34,803 km = 33,027 common carrier + 997 tie-in + 779 dedicated; operational 27,905. June 2025 (press): 34,233 authorised, 25,429 operational, 32,661 common carrier.
PPAC via press: crude 10,445 km, 153.1 MMTPA at 1 Apr 2025 (IOCL 5,324 km); product pipelines 13,652 authorised / 9,301 operational (Jun 2025) vs 24,130 (1 Sep 2025), definitions differ.
MoPNG draft CCUS roadmap ~25,000 km "oil and gas": close to PNGRB's gas-only operational figure, so scope doubtful; PDF not opened.

Not verified: ceew.in, pngrb.gov.in, carbon-pulse.com, mopng.gov.in all returned DNS errors from this environment; nothing was opened as a primary document. Open the CEEW PDF (ceew.in/node/4960) and PNGRB 20260331_NGPL.pdf on the Mac before relying on the numbers.
