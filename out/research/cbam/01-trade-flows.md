## B. Iron and steel — volumes and product groups

### Calendar-year volumes (EU-import side)
- EU imported 29.7 Mt steel in CY2025, of which 2.4 Mt (~8%) from India. Crisil Intelligence, "EU ripples for steel" (SectorVector, 30 Jul 2026), citing JPC/Ministry of Steel/EUROFER, https://intelligence.crisil.com/content/dam/crisil-intelligence/what-we-think/reports/2026/07/eu-ripples-for-steel.pdf [primary-via-summary]
- ~40% of India's rolled-steel exports went to the EU in CY2025; India exports only 3–5% of its rolled-steel output. Same Crisil report. [primary-via-summary]
- EU flat-steel imports from India: 3.18 Mt in 2024 (+15.7% y/y), of which hot-rolled flat 1.6 Mt (+14.7%), coated 1.01 Mt (+16.2%). GMK Center infographic (12 Mar 2025), https://gmk.center/en/infographic/eu-increased-imports-of-flat-products-by-7-5-y-y-in-2024/ [secondary]
- EU flat-steel imports from India: 2.26 Mt in 2025 (−29% y/y, lowest since 2021); hot-rolled flat 789.6 kt (−50%+); coated 832.3 kt (−18%). Total EU flat imports from third countries 23.06 Mt (+6.4%). GMK Center (3 Mar 2026), https://gmk.center/en/infographic/the-eu-increased-imports-of-flat-rolled-products-by-6-4-y-y-in-2025/ [secondary]
- India rolled steel to EU Jan–Aug 2025 = 1.47 Mt, −47.7% vs 2024 (as summarised; EU-side data). GMK Center, same cluster [secondary]
- India top source of EU finished-steel imports Jan–Apr 2024, 15.8% share (Türkiye 12.6%, Vietnam 10.3%); imports from India +62% y/y. EUROFER via Eurometal, https://eurometal.net/eurofer-flats-drive-eu-import-growth-exports-rise/ [press]
- India among the key suppliers making up 75% of EU flat imports in 2024 (S. Korea, India, Türkiye, Taiwan, Vietnam, China, Ukraine; list is not a strict ranking). GMK Center (Mar 2025) [secondary]
- 2024 value rankings (GMK, Eurostat-type data): one steel category puts India first at EUR 3.9 bn (S. Korea 3.6, China 3.5, UK 3.2). A broader "iron and steel" category has China 12.5 bn (37.3%), Türkiye 4.6, UK 2.9, US 2.0, India 1.7. GMK Center, https://gmk.center/en/news/the-eu-exported-steel-products-worth-e77-8-billion-in-2024/ (14 Mar 2025) [secondary] — the categories are not identified; see Conflicts.

### CBAM-scope product split (2025)
- India CBAM-covered iron & steel exports to EU ≈ 4 Mt in 2025: flat ≈ 2.6 Mt; long ≈ 0.83 Mt; "other I&S" 0.57 Mt = pig iron 0.12 Mt + ferro-alloys 0.17 Mt + ingots/primary forms 0.28 Mt. Sandbag, "CBAM impact on India" (4 Aug 2026), https://sandbag.be/wp-content/uploads/04.08.2026-Sandbag-CBAM-impact-on-India-updated.pdf; Argus coverage https://www.argusmedia.com/en/news-and-insights/latest-market-news/2860894-indian-steel-s-cbam-hit-softer-than-expected-sandbag [primary-via-summary]
- The search summaries for Sandbag give no separate tonnage for tubes/pipes (7304–7306), semis (7207) or articles (7318/7326). The ≈4 Mt total less the three groups (≈4.0 Mt) leaves little room for them. Derived, not stated.
- Sandbag: the 2.6 Mt flat could in principle be supplied from gas-DRI routes; ArcelorMittal Nippon Steel Hazira (≈8.6 Mt/yr finished capacity) is the likely low-emission supplier. Same source [primary-via-summary]

### Ferro-alloys (CN 7202) — CBAM status
- CBAM Annex I (Reg. (EU) 2023/956) covers all of CN 72 except: ferro-silicon (7202 2x), ferro-silico-manganese (7202 30), ferro-silico-chromium (7202 50), Fe-Mo, Fe-W, Fe-Ti, Fe-V, Fe-Nb, ferro-phosphorus, ferro-silico-magnesium, other 7202 99, and scrap (7204). Ferro-manganese (7202 1x), ferro-chromium (7202 4x) and ferro-nickel (7202 60) are therefore covered, as are pig iron (7201) and DRI (7203). Regulation text from prior knowledge, not re-retrieved this session [primary — verify against EUR-Lex]
- Argus: CBAM covers ferro-manganese and ferro-chrome; from Jan 2026 certificate costs add ≈5–10% to landed prices at default intensities. https://www.argusmedia.com/news-and-insights/latest-market-news/2769945-viewpoint-eu-curbs-darken-india-s-mn-alloy-outlook (late 2025) [press]
- Separate EU ferro-alloy safeguard (FeMn, FeSi, SiMn, FeSiMg): India TRQ ≈ 197,030 t for the first 12-month period; Europe ≈ 40% of India's ferro-alloy exports; combined measures could halve India's shipments to Europe. Argus / Mysteel, https://www.mysteel.net/news/5107131-how-will-eu-safeguard-measures-reshape-indias-ferro-alloys-exports (late 2025) [press]
- Practical point: India's large SiMn trade to the EU is outside CBAM but inside the ferro-alloy safeguard. Its FeMn/FeCr trade is inside CBAM. Derived from the two items above.

## F. EU steel safeguard → 2026 "steel measure"; India's allocation; stacking with CBAM

- Commission proposal 7 Oct 2025: tariff-free quotas cut ≈47% vs 2024 to ≈18.3 Mt/yr; out-of-quota duty 50% (from 25%); melt-and-pour origin rule. Reuters via Yahoo Finance, https://finance.yahoo.com/news/eu-halve-steel-import-quotas-153611116.html [press]; Akin Gump alert, https://www.akingump.com/en/insights/alerts/eu-proposes-higher-tariffs-and-lower-quotas-on-steel-imports-and-plans-to-renegotiate-its-wto-tariff-commitments [secondary]
- Provisional Parliament–Council deal Apr 2026 (keeps key elements; 30 product categories). Commission statement reposted, https://www.cde.ual.es/en/la-comision-celebra-el-acuerdo-politico-sobre-la-nueva-medida-de-la-ue-en-materia-de-acero/ (14 Apr 2026) [primary-via-summary]. EP plenary approved 19 May 2026, 606–16–39. S&P Global / Hellenic Shipping News, https://www.spglobal.com/energy/en/news-research/latest-news/metals/051926-eu-lawmakers-approve-tighter-steel-import-curbs-to-counter-global-overcapacity [press]
- Adopted as Regulation (EU) 2026/1384. It replaces the safeguard (Impl. Reg. 2019/159, expired 30 Jun 2026) and applies from 1 Jul 2026. ≈18.35 Mt across 26 categories, based on 2022–24 import shares. Quotas split into an MFN pool (all exporters) and an FTA pool. Melt-and-pour evidence is required from 1 Oct 2026 and may feed quota allocation from 1 Oct 2027. Reed Smith, https://www.reedsmith.com/our-insights/blogs/trade-compliance-resource-hub/5117/eu-steel-regulation-published-key-features-application-dates-and-what-to-do-next/ [secondary]. Status as of Oct 2026: in force (the "26" vs "30" category counts differ between summaries).
- Commission published country allocations on 30 Jun 2026 for 1 Jul–31 Dec 2026. India's allocation lower for HRC. BigMint, https://www.bigmint.co/insights/detail/eu-releases-country-specific-steel-quotas-lower-hrc-allocation-to-weigh-on-indian-exports-765072 [press]
- India country-specific quotas (annual) per EU June allocation: HRC 597,274 t; CRC 269,974 t; metallic-coated (cat. 4.A) 233,183 t. As summarised from Business Standard/BigMint cluster, https://www.business-standard.com/india-news/eu-steel-quota-opens-2-8-million-tonnes-export-window-for-india-126091701446_1.html [press]
- Crisil: India's share of volume-based quotas falls 41%. New India-specific product quotas (notably heavy plate) limit the overall cut to ~30%. Total Indian steel exports seen −25–30% in FY2026-27; India may lose net-exporter status. Crisil (URL in B) [primary-via-summary]
- Crisil/GMK framing: India's country quota cut from 2.79 Mt to 1.94 Mt (−30%) vs 2.4 Mt shipped in 2025. GMK Center, https://gmk.center/en/news/eu-steel-quotas-could-reduce-indian-exports-by-25-30-crisil/ (31 Jul 2026) [secondary]
- India–EU FTA (concluded 27 Jan 2026; not yet signed or in force as of Sep 2026). Steel TRQ 1,641,469–1,641,470 t/yr = MFN 946,616 t + FTA preferential 694,853 t. Largest line HRC 509,605 t. About 68.4% of 2025 shipments (2.4 Mt). SteelOrbis, https://www.steelorbis.com/steel-news/latest-news/india-secures-larger-eu-steel-quota-under-fta-but-cbam-burden-remains-1477418.htm; GMK, https://gmk.center/en/news/india-will-be-able-to-export-1-64-million-tonnes-of-steel-per-year-to-the-eu-under-the-fta/ (15 Sep 2026); Angel One, https://www.angelone.in/news/economy/india-eu-fta-sets-1-64-million-tonne-steel-quota-including-6-95-lakh-tonnes-under-fta [press]
- Commerce ministry "conservative estimate": up to 2.8 Mt/yr to EU = 1.9 Mt country-specific (incl. ≈950 kt FTA partner quota) + ≈0.9 Mt from residual/"FTA-others" pools. Business Standard (17 Sep 2026, URL above); KNN, https://knnindia.co.in/news/newsdetails/sectors/india-secures-19-mt-eu-steel-quota-another-09-mt-expected-through-residual-quotas-govt [press]
- EEPC India: new quotas could cut India's iron & steel exports to EU by ~40%, ≈USD 1.36 bn/yr. KNN, https://knnindia.co.in/news/newsdetails/sectors/exportimports/new-eu-steel-quotas-may-cut-indias-exports-by-40-eepc-india [press]
- Under the old safeguard, Indian mills had exhausted their Q1 CY2026 quota. BigMint (Jan–Feb 2026) [press]

## G. 2026 behaviour after the definitive period began (1 Jan 2026)

- India→EU steel Jan–Jun 2026 = 1.01 Mt vs 1.26 Mt Jan–Jun 2025 (−19.8%). Total Indian steel exports +27% to 4.63 Mt; Vietnam, UK and UAE absorbed the diverted volume. BigMint via GMK, https://gmk.center/en/news/india-increased-steel-exports-by-27-y-y-in-1h2026/ (29 Jul 2026) [secondary]
- Outlook in the same report: finished-steel exports to Europe stay under pressure, while semi-finished (slab) supplies to Europe are likely to rise. [secondary]
- H1 FY2026-27 (Apr–Sep 2026): total exports incl. semis and stainless +38% to 5.53 Mt; imports −1.5% to 4.84 Mt; exports to EU −26% "amid CBAM, tighter new steel import regime" (headline only). BigMint via Mysteel, https://www.mysteel.net/news/5144448-india-turns-net-steel-exporter-in-h1fy27-as-shipments-surge-to-5-year-highs (15 Oct 2026) [press]
- Pricing, Jan–Feb 2026: BigMint India HRC export index to EU dipped in mid-January, then rose. It stood at USD 550/t FOB on 3 Feb, with offers at USD 600/t CFR Antwerp. The rise came from supply tightness (CBAM + safeguard), not demand. EU buyers resisted at first, then accepted. Mills pushed prices up on CBAM's start. BigMint, https://www.bigmint.co/insights/detail/india-bigmint-s-hrc-export-index-for-eu-rises-w-o-w-driven-by-domestic-price-gains-720211 (4 Feb 2026) [press]. This is the opposite of a discount: no report of Indian mills offering CBAM discounts was found.
- GTRI expectation (not observed data): exporters may have to accept 15–22% price cuts. Buyers are adding CBAM-deduction clauses. GTRI via Maritime Gateway, https://www.maritimegateway.com/eu-carbon-tax-from-2026-set-to-dent-indias-steel-aluminium-exports-gtri [secondary]
- Argus: Indian BF-route HRC default > 4 tCO2/t, implying > EUR 200/t. Argus calculation from the Dec 2025 draft defaults: EUR 269.78/t for BF HRC. https://www.argusmedia.com/en/news-and-insights/latest-market-news/2766194-viewpoint-risks-mount-for-indian-steel-exports-in-2026 [press]
- Cold-rolled flat anti-dumping: definitive duties on India, Japan, Taiwan, Türkiye, Vietnam (Aug 2026). Range 5.6–28.0%; India undercutting margin 11.0%. Eurometal, https://eurometal.net/2026/08/07/ [press]. A third layer on top of quota + CBAM for CRC.

## Conflicts

1. India→EU steel volume, 2025: 2.4 Mt (Crisil, "steel", CY2025) vs 2.26 Mt (GMK, flat only, EU-side) vs ≈4 Mt (Sandbag, full CBAM scope incl. long, pig iron, ferro-alloys, ingots) vs 3.8 Mt (Procurement Resource, FY2025-26). These are mostly scope/period differences. Sandbag's long products alone (0.83 Mt) explain much of the 2.4 vs 4 gap.
2. Direction of 2025 flows: EU-side GMK data show India flat −29% in CY2025 and rolled −47.7% Jan–Aug 2025. India-side BigMint shows India→EU +69% Apr–Sep 2025, and Argus/JPC show Italy +51% in FY2025-26. Possible causes: shipment vs customs-clearance timing (pre-CBAM stockpiling arrived late 2025), product scope, or a garbled summary. Unresolved without Comext.
3. India→EU steel value: USD 5.4 bn (RIS, CY2024) / EUR 3.9 bn (GreenSutra, CY2024) / USD 3.05 bn (GTRI, FY2024-25) / USD 3.63 bn (Trading Economics, CY2025) / USD 6.64 bn (AL Circle, "FY2024"). GTRI's FY24→FY25 −35.1% implies FY24 ≈ USD 4.7 bn, not 6.64. Treat the 6.64 figure as suspect.
4. GMK 2024 value rankings: India #1 at EUR 3.9 bn vs #5 at EUR 1.7 bn, depending on the undisclosed product category.
5. India quota: 1.64 Mt (FTA text, guaranteed) vs 1.9 Mt (commerce ministry, country-specific) vs 1.94 Mt (Crisil/GMK, down from 2.79 Mt) vs 2.8 Mt (ministry incl. residual pools). HRC: 597,274 t (EU June allocation summary) vs 509,605 t (FTA text summary).
6. Number of product categories in the new steel regime: 26 (Reed Smith summary) vs 30 (Commission statement summary).
7. Aluminium share of India's exports going to EU: ~25% (Construction World) vs ~11% (derived from GTRI).
8. Cement/fertiliser exports to EU: "none" (Aakhya) vs listed as exported (RIS); no numbers either way.
9. "Iron & steel shipments crashed by over 51% in FY26" (Vision IAS summary, https://visionias.in/current-affairs/upsc-daily-news-summary/article/2026-01-05/the-indian-express/economy/cbam-isnt-the-end-it-could-be-start-of-a-green-story). No primary source found, and it conflicts with JPC/Argus showing FY26 EU volumes up. Do not use.
