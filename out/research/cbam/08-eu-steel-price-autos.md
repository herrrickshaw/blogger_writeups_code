# 08 — EU steel prices and carmaker margins (24 Oct 2026), for "war → energy → dearer steel → cars without profit"

## EUROFER, European Steel in Action 2026 (annual report; user upload; text in 07-eurofer-annual-report-2026.txt) [primary]
- Apparent consumption +4.4% to 134 Mt in 2025 after three annual declines; ~10 Mt below 2019. Forecast +0.4% 2026, +2.2% 2027.
- Crude steel production -2.9% to 125.8 Mt, lowest ever; ~20 Mt below pre-pandemic, ~60 Mt below pre-2008.
- Imports of semi-finished and finished steel +14% to almost 40 Mt; record 30% import share (27% in 2024); Q4 2025 imports +53%.
- Trade deficit 24 Mt (all products), 15 Mt (finished).
- Over five years EU steel lost >30 Mt of capacity and ~30,000 direct jobs; capacity utilisation at historic lows in 2024. 293,000 direct employees.
- Electricity and gas prices "two to three times higher" than competitors in US, India, China, MENA. Wholesale power still >€85/MWh vs historical ~€45. Asks for €50/MWh total-energy-cost target by 2030.
- Renewable hydrogen €7–12/kg. Sector needs ~165 TWh fossil-free power and 2 Mt renewable H2 a year by 2030.
- 13 DRI plant projects (26.9 Mt DRI) and 20 EAF projects (44.1 Mt) possible by 2035; "majority of projects are facing delays due to high energy costs of natural gas and electricity ... and the slow scale-up and high costs of hydrogen".
- Energy prices rose further "due to the U.S.-Iranian conflict and the closure of the Hormuz-Strait" (2026).
- Construction 37% of steel use (+1.3% 2025); automotive production -4.3% in 2025, third consecutive contraction; growth only from 2027 (+2.9%).
- US 50% tariff on EU steel. OECD overcapacity >700 Mt (720 Mt by 2027).
- CBAM chapter: imported steel now "subject to a carbon cost broadly equivalent to that faced by European producers"; wants CBAM downstream, anti-circumvention, export solution (Commission's Dec 2025 Temporary Decarbonisation Fund judged insufficient).
