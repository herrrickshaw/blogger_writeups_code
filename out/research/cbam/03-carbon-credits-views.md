## 2. CBAM Article 9 deduction — does CCTS count?

- Commission draft implementing regulation on carbon price paid in third countries (Art. 9): published 13 May 2026, consultation closed 10 Jun 2026, NOT adopted as of mid-Oct 2026 — Global ELR https://www.globalelr.com/2026/06/european-commission-publishes-draft-implementing-rules-on-cbam-carbon-price-deductions/ ; ClearBlue Markets https://www.clearbluemarkets.com/knowledge-base/eu-cbam-draft-rules-clarify-carbon-price-deductions ; Kolum https://www.kolum.earth/en/cbam/weekly/2026-05-02 — May–Oct 2026 — [secondary]
- Rebates, free allocation, tax exemptions/refunds and indirect-cost compensation must be NETTED OUT of the price paid. There is one carve-out: revenue recycled into public, transparent, emissions-reducing subsidies for the same installation. "A nominal carbon price may yield a near-zero deduction" — same sources — 2026 — [secondary]
- Formula: certificate reduction = (effective carbon price ÷ EU reference price) × embedded emissions covered by that price — same sources — 2026 — [secondary]
- International credits count only if authorised under Paris Art. 6, and only up to 10% of reported emissions — Fastmarkets, "EU draft lays down rules on how prices paid abroad affect CBAM carbon price, cap Article 6 credits at 10%" https://www.fastmarkets.com/insights/eu-draft-lays-down-rules-on-how-prices-paid-abroad-affect-cbam-carbon-price-cap-article-6-credits-at-10/ — May 2026 — [press]
- European Parliament plenary position on the CBAM downstream extension: adopted 15 Sep 2026 (464–50–159). It REMOVES the option to offset CBAM obligations with Art. 6 credits. This is a negotiating position, not law; trilogue is pending — HKTDC https://research.hktdc.com/en/article/MjQ0ODc4NzAwMg ; Eurometal https://eurometal.net/european-parliament-approves-broader-cbam-scope-limitations-remain/ ; VATupdate 17 Sep 2026 https://www.vatupdate.com/2026/09/17/european-parliament-backs-expansion-of-cbam-to-downstream-steel-and-aluminium-products/ — Sep 2026 — [secondary]
- No country's scheme is recognised so far, and the draft itself recognises none — Global ELR / cbamguide (above) — as of 12 Oct 2026 — [secondary]
- EU had not recognised CCTS as a qualifying scheme as of 5 Aug 2026. India is negotiating recognition with the EU and the UK — trade-news site via search summary (cbamguide.com/countries/india) https://cbamguide.com/countries/india/ — Aug 2026 — [secondary]
- India–EU talks, New Delhi, 12 Oct 2026 (Agarwal; Gerassimos Thomas, DG TAXUD): agreed only to "continue technical dialogue on carbon pricing, emissions monitoring and verification, and capacity building". No recognition announced — DD India, KNN https://knnindia.co.in/news/newsdetails/global/india-eu-discuss-carbon-markets-cbam-and-technical-cooperation — Oct 2026 — [press]
- Design objection (LiveLaw analysis, "Carbon Credits, Not Carbon Prices: Why CCTS May Not Clear CBAM's Article 9 Bar"): CCTS is intensity-based baseline-and-credit. Out-performers earn CCCs, and a compliant entity "incurs no direct payment obligation", so it is uncertain whether that counts as a price "effectively paid" — https://www.livelaw.in/amp/articles/carbon-credits-carbon-prices-ccts-cbam-article9-bar-543236 — 2026 — [secondary]
- CII–IIM Ahmedabad report (29–30 Aug 2026): "domestic carbon prices are not automatically deducted under CBAM, requiring robust evidence of payments". It recommends keeping an intensity-based (baseline-and-credit) system, setting a transparent price collar and publishing a post-2026-27 target trajectory — ANI https://aninews.in/news/business/india-needs-credible-carbon-market-to-protect-exporters-as-eu-cbam-takes-effect-cii-iim-report20260830204556/ ; NewKerala https://www.newkerala.com/news/a/india-needs-credible-carbon-market-protect-exporters-as-718.htm — Aug 2026 — [press]
- **Analytical note (ours, derived from the draft rules above, not sourced):** under the net-of-rebates logic, a CCTS plant that beats its target pays nothing and earns sellable CCCs, so its effective price is ≤0. A plant that misses its target pays the CCC price only on its shortfall (target minus actual × output), not on all its emissions. So even with recognition, the deductible amount per tonne of embedded CO2 would be a small fraction of the CCC price, and the CCC price is itself a small fraction of the EU price.
- **Voluntary credits (Verra VCS, Gold Standard):** no source shows them as deductible. The draft's only credit route is Art. 6-authorised credits used within a third-country carbon-pricing scheme (capped at 10%), and Parliament wants even that removed. A finance lobby (June 2026) asked the Commission to broaden recognition to domestic and international credits, which confirms they are not currently covered — Carbon Pulse https://carbon-pulse.com/category/americas/us-canada/page/11/?site=cpp ; CP Daily 7 Apr 2026 https://carbon-pulse.com/500286/ — 2026 — [press]. CONFIRMED only by inference. No explicit Commission statement was read (ec.europa.eu is blocked).
- CBAM's own price: Q1 2026 certificate price €75.36/t; Q2 2026 €75.28/t — vendor guides via search summary — 2026 — [secondary]
- CBAM obligation phase-in mirrors the EU free-allocation phase-out ("CBAM factor" 2.5% in 2026, 5% in 2027, 10% in 2028) — one source via search summary (IEEFA-query results) — 2026 — [secondary]. Check against Reg. 2023/956 Art. 31 before use.

## 4. Think-tank and industry views

- **GTRI (Ajay Srivastava):** even with CCTS, "projected carbon prices will remain below USD 10/tCO2", far below EU ETS, so CCTS is a poor offset. The fix is cutting carbon intensity (energy efficiency, process optimisation), not credit-price offsets. He also warns that the proposed downstream and indirect-emissions expansion turns CBAM into "a much wider carbon tax" on manufactured goods — Outlook Business, "How CBAM Expansion Is Adding New Challenges for India Despite the FTA" https://www.outlookbusiness.com/economy-and-policy/how-eus-cbam-expansion-is-adding-new-challenges-for-india-despite-the-fta — 2026 — [press]
