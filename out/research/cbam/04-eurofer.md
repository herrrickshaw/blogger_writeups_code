# 04 — EUROFER "European Steel in Figures 2026" and quarterly outlooks

eurofer.eu, worldsteel.org are proxy-blocked from the drafting environment. Figures below come from
press summaries of EUROFER releases [press]; none read from the brochure PDF itself.

## 2025 (European Steel in Figures 2026 / Q1-2026 outlook) [press]
- EU crude steel production 125.8 Mt, -2.9% y/y (record low). worldsteel's EU-27 total differs.
- Apparent steel consumption 134.4 Mt, +4.4%.
- Imports = 30% of apparent consumption in 2025; record 37% in Q4 2025.
- India 2025: Q1 imports from India -56% y/y; Q2 India share 8% (-50% y/y).

## Q1 2026 [press]
- India share of EU finished-steel imports 8.9% (+12% y/y). Imports = 23% of consumption.

## H1 2026 (EUROFER Economic & Steel Market Outlook Q3 2026, released 1 Oct 2026) [press]
- Total imports -5%; finished steel -3%; flat +2%; long -18%.
- Supplier shares of finished steel: Turkey 15.7%, China 12.3%, South Korea 12%, Indonesia 8.8%,
  India 7.7% (volume +1% y/y), Ukraine 6.6%. Top five = 56%.
- Monthly finished-steel trade deficit averaged 1.035 Mt.
- 2026 consumption forecast +0.1% to 135 Mt.
- New EU steel import regime (replacing safeguard) with country-specific quotas announced 30 Jun 2026.
