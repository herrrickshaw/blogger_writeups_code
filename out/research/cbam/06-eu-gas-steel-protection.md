## 5. How CBAM and the import regime protect EU producers

- 2026 is the first definitive CBAM year. EU producers in CBAM sectors keep 97.5% of free allocation. Imports get a matching Free Allocation Adjustment (FAA = benchmark-based SEFA x imported mass), so importers pay on about 2.5% of the gross obligation when their emissions sit at the benchmark — https://greensutra.in/news/cbam-free-allocation-adjustment-2026/ (2026) [secondary]
- The legal basis is Commission Implementing Regulation (EU) 2025/2620, adopted 16 Dec 2025 and applying from 1 Jan 2026 — same source [secondary]
- Omnibus Regulation (EU) 2025/2083 moved the start of certificate sales and surrender to 1 Feb 2027 — same source [secondary]
- Phase-in schedule: CBAM covers 2.5% in 2026, 5% in 2027, 10% in 2028, reaching 100% in 2034 — https://expertdirectory.s-ge.com/data/files/MSE_Commentary_Apr2026_CBAM.pdf (Apr 2026) [secondary]; free allocation cut by 2.5% in 2026 and 5% in 2027 — https://sandbag.be/2026/07/16/response-thyssenkrupp/ (16 Jul 2026) [secondary]
- Labelling: some sources call 97.5% the "CBAM factor" (Art. 10a(1a) ETS Directive); others call the payable 0.025 the CBAM factor — https://greensutra.in/news/cbam-free-allocation-adjustment-2026/ vs https://www.decerna.co.uk/knowledge-base/life-cycle-assessment/5-carbon-border-adjustment-mechanism-cbam/cbam-certificates-pricing (2026) [secondary]
- Key point for the article: the FAA is calculated from the EU benchmark, not from the importer's own emissions. An importer whose emissions are above the benchmark (as with most Indian BF-BOF and coal-DRI steel) pays the full ETS price on all emissions above about 97.5% of the benchmark. An EU producer at the benchmark pays almost nothing. Derived from the mechanism described above.
- CBAM certificate reference price: Q1 2026 EUR 75.36/tCO2; Q2 2026 EUR 75.28/tCO2. In 2026 it is a quarterly average of ETS auction prices — https://gmk.center/en/news/the-ec-has-announced-the-price-of-cbam-allowances-for-q2-2026/ (6 Jul 2026, reporting the Commission) [press]
- CBAM prices carbon only. It does not price the gas or electricity cost gap (EU gas about 4-5x the US, power about 2-3x). The Commission handles energy separately: the Steel and Metals Action Plan pushes PPAs, grid access and state-aid electricity relief — https://gmk.center/en/news/new-eu-state-aid-program-will-depend-on-implementation-at-national-levels/ (4 Jul 2025) [press]; https://www.gleisslutz.com/de/know-how/aktionsplan-fuer-stahl-und-metalle-der-europaeischen-union (2025) [secondary]
- New steel import regime replacing the 2018 safeguard (expired 30 Jun 2026): tariff-free quota of 18.3 Mt, -47% from 2024 quota levels; 50% out-of-quota duty (up from 25%); melt-and-pour origin rule; no fixed expiry. European Parliament approved it 19 May 2026 (606-16-39); in force 1 Jul 2026 — https://www.spglobal.com/energy/en/news-research/latest-news/metals/051926-eu-lawmakers-approve-tighter-steel-import-curbs-to-counter-global-overcapacity (19 May 2026) [press]; https://www.akingump.com/en/insights/alerts/eu-proposes-higher-tariffs-and-lower-quotas-on-steel-imports-and-plans-to-renegotiate-its-wto-tariff-commitments (Oct 2025; quarterly quotas, no carryover in the proposal) [secondary]
- The melt-and-pour requirement applies from October 2026 (one commercial source) — https://www.tacto.ai/en-us/commodities/steel-price (2026) [secondary]
- India's country-specific HRC quota was fully used up on 8 Oct 2026. Turkey's quota is also exhausted, with provisional anti-dumping duty of about 15% expected — https://news.metal.com/newscontent/104148908 (SMM, Oct 2026) [press]
- Commission CBAM package, 16-17 Dec 2025: proposal COM(2025) 419 extends CBAM to about 180 downstream steel- and aluminium-intensive products (machinery, vehicle parts, appliances, construction equipment, some scrap) and tightens anti-circumvention rules. A separate Temporary Decarbonisation Fund takes 25% of 2028-29 CBAM revenue (covering 2026-27 production) and is the "export solution" — https://www.reedsmith.com/our-insights/blogs/trade-compliance-resource-hub/4874/commission-proposes-expansion-of-eu-cbam-to-downstream-goods-and-addresses-potential-cbam-abusive-practices/ (19 Dec 2025) [secondary]; https://icapcarbonaction.com/en/news/eu-cbam-enters-compliance-phase-and-outlines-path-ahead (2026) [secondary]; https://eu.bellona.org/?p=42567 (Dec 2025) [secondary]

Trilogue status is not confirmed — https://www.akingump.com/en/insights/alerts/cbam-expansion-poised-to-impact-global-supply-chains (2026) [secondary]
