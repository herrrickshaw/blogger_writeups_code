- Current (as of 13 Oct 2026): search summaries from June 2026 conflict with the "E27 by 2028" milestone and show new E22 to E30 policy steps.
  - The Centre exempted E22 to E30 petrol from central excise duty by notifications dated 10 June 2026 (https://www.etvbharat.com/amp/en/bharat/government-exempts-22-pc-to-30-pc-ethanol-blended-petrol-from-central-excise-duty-enn26061100829 and https://www.tribuneindia.com/news/india/centre-waives-excise-duty-on-ethanol-blended-petrol/, summaries only). E22 to E30 standards (IS 19850) were reported as notified in May 2026, and the blends were not yet on retail sale.
  - A Kotak Neo report says E21 is being considered for 2027 and E25 possibly by 2029, with no formal E25/E30 roadmap yet (https://www.kotakneo.com/news/sectors/india-ethanol-blending-e21-2027-e25-2029-roadmap-2026/, summary only). That conflicts with "E27 by around 2028".
  - Gadkari is reported to say blends above E20 attract 18% GST versus 5% on E20 (https://bioenergytimes.com/gadkari-pushes-e85-fuel-seeks-lower-taxes-on-higher-ethanol-blends/, summary only; no official notification seen).
  - I found no 2026 GST Council change to the 5% ethanol rate; the 5% rate dates from the 48th Council, Dec 2022 (https://www.outlookbusiness.com/news/gst-council-recommends-5-tax-on-ethyl-alcohol-from-18--news-245803, summary only). E20 remains the retail blend as far as I could see.
- Suggested wording: "Update, 13 October 2026: E22 to E30 fuel standards were notified in May 2026 and central excise duty on such blends was waived in June 2026, but E20 remains the retail blend and no formal E27/E30 timetable was found; the E27-by-2028 and E30-by-2030 milestones above should be read as unconfirmed targets."
- Confidence: roadmap conflict is single-source (Kotak Neo); excise exemption is supported by 3+ news summaries (Tribune, ETV Bharat, KNN, Tradejini) but no primary notification was opened. GST 18% claim is unverified.
