### 2. would-more-sgst-fix-donor-state-problem (HIGH)
- Claim in post: "All three of these require either a constitutional-level political negotiation (raising 41% needs Union Cabinet and Finance Commission buy-in) or a future Finance Commission choosing to change its own formula"; also "Raise the vertical devolution share from 41% to 50%" and "Karnataka has specifically proposed using GSDP contribution as an indicator, under which its own horizontal share would rise from roughly 3.64% to 5.24%." The post never mentions the 16th Finance Commission (grep for 16th/15th returns nothing).
- Current (as of 13 Oct 2026): The 16th Finance Commission report was tabled on 1 Feb 2026 and accepted by the Government. It kept the states' share at 41% for 2026-31 and reworked the horizontal criteria: contribution to GDP now carries 10% weight (replacing the 2.5% tax-effort criterion), area weight rises to 10%, and income-distance weight is reduced. ICRA is quoted as estimating 14 of 28 states gain share. So the 41% vs 50% ask was effectively decided against the states for 2026-31, and a GDP-contribution criterion has already been adopted in part. The Karnataka and Maharashtra actual shares were not found.
  - https://prsindia.org/policy/report-summaries/report-of-the-16th-finance-commission-for-2026-31 (summary only)
  - https://moneylife.in/article/16th-finance-commission-retains-41-percentage-tax-devolution-interse-shares-to-shift-as-criteria-are-reworked-report/79561.html (summary only)
  - https://visionias.in/current-affairs/news-today/2026-02-02/economy/16th-finance-commission-fc-report-presented-in-the-lok-sabha-by-finance-minister (summary only)
- Suggested wording: "Update, 13 Oct 2026: the 16th Finance Commission (report tabled 1 February 2026) retained the states' share at 41% for 2026-31 and added a 10% weight for contribution to GDP in the horizontal formula; the proposals described here pre-date or are only partly reflected in that award. Karnataka's actual 16th-FC share should be checked against the report before quoting 3.64% to 5.24%." Reword "a future Finance Commission" to "the next Finance Commission (after 2031)".

### 3. the-diesel-truck-isnt-why-your-eggs (HIGH)
- Claim in post: "Sugar's all-India retail average stood at ₹47.66/kg on 11 July 2026, even as the government simultaneously permitted 40 lakh metric tonnes of sugar to be diverted to ethanol production". Also "+35–40% Rise in egg prices over the past year (NECC data, as of Sept 2026)" and "a reported 15% year-on-year rise in overall poultry feed costs".
- Current (as of 13 Oct 2026):
  - Sugar: search summaries report retail sugar jumped from about ₹45/kg in July to about ₹65/kg in August 2026, with ₹62-65/kg later; the government imposed stock limits on bulk consumers (10 tonnes/month threshold) from 1 Sep to 30 Nov and says diversion is not the main driver. One summary says the 40 lakh tonne permission dates to the 2024-25 season, and that 2025-26 diversion is closer to 30 lakh tonnes (about 9% of production). So the "last mile has not moved" example for sugar is overtaken: sugar retail rose roughly 35-45% within weeks of the 11 July datapoint, and the 40 lakh tonne figure may be the wrong season. Sources: https://deccanchronicle.com/business/economics/india-imposes-sugar-stockholding-limits-on-bulk-consumers-tightens-curbs-1980742 ; https://eng.ruralvoice.in/government-tightens-sugar-stock-rules-as-prices-stay-elevated-orders-strict-compliance-from-mills ; https://www.outlookbusiness.com/planet/industry/govt-permits-sugar-mills-distilleries-to-make-ethanol-without-any-restrictions-in-2025-26-market-year (all summary only; price figures are approximate and the Outlook summary's season attribution is unverified).
  - Eggs/feed: a CLFMA-based summary (source page unclear; closest links https://agrospectrumindia.com/news/59/34770/poultry-prices-slide-as-maize-costs-climb-putting-pressure-on-indias-livestock-economy.html and https://indiaseatradenews.com/?p=104359, summary only) gives egg prices of ₹627/100 in June, ₹687 in July, then ₹546 in August 2026, soybean meal peaking at ₹58,156/t in Aug and easing to ₹48,750-49,000, layer feed up from ₹24-26 to ₹30-32/kg, maize up about 14% over June-Aug. I could not find a September or October NECC print, so the "35-40% YoY as of Sept 2026" claim is neither confirmed nor refuted, but the August dip suggests it should be re-checked.
- Suggested wording: "Update, 13 Oct 2026: after the 11 July reading of ₹47.66/kg, press reports put retail sugar at roughly ₹62-65/kg by late August-September 2026, and the government has since imposed stockholding limits on bulk buyers (1 Sep-30 Nov); the ethanol-diversion figure quoted above should be read against the season it applies to (reports put 2025-26 diversion nearer 30 lakh tonnes). Egg farm-gate prices fell in August 2026 after peaking in July; the year-on-year figure above should be read as a Sept 2026 snapshot."
- Confidence: single source per item (search summaries, not opened); sugar price rise reported by 2 outlets; egg Sept figure unverified.
