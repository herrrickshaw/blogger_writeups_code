  - Red Cross share: the Olav Thon Group page says 34.5% of gross revenue goes to the Red Cross, with the rest covering winnings, store fees and operations (https://thon.no/en/social-responsibility/thon-gives-back/recycling--lottery/, search summary only). Same page: NOK 737.4M to the Red Cross as of Nov 2023, which matches the post's cumulative figure.

The post presents the scheme as running uninterrupted ("16 years"). A 2025 Red Cross magazine reportedly says income since 2008 passed NOK 1 billion (https://www.rodekors.no/globalassets/_rapporter/rode-kors-magasinet/2025/2025_04_rode-kors-magasinet.pdf, search summary only). So "NOK 737.4M by 2023" is stale as a latest figure.
  - Canada: I found no evidence of any provincial rollout or government consideration of a lottery-style refund. Coverage describes only the study and policy proposals (https://beyond.ubc.ca/how-a-lottery-style-refund-system-could-boost-recycling/ , https://cleantechnica.com/2025/07/29/would-a-lottery-reward-make-people-more-likely-to-recycle/ , search summaries only). The coverage is dated 2025 and says the experiments ran in BC and Alberta and were published in Waste Management; the post's "(2024)" and "2023–24" dating could not be confirmed.
  - 2026 deposit rate "NOK 2–3 (2026 rate)": not found; unverified.
- Suggested wording: "As of the latest figures we could find, the Thon Group states that 34.5% of gross revenue goes to the Red Cross (the share of net proceeds after prizes may be higher); the Red Cross reported more than NOK 1 billion received since 2008 in 2025, and the lottery paused sales in late 2025 after reaching its annual statutory turnover limit." For Canada: replace "provincial rollout under consideration" with "we found no announced provincial rollout as of October 2026; the work so far is a research study with policy proposals."
- Confidence: single source each for the 34.5% share and the 2025 closure (both search summaries, not opened); the Canada rollout point is absence of evidence only (unverified); 2026 rate unverified.

- Suggested wording: "Update: on 24 July 2026 the Union Cabinet approved the Rs 3,030 crore BHAVYA-Rasayan scheme for three chemical parks. It supports park infrastructure and is not a production-linked incentive; we found no approved chemicals PLI as of 13 October 2026."
- Confidence: single source family (two outlets reporting the same Cabinet decision; PIB original not opened). Absence of a chemicals PLI is unverified.
