## 1. EU level

**Instruments**
- RED III (Directive (EU) 2023/2413), transport provisions. Member States may choose either 29% renewable share in transport by 2030 or a 14.5% GHG-intensity reduction. Binding combined sub-target 5.5% advanced biofuels + RFNBO by 2030, with at least 1% RFNBO inside it (C2: Clean Hydrogen Observatory / Hydrogen Council summaries; France amendment text). Transposition deadline May 2025 for the transport provisions.
- Caps and lists [BK, partly supported by Argus trade summary]: crop-based cap at 2020 level +1pp, max 7%; high-ILUC phase-out; Annex IX Part B (UCO, tallow cat. 1-2) soft cap 1.7% of transport energy, Member States may deviate with justification (the 1.7% is C: Argus summary says "soft cap of 1.7pc"; Argus says some states exceed it). Annex IX Part A feeds the advanced sub-target.
- Hydrogen in refineries: RFNBO hydrogen used in refining is counted toward the RFNBO transport target [BK]; Germany's draft explicitly obliges suppliers to use RFNBO instead of grey hydrogen in refineries (S: hydronews summary). Details of Article 27 counting rules not found.
- ReFuelEU Aviation (Reg. 2023/2405): 2% SAF 2025, 6% 2030 with synthetic sub-mandate 1.2% (average 2030-31), 20% 2035 with 5% synthetic, then 34% 2040, 42% 2045, 70% 2050 with 35% synthetic [BK for 2040-2050; 2025/2030/2035 and synthetic 1.2%/5% supported by Oeko-Institut/EASA search snippets, S]. Supplier-level flexibility: 2025-2034 minimum shares may be met as an average across Union airports (EASA, S).
- FuelEU Maritime (Reg. 2023/1805): GHG-intensity limit vs 91.16 gCO2e/MJ baseline: -2% 2025-2029 (89.34), -6% 2030-2034 (85.69) (S: Britannia P&I). RFNBO multiplier 2 from 1 Jan 2025 to 31 Dec 2033 per Parliament summary; BIMCO and Britannia describe end as 2034 (sources disagree, 2033 appears the legal date). 2% RFNBO sub-target from 2034 if RFNBO <1% of fuel mix in 2031, waivable by Commission (S). First compliance cycle (2025 data): verification 31 Mar 2026, penalties paid by 30 Jun 2026; no published results found.
- Hydrogen: RFNBO delegated acts 2023/1184 and 2023/1185 (additionality, correlation, GHG methodology). Low-carbon hydrogen methodology: Commission Delegated Regulation (EU) 2025/2359, published OJ 21 Nov 2025, in force (S-C2: EUR-Lex listing + hydronews). Threshold 70% saving, 3.38 kgCO2e/kg H2 (S: Deloitte/IEA snippets).
- European Hydrogen Bank, third auction (IF25): results 7 May 2026; 9 projects, about EUR 1.09 bn, about 1.1 GW electrolysis, 58 bids from 11 countries, over 7x oversubscribed; premium range EUR 0.57-3.49/kg per Hydrogen Europe, but pv-magazine and an oenergetice headline say lowest EUR 0.44/kg (sources differ). Fixed premium per kg, up to 10 years. Fourth auction IF26 due by end 2026, budget EUR 500 m (S). Earlier rounds, Innovation Fund details: not found.

**Mechanism**: supplier obligations set in national law (energy share or GHG intensity); multipliers for RFNBO in transport [BK] (the Hydrogen Council summary also mentions multipliers).

**Hydrogen link**: RFNBO sub-target 1% by 2030 in transport; industrial RFNBO 42% by 2030 (not searched). HVO/HEFA need hydrogen; refinery RFNBO hydrogen counting see above.

**Status flags (2026)**
- Transposition: counts conflict. Clean Hydrogen Observatory (Dec 2025): only Romania, Czechia, Slovakia, Lithuania fully transposed RFNBO mandates for transport and industry; Finland and Denmark transport only (S via search summary). A 2026 note citing BloombergNEF says 12 of 27 have legislated RFNBO quotas (S, conflicts). EBB (Jan 2026): only two states met the May 2025 deadline, some texts may arrive 2027 (S). Commission opened infringement procedures against all/most states; Sweden was referred to the CJEU in Oct 2025 (permitting provisions, S). Oct 2026 count: not found.
- RFNBO delegated act revision: Commission roadmap pointed to a revised act by June 2026 (about 18 months ahead of the 2028 review); expectations of delaying additionality and hourly correlation to 2032-33 (consultancy, advocacy sources). Adoption NOT confirmed.
- RED post-2030 revision ("RED IV"): MLex 8 Oct 2026 says Commission preparing it; 13 industry groups ask to keep binding transport targets post-2030; consultation launched early 2026 (Cleary Gottlieb). Content/timing unverified. Energy omnibus slated Q3 2026.
- ETS: Commission proposed a targeted EU ETS revision 17 Jul 2026 and a MRV/FuelEU Maritime revision (agreement expected 2027) (S: EC page, Bureau Veritas). ETS2 supply-control amendment endorsed Feb 2026 (S). Contents of these proposals not found.
- ReFuelEU: formal review due 2027 (Carbon Gap, S&P Apr 2026) vs 2026 (Oeko-Institut) - sources conflict; industry pressing for book-and-claim; no proposal found.
- Payment/penalty: penalties for missing RFNBO quotas widely judged too low (Hydrogen Council note); Germany's EUR 14/kg cited as strongest.

## 2. Germany

**Instruments**
- GHG quota (THG-Quote, BImSchG / 38. BImSchV), since 2015; reformed by "Zweites Gesetz zur Weiterentwicklung der Treibhausgasminderungs-Quote" (RED III implementation). Cabinet 10 Dec 2025; Bundestag adopted 24 Apr 2026; Bundesrat consent reported early May 2026 (S: Argus/Gleiss Lutz summaries; publication in Bundesgesetzblatt not confirmed). Applied retroactively from 1 Jan 2026 (Shell Bundestag statement, S).
- Levels. Cabinet draft: 12% 2026, 25% 2030, 36% 2035, 59% 2040. Bundestag-adopted per Gleiss Lutz: 2027 17.5%, 2030 26.5%, 2035 41%, 2040 65% (draft values: 16/25/36/59). Sources differ on 2040 (59 vs 65) because they describe draft vs adopted text; adopted figures from one law-firm source only (S). Existing-law figure 25.1% for 2030 cited by Argus.
- Advanced biofuels sub-quota: 1% to 2% in 2026, rising to 9% by 2040 (topagrar/hydronews, S; draft stage). Double counting of advanced biofuels abolished from 2026. Palm-oil residues (POME) excluded from 2027, 2026 transitional (Argus, S).
- RFNBO transport quota: Hydrogen Council note (Apr 2026) cites 0.1% in 2026, 5% in 2035, 10% by 2040 (S, press); Bundesrat had demanded higher mid-term minimums for 2030/2032/2034 (UNITI, S). 2030 value NOT found. Penalty EUR 14/kg cited (Hydrogen Council).
- Annex IX Part B: Germany expected to keep an overall 1.7% limit (Argus, S). Sector scope: road, with industry lobbying to limit to road; a SAF element mentioned in a cabinet-news headline (ukragroconsult) not verified.

**Coverage and mechanism**: obligated parties are fuel suppliers (those placing fuel on market); compliance by tradable GHG-quota certificates (reduction of GHG intensity vs fossil comparator); credits for biofuels, RFNBO, renewable electricity in EVs, and refinery measures. Argus: prices of 2027 quota certificates rose after adoption.

**Hydrogen link**
- Green hydrogen in refineries: law obliges use of RFNBO hydrogen in refineries in place of grey (hydronews, S).
- Hydrogen core grid (Kernnetz): approx. 9,040 km, completion planned by 2032, about 60% repurposed gas pipes; more than 500 km in operation by end 2025; capacity reservations opened 19 Mar 2026; reservations roughly doubled from about 3 GW to almost 6 GW by Sep 2026; draft integrated network development plan Mar 2026 (hydrogen 7,007 km, about EUR 20.1 bn to 2037) (S: IHK / industrial-production / regional news).
- H2Global, Wasserstoffbeschleunigungsgesetz, electrolyser subsidies: NOT found.

**Status flags**: final Bundesgesetzblatt text and RFNBO 2030 sub-quota unverified; stakeholder fight over UCO and waste-based biofuels and electricity crediting.

## 3. United Kingdom

**Instruments**
- RTFO (since 2008; Order 2007 as amended), obligated: suppliers of road and non-road fuel; tradable RTFCs. Main obligation rising to 14.6% by 2032 then flat (earlier raised from 9.6%); DfT statutory review/policy update 2025 proposed post-2032 trajectory, rewards for low-carbon fuels, nuclear-derived fuels, hydrogen fuel cell mobile generators; legislation targeted for 2026 for 2027 obligation year (HSF Kramer, Mondaq; S). All RTFO-compliant hydrogen currently counts as a development fuel (double certificates) (CMS, S). Proposals: PPAs to evidence additional power for RFNBO; extend RFNBO support to maritime, rail, non-road; biohydrogen from SMR/ATR to lose eligibility (CMS summary; date not confirmed). 2026 annual percentages and whether the amending SI passed: NOT found.
- SAF Mandate (SAF Mandate Order 2024; from 1 Jan 2025): 2% 2025, 3.6% 2026, 5.2% 2027, rising to 10% 2030 and 22% 2040 (ICAO Aviation Climate Week 2026 slide deck, S; 2025/2026/2027 and 22% in 2040 from this source). Obligated: jet fuel suppliers of at least 15.9 TJ per year; tradable SAF certificates, buy-out price as alternative [BK for buy-out level]. HEFA cap and PtL obligation: PtL obligation from 2028: 0.2%, 0.5% in 2030, 3.5% in 2040 (Norton Rose Fulbright, S).
- Sustainable Aviation Fuel Act: ICAO slides state Royal Assent 5 March 2026 (S); earlier law-firm notes expected end-2026. Revenue Certainty Mechanism: guaranteed strike-price contracts (CfD-like) for SAF producers, counterparty expected to be the Low Carbon Contracts Company (Orrick, S); funded by a levy on aviation fuel suppliers; first levy consultation closed 8 Jan 2026, second DfT levy consultation published June 2026; contract allocation strategy due later 2026 (Burges Salmon, S). Lords amendments on ring-fencing for UK fuel and excluding PtL: outcome not confirmed.
- Hydrogen: HAR1 - 11 projects, weighted average strike price GBP 241/MWh (about GBP 9.50/kg), 15-year Low Carbon Hydrogen Agreement with LCCC; 9 of 11 signed by Jul 2025 (S&P, h2-view; C2). HAR2: 27 projects shortlisted Apr 2025, 765 MW (target 875 MW); due diligence completed by Mar 2026 (parliamentary answer, S); final awards expected 2026 but NO announcement found. Hydrogen Production Business Model and Low Carbon Hydrogen Standard details not searched specifically (LCHA operated by LCCC is confirmed).

**Stacking**: not found.

**Status flags**: RTFO reform SI, HAR2 awards, RCM levy design and allocation all pending/unconfirmed.

## 4. Netherlands (HBE to ERE)

- From 1 Jan 2026 the system moves from renewable-energy units (HBE) to GHG-intensity-based emission reduction units (ERE); carried-over HBEs convert to EREs on 1 Apr 2026 (engine.online, Argus; S).
- Draft (June 2025) reported by Argus: 14.4% renewable share for road in 2026, 27.1% by 2030; crop cap 1.4% (land); Part B caps 4.29% land and 11.07% inland shipping; maritime cannot claim Part B; UCO excluded from ERE eligibility; advanced and RFNBO sub-targets decoupled; marine UCOME treated as fossil (Argus correction). Dutch parliament passed a motion to extend RED III timeline to 2035 (motion only, outcome unconfirmed).
- RFNBO transport quota: Hydrogen Council note says passed March 2026, 1.8% by 2030; industry quota still in Parliament (S). Final regulation figures not verified.
- Sources: https://engine.online/news/explained-dutch-shift-to-ere-tickets-7624 ; https://www.argusmedia.com/news-and-insights/latest-market-news/2702749-netherlands-publishes-red-iii-biofuels-draft ; https://www.argusmedia.com/en/news-and-insights/latest-market-news/2747139-netherlands-to-treat-marine-ucome-as-fossil-correction (summary only; draft-stage numbers, confidence S).

## 5. France (TIRUERT)

- TIRUERT (tax incentive for use of renewable energy in transport) due to be replaced by IRICC from 1 Jan 2026; reportedly deferred a year, with TIRUERT extended to 31 Dec 2026 and IRICC from 1 Jan 2027 (Assemblée nationale amendment text, S); another PLF 2027 amendment mentions entry into force at latest 1 Jan 2028 (conflict). Diesel target 10.3% vs proposed 10% for 2026 (amendment withdrawn; adopted rate not found). Renewable-hydrogen minimum 0.3% amendment for petrol/diesel not known to be adopted. Final law texts not seen. Sources: https://www.assemblee-nationale.fr/dyn/17/amendements/1906A/AN/779 ; https://www.assemblee-nationale.fr/dyn/17/amendements/3210A/CION_FIN/CF930 (amendments, not law; confidence U-S). France still allows double counting (Argus Dec 2025 viewpoint).
