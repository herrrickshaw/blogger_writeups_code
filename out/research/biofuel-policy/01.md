"Summary only" means I did not open the page. Several summaries said they had not checked the underlying statute or Federal Register text.

## 1. United States (federal)

### 1a. Renewable Fuel Standard (RFS), Set 2 rule for 2026-2027
- Started 2005/2007. Current instrument: EPA "Set 2" final rule for 2026 and 2027. Announced 26-27 March 2026 (sources differ by a day), published 1 April 2026, effective 15 June 2026. C2.
- Volumes in billion RINs, per EPA table as relayed by search summaries:

| Category | 2026 | 2027 |
|---|---|---|
| Total renewable fuel (incl. reallocation) | 26.81 | 27.02 |
| Advanced biofuel | 11.10 | 11.32 |
| Biomass-based diesel (BBD) | 9.07 | 9.20 |
| Cellulosic | 1.36 | 1.43 |
| Conventional (implied-ethanol limit) | 15 billion gal | 15 billion gal |

- The June 2025 proposal was lower (total 24.02 and 24.46 billion RINs for 2026 and 2027). C2.
- BBD is set in RINs, not gallons, for the first time. One source (EMA) gives 9.07 billion RINs for 2026. Another (Crux) says 8.86 billion "gallons", and OPIS says 8.86 billion RINs. These conflict; check the EPA fact sheet or Federal Register. U.
- Small refinery exemptions (SREs): 70% of the SRE volumes granted for 2023-2025 are reallocated into the 2026-2027 percentage standards. EPA had considered 0-100%. C2. I found nothing on the August 2025 SRE decisions or on lawsuits against the rule.
- Foreign fuel: the half-RIN rule for imported fuel and fuel from foreign feedstocks was NOT finalized for 2026-27. EPA says it intends to finalize it "beginning in 2028" in a later action. One summary (NatLawReview/Mondaq) phrased it as "Starting in 2028, foreign fuels and feedstocks will receive half the RFS compliance value". The Crux/OPIS reading (intended, future action) is more precise. Treat as proposed, not enacted. C2 on the intent; the final-rule status is U.
- Renewable electricity (eRINs) removed as a qualifying fuel. 2025 cellulosic volume partly waived. In 2027 renewable diesel gets 1.5 RINs/gal, the same as biodiesel (Crux, single source, C1).
- COVERAGE: road fuels (gasoline, diesel), plus jet and heating oil via RINs. The obligated parties are refiners and importers (the "obligated parties"). RIN categories: D3 cellulosic, D4 biomass-based diesel, D5 advanced, D6 conventional (corn ethanol), D7 cellulosic diesel. The D-code descriptions are standard background and were not in the search summaries. Not verified in this session.
- MECHANISM: tradable RINs and an annual percentage standard.
- RNG/biogas groups say the D3 target is still too low (DTN/NatLawReview summaries, advocacy view).

### 1b. 45Z Clean Fuel Production Credit (IRC section 45Z)
- Started 1 Jan 2025 (IRA). Amended by the One Big Beautiful Bill Act (OBBBA, 2025). IRS-listed changes (C2): extended to 31 Dec 2029; feedstocks must be grown/produced in the US, Mexico or Canada; prohibited-foreign-entity limits; the special SAF rate is eliminated; negative emissions rates barred except for animal-manure fuels; indirect land-use change excluded from emissions rates. The House committee version (to 2031) was not enacted.
- Rates: per-gallon amount multiplied by an emissions factor of (50 minus the emissions rate in kg CO2e/mmBtu) divided by 50. Eligibility ceiling is 50 kg CO2e/mmBtu. Pre-OBBBA amounts: $0.20/gal base and $1.00/gal with prevailing wage and apprenticeship (PWA) for non-aviation fuel; SAF $0.35 and $1.75. After 2025 SAF falls to the non-SAF amounts ($0.20 / $1.00 per gal). Sources for the amounts are 2025 articles; the post-2025 SAF equalisation is from one source (C1). The "50 / emissions rate" formula and amounts were seen only in secondary summaries.
- Regulatory status: proposed regulations REG-121244-23 issued 3 Feb 2026; public hearing set for 28 May 2026; taxpayers must use the 45ZCF-GREET model. Two searches found NO final regulations as of mid-Oct 2026 (absence of evidence only; result sets did not show Sept/Oct 2026 items). The annual emissions-rate table guidance applies retroactively to tax years ending on or after 10 Jan 2025.
- COVERAGE: domestically produced transportation fuel (ethanol, biodiesel, renewable diesel, SAF). Not the producer's own use; sales to unrelated parties.
- MECHANISM: tax credit per gallon, transferable/elective-pay rules referenced.
- Stacking: a facility cannot claim 45Z together with 45V, the section 48 specified clean hydrogen credit, or 45Q (C1, CRS/3Degrees summaries). Stacking with RINs or LCFS credits: not found in this session.
- Contested: Canadian ethanol groups blame 45Z for rising US ethanol imports into Canada (see Canada).

### 1c. 45V Clean Hydrogen Production Credit
- Started 2023 (IRA); up to $3/kg, scaled by lifecycle carbon intensity, for 10 years from placed-in-service (C1: JMCO/CRS snippets, not opened). 
- OBBBA (2025): construction must begin before 1 Jan 2028 (previously 2033). C2 (CRS March 2026 and ICCT; JMCO). One source describes it as a production sunset; the CRS/ICCT framing is a construction-start test. Use the construction-start framing but flag it.
- 2026 Treasury guidance on "beginning of construction" for 45V: not found.
- DOE hydrogen hubs: about $7.56 billion of awards cancelled in fall 2025, including two West Coast hubs. An April 2026 Bloomberg report said about $5 billion for the five remaining hubs sat on a "retain or modify" list. Reported plan, not confirmed (single press source, via search summary).

### 1d. State low-carbon fuel programs
- California LCFS: amendments adopted late 2024, first disapproved by OAL in Feb 2025, then took effect July 2025 (Argus: "tougher standards the state imposed in July"). Credit bank about 38.3 million credits at end-2025, down 8.46% in Q1 2026 (OPIS). Q1 2026 credits 6.78 million vs deficits 9.76 million. Spot about $63-66.50/t in Jan 2026; Dec-2026 futures up to $72/t (Argus; January report). Sylvera says Q1 2026 average US$63. A cap near US$268.90/t for Jun 2025 - May 2026 appears in one Chinese-language Sylvera page only (U). Targets: I did not retrieve the specific CI-reduction percentages; not found.
- Oregon Clean Fuels Program: 20% cut by 2030 with work on a 50% target by 2040 (Argus summary, 2025). 2026 prices: not found.
- Washington Clean Fuel Standard: Ecology amended chapter 173-424 WAC; adopted 20 Oct 2025, effective 20 Nov 2025; 2026 benchmark 92.00 gCO2e/MJ gasoline and 93.10 diesel (7% below 2017). 2SHB 1409 (signed 17 May 2025): 45% below 2017 by 2038, possible 55%; 3-4% annual cuts 2028-2038. New rulemaking opened Jan 2026 to set 2028-2038 standards and move to WA-GREET 4.0; status not found. Amendments add electrolysis-energy provisions for alternative jet and marine fuel (hydrogen link). Source: Ecology/Trinity (C2).
- New Mexico Clean Transportation Fuel Standard: obligations accruing; compliance proof April 2028; max credit price $270/t for the first compliance period (Argus, single source, C1).
- Other new state programs: none confirmed by this research.

### 1e. SAF incentives
- Federal: 45Z SAF premium removed after 2025 (see 1b). A "US opens registration for SAF credits under 45Z" item (safinvestor.com) was seen as a title only.
- Illinois: $1.50/gal credit for airlines buying or using SAF in the state, from 1 July 2023; needs 50% CI reduction; domestic-feedstock-only from 2028. Stackable with federal credits (Argus/ABUSA, 2023-24 sources; current status not re-verified).
- Nebraska: 75 cents/gal income tax credit beginning 1 Jan 2027, plus up to 50 cents extra; the cap was reportedly under review (single source, C1).
- Washington and Minnesota have SAF programs; amounts not found. Washington's Clean Fuel Standard rule amendments added SAF/aviation credit opportunities.

### 1f. Hydrogen link (US)
- HEFA/renewable diesel hydrotreating needs hydrogen (technical background, not in the sources). Whether refinery hydrogen is counted in 45Z or LCFS CI: not found.
- Hydrogen credits do not stack with 45Z on one facility (see 1b). RFS-specific treatment of renewable hydrogen: not found.
- Washington's rule adds electrolysis-energy provisions for e-fuels.

## 2. Canada

### 2a. Clean Fuel Regulations (CFR, SOR/2022-140)
- In force since 2022/2023 (credit market from July 2023). Mechanism: GHG-intensity (carbon-intensity) reduction requirement on liquid fossil fuel (gasoline, diesel) primary suppliers; tradable compliance credits from low-carbon fuels, projects and charging/hydrogen. Exact CI-reduction schedule (e.g. 15% by 2030): not retrieved in this session.
- Hydrogen: the CFR allows credits for hydrogen used in fuel cell vehicles or as a vehicle fuel; the hydrogen must meet low-CI criteria and have an approved documented CI (IEA policy page, summary only).
- Status (all single-source or partial): ECCC announced targeted amendments on 5 Sept 2025; discussion paper comments closed 15 Jan 2026. Options: a minimum domestic-content requirement or a credit multiplier (or hybrid) for domestic biofuel. As of 30 June 2026 the Farms and Fuels Alliance was still urging a Canada Gazette Part I notice with at least a 1.4x multiplier for domestic ethanol. No Gazette notice found by mid-Oct 2026. An RFD-TV item dated 3 Oct 2026 said revisions could come "this week"; I could not confirm. Producers fear a delay to 2027; a full CFR review is due in 2027 (OPIS). The Canadian Hydrogen Association asked that any domestic-content rule be fuel-neutral.

### 2b. Biofuel production incentive
- A BC Bioenergy article mentions a temporary biofuel production incentive from 2025 changes; no terms found. Budget 2024 referred to up to C$500 million from the CFR/low-carbon fuel funding to support domestic biofuel production (Argus summary, "C$500mn"). A figure of "$370 million" in my query was not confirmed. Treat the incentive as not verified.

### 2c. Investment tax credits
- Clean Hydrogen ITC: refundable; rates 40% (CI < 0.75 kg CO2e/kg H2), 25% (0.75-<2), 15% (2-<4); halved in 2034, ends after 2034 (EY, IEA; pre-2026 sources). Bill C-15 (Budget 2025 Implementation Act, No. 1) received Royal Assent on 26 March 2026 (Torys). The Budget 2025 extension to methane pyrolysis from 16 Dec 2024 reportedly was NOT in Bill C-15 (Bennett Jones, single source). Labour requirements apply (10-point reduction if not met).
- Clean Technology ITC: 30% for property acquired from 1 Jan 2024, declining to 20% in 2032, 10% in 2033, 5% in 2034 (Doane Grant Thornton, older source).
- Provincial: BC LCFS and others were NOT searched; not found.

## 3. Brazil

### 3a. Fuel of the Future Law (Law 14.993/2024; one source writes 14.933, likely a typo)
- Gasoline ethanol: E30 in force from 1 Aug 2025 (from 27%/27.5%), the legal range allows up to E35 subject to technical feasibility (C2). A proposed E32 stalled: the MME cancelled a CNPE meeting on 11 May 2026 (Brazilian press summaries; status after May 2026 not found).
- Biodiesel: B15 in force from 1 Aug 2025 (C2). The law foresees B16 from March 2026 but it depends on technical feasibility. As of May 2026 (Poder360) B16 was expected to slip to 2027, possibly Aug 2027; tests were to last at least 6 months. Status after May 2026 not found.
- Green diesel (PNDV): CNPE sets annual minimum volumes; level not retrieved.
- RenovaBio CBIOs: 2026 compulsory target 48.09 million CBIOs (CNPE Resolution 21/2025); ANP issued individual distributor targets on 31 March 2026 (Mayer Brown, summary). Mechanism: tradable decarbonisation credits (CBIO) by fuel distributors.
- Natural gas decarbonisation programme: CNPE set the 2026 target at 0.5%, below the 1% legal floor, on 1 April 2026 (Mayer Brown).
- ProBioQAV (SAF): GHG-reduction target for operators on domestic flights, 1% in 2027 rising gradually to 10% in 2037 (C2). It is a GHG target, not a blend percentage. Decree 13.094 of 12 Aug 2026 regulates it (single source, S&P/Mayer Brown via search summary): book-entry certificates (CS-SAF) issued per SAF volume sold, CI calculated by RenovaBio or CORSIA methodology. ANAC draft MRV resolution open for consultation to 1 Oct 2026; ANP and ANAC rules due by 18 Dec 2026. Interaction with CORSIA (mandatory phase from 2027) unresolved. S&P headline: Petrobras SAF plant delays deepen as the 2027 mandate looms (title only).
- COVERAGE: ethanol (road petrol), biodiesel and green diesel (road diesel), SAF (aviation). Obligated: fuel distributors/producers (CBIOs), aircraft operators (ProBioQAV).

### 3b. Hydrogen
- Law 14.948/2024 (2 Aug 2024): legal framework; low-carbon hydrogen defined as up to 7 kg CO2e per kg H2 lifecycle; creates Rehidro (PIS/Pasep and Cofins relief for producers and equipment) (C2).
- Law 14.990/2024: PHBC, tax credits on low-carbon hydrogen sales 2028-2032, up to R$18.3 billion, distributed through a competitive process (Mattos Filho, Mayer Brown, PwC; C2).
- Decree 13.096/2026 (Sept 2026, Agência Gov headline) regulates the National Hydrogen Policy, certification system, Rehidro and PHBC. Local-content minimums: 15% electrolysers, 40% other production routes, up to 60% for distribution/transport equipment (single source, via search summary). First PHBC auction: conflicting. NeoFeed says the last months of 2026; an Eixos headline says it slips to 2027 per the Finance Ministry (headline only). 
- Interaction with biofuels: not found (no source on whether PHBC stacks with RenovaBio or ProBioQAV).

## 4. Argentina
- Law 27.640 (2021): mandatory blends bioethanol 12% in petrol; biodiesel in diesel 7.5% restored in January 2026 by Resolution 612/2026 after a 0.5-point cut in November 2025 (Safras, single source). The law sets later increases between 18% and 27% to be proposed to Congress (art. 8). Biofuels exempt from the liquid fuels and carbon taxes (art. 22). The USDA reportedly said voluntary petrol blends up to 15% are allowed (single source, via summary).
- Pending: the Senate gave half-sanction on 17 Sept 2026 (41-25) to a bill that would repeal Law 27.640, raise bioethanol 12% to 15% within 12 months and biodiesel (7% or 7.5% per sources, which differ on the starting point) to 10%, deregulate prices, set a transition to 2036 and a floor for non-integrated SMEs (C2: Los Andes, Palabras del Derecho, iProfesional, c5n titles). It still needs the Chamber of Deputies. Not law as of the sources seen.
- Hydrogen: not searched; not found.
