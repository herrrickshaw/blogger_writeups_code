# Biofuel + hydrogen policy notes 03: Asia-Pacific (as of 14 Oct 2026)

Method note: 25 WebSearch calls (standard). Every claim below comes from search-result summaries; NO page was opened (WebFetch not used). So "confidence" is limited: "2+" = two or more independent results agreed; "single" = one result; "unverified" = not confirmed. Many results are trade press, exam-prep or blog sites; official PIB/METI/MNRE texts were not read.

## INDIA (brief; blog already covers in depth)
- Ethanol: E20 reached nationwide in 2025-26 (PIB doc says blending rose from <1.5% in 2013-14 to 20% in 2025-26). Sources differ on framing ("5 years ahead of schedule" vs "ahead of revised timeline"); one says E20 mandatory nationwide since April 2026. BIS standards for E22/E25/E27/E30 reportedly notified May 2026 (one report gives 15 May 2026). AFTER E20: only proposals. A Kotak Neo report says E21 for 2027 and E25 for 2029 are being evaluated "subject to scientific validation"; July 2026 explainer: E25/E30/E100 "remain a proposal". No E27/E30 mandate found. Confidence: E20 = 2+; post-E20 = single/proposal.
- National Policy on Biofuels: 2018; amended June 2022 (E20 moved from 2030 to 2025-26; wider feedstocks) (IEA policy page, summary). Biodiesel target 5% by 2030 is from a secondary site only; actual biodiesel blend ~0.6% in 2025-26 (same site). Unverified vs policy text.
- SAF: indicative targets 1% (2027) and 2% (2028) for international flights, announced 25 Nov 2023 by the Petroleum Ministry after NBCC meeting. A Parliament answer cited 5% by 2030 (single). Indicative, not a legal mandate per summaries; airports for SAF blending infrastructure not yet identified (Tribune). No domestic-flight target found.
- CBG/CBO (Compressed Biogas Blending Obligation): voluntary to FY2024-25, mandatory from FY2025-26: 1% of CNG/PNG (FY26), 3% (FY27), 4% (FY28), 5% from FY2028-29. Confidence: 2+ (Tribune, Vikaspedia, Autocar Pro, NaturalGasWorld seen).
- Hydrogen: National Green Hydrogen Mission (2023): 5 Mt/yr by 2030, Rs 19,744 crore (Rs 197.44 bn) outlay (one source). SIGHT production incentive: reported as up to Rs 50/kg in year 1 tapering to zero over 3 years (one source gives Rs 26-50/kg range); this conflicts/is unclear. Reported auction results: Rs 49.75/kg green ammonia (as written in summary; likely Rs/kg ammonia) and Rs 279/kg green hydrogen (refinery lots); 8.62 lakh t/yr capacity incentives awarded (headline, psuwatch). Green hydrogen consumption obligation for refineries/fertiliser plants: NOT found as notified. Only 2021-22 drafts seen (e.g. 0.15% 2023-24 rising toward 10% by 2029-30) plus one blog claiming FY2025-26 ranges (refineries 0.2-2%, fertiliser 0.08-0.46%), unverified. Treat as proposed/unverified.
- Interaction: no stacking rules found. Status flags: post-E20 blends unmandated; GH2 obligation unnotified (as far as found).

## INDONESIA
- Biodiesel (FAME, volumetric mandate on diesel suppliers; palm-based): B40 in force since 2025. B50 timeline contradictory in 2026: Jan 2026 government said B50 postponed, B40 kept through 2026 and export levy raised; March 2026 Argus: President Prabowo said B50 this year; June 2026 reports (Hydrocarbon Processing, Petromindo, RRI) say B50 launch 1 July 2026. I found NO confirmation it actually started or that the extra biodiesel quota was allocated (a May report said quota not yet provided). Flag: status uncertain.
- Funding: BPDP (palm fund) financed by CPO export levy; CPO levy raised from 10% to 12.5% from 1 March 2026 (refined products +2.5 pts). BPDP collected Rp 17.4 trn Jan-May vs Rp 26.84 trn full-year target (64%). Subsidy burden rises with B50 (IDN Financials). Confidence: levy = 2+ (Petromindo, Hydrocarbon Processing); BPDP figures = single.
- Bioethanol: E10 mandate targeted for 2027, E20 in 2028-29 (energy ministry, Sept 2026 per summary); needs ~1.2 million KL ethanol next year vs ~70,500 KL domestic; industry estimate deficit 3.5 million KL/yr; imports likely; earlier statements said "no later than 2028". At risk of slipping. Confidence: 2+ (Antara, Kontan, S&P).
- SAF and hydrogen strategy: NOT searched/found. 
- Hydrogen link: HVO/co-processing not covered by FAME mandate; no hydrogen incentive found.

## CHINA
- Fuel ethanol/biodiesel domestic mandates: NOT found in searches (gap). 
- UCO: 13% export VAT rebate on UCO and certain modified fats/oils cancelled from 1 Dec 2024 (MOF/STA announcement 15 Nov 2024). Jan-May 2026 UCO exports 1,367,468 t (+36.8% y/y); May 310,000 t (+61%); biodiesel exports May 38,601 t (-37% y/y), YTD +12.3% (Fastmarkets). Confidence: 2+ (USDA via S&P/Hellenic; Fastmarkets).
- SAF: no binding national mandate found. 15th FYP (2026-30, approved March 2026) names SAF but sets no blending target. Industry expects 2-5% by 2030; pilot airports (Beijing, Shanghai, Chengdu, Zhengzhou) discussed. 2024 pilot/targets (2% by 2025, 15% by 2030 per one summary; CAAC 50,000 t cumulative by 2025) not shown to be mandatory. SAF export pilot from April 2025: 3 producers, 828,000 t/yr quota, later ~1.2 Mt/yr. Confidence: no-mandate = 2+; targets = single/unverified.
- Hydrogen: Renewable Energy Development Plan for 15th FYP reportedly puts hydrogen in non-fossil system with 2 Mt/yr renewable hydrogen by 2030 (metal.com, July 2026, trade press). Policy reportedly shifting from subsidies to demand-side mandates/blending standards (ammonia co-firing, H2 steel, green methanol for shipping). March 2026: three ministries launched pilot rewarding five city clusters for hydrogen targets (steel focus). No per-kg subsidy found. Confidence: single/trade press.
- Interaction: UCO rebate removal pushes feedstock to domestic SAF/HVO; no hydrogen-credit stacking info.

## JAPAN
- Ethanol: government plan (Nov 2024) E10 by 2030, E20 by 2040; METI action plan discussed June 2025 (advancedbiofuelsusa, single; 2026 status not checked).
- SAF: long-stated target 10% of aviation fuel by 2030 (target, not law). NEW (Aug 2026, S&P Global summary): METI subcommittee proposed 17 Aug 2026, Natural Resources and Fuel Committee approved 24 Aug, a supply obligation of 1% in FY2030-31, 3% FY2031-32, 5% per year FY2032-33 to FY2034-35, on suppliers of >=3,000 kl/yr jet fuel for international flights at seven designated airports; relief only for unavoidable circumstances. This is far below the 10%-by-2030 headline and weaker than an earlier GHG-based proposal. Legal vehicle not identified. Confidence: single (S&P summary); status = approved by committee, not yet shown as in force.
- Hydrogen: Hydrogen Society Promotion Act in force 23 Oct 2024; JPY 3 trillion price-gap CfD (15 years of support, then 10 years mandatory supply; base price vs reference price, typically LNG/coal CIF) plus hub support (FY2025 budget JPY 5.7 bn for FEED). Targets up to 3 Mt/yr hydrogen (incl. ammonia) by 2030, ~12 Mt/yr by 2040. 27 applications by March 2025 deadline; six projects selected as of March 2026 (METI/APERC presentation summary); JERA and Mitsui blue ammonia (Louisiana) certified Dec 2025, ~65% of pool left (Wood Mackenzie). Confidence: 2+.

## SOUTH KOREA
- SAF: mandate for departing international flights from 2027 at 1%; 3-5% by 2030 and 7-10% by 2035 (final 2030/2035 targets to be confirmed in 2026 and 2029); penalties initially deferred (one source: 1.5x price gap x shortfall); from 2028 airlines to use SAF blend for >=90% of annual uplift at Korean airports (single source for the 90% item). Confidence: 2+ for 1% from 2027 (Argus, ESG Today, OFI, ResourceWise); date of Argus piece unclear.
- Hydrogen: Clean Hydrogen Power Bidding Market (CHPS). 2024 round offered 6,500 GWh/yr, only 750 GWh/yr awarded (Korea Southern Power, ammonia-coal co-firing, single bidder). 2026 round scaled back: 500 GWh/yr clean hydrogen and 930 GWh/yr general hydrogen; operation 2029/2028; coal-ammonia co-firing excluded; bidding expected H2 2026 (S&P, June 2026). Clean hydrogen certification threshold <=4 kg CO2e/kg H2 (under Hydrogen Act). 2026 results not found. Confidence: 2+ for design; results unverified.

## SINGAPORE
- SAF levy (CAAS): applies to tickets sold from 1 April 2026 for flights departing on/after 1 Oct 2026; covers O-D passengers, O-D cargo, general and business aviation (not transit). Sized to fund the 1% SAF target for 2026 (target = 1% in 2026, per CAAS statement). Economy/premium (premium = 4x economy): Band 1 SE Asia S$1 / S$4; Band 2 NE Asia, South Asia, Australia, PNG S$2.80 / S$11.20; Band 3 Europe, Africa, Middle East S$6.40 / S$25.60; Band 4 Americas S$10.40 / S$41.60. Earlier estimates were higher (S$3/6/16 for Bangkok/Tokyo/London). Mechanism: levy pays for airline-side SAF procurement, not a fuel-supplier mandate. A safinvestor headline says "April 2026" (it is the sales start). 2030 target (3-5%) NOT verified. Confidence: 2+ (NST, ESG Today, Biofuels News, Asian Aviation).

## AUSTRALIA
- Hydrogen Production Tax Incentive (HPTI): legislated (Future Made in Australia (Production Tax Credit and Other Measures) Act); A$2/kg renewable hydrogen, for production 1 Jul 2027 to 30 Jun 2040, up to 10 years per facility, min 10 MW electrolyser, FID before 1 Jul 2030 for later-approved facilities; run by CER and ATO (ATO guidance updated 10 Sep 2026). Grid-matching instrument commenced 29 July 2026 (Andersen). Confidence: 2+.
- Hydrogen Headstart: up to A$2.25 bn to 2038-39; Round 1: Murchison A$814 m, Hunter Valley Hydrogen Hub A$432 m; Round 2 up to A$1 bn reaffirmed in 2026-27 Budget, outcomes expected late 2026 to early 2027 (IEA policy page).
- Cleaner Fuels Program: A$1.1 bn, ten-year production-linked incentives for low carbon liquid fuels (SAF, renewable diesel); design to be finalised mid-2026, applications expected 2026-27; per-litre rate NOT found. 
- SAF: no mandate yet. DCCEEW consultation opened 18 Aug 2026 on a demand mechanism for low carbon liquid fuels: volumetric targets for fuel suppliers 2029-2035, then carbon-intensity targets; shortfalls met by credit surrender or buy-out price. 2024 Aviation White Paper had said mandates not before mid-2028. Fuel-security debate cited after Iran-war disruption (single/ trade press). Fuel Quality Standards: only a mention of an existing renewable diesel standard; not explored. Status: proposed.
