## making-plastic-releases-heat-unmaking -> out/update-2026-10-28-making-plastic-releases-heat-unmaking.html
- Instruments: Plastic Waste Management Rules EPR; "19 January 2026 notification"; CPCB notice 28 Nov 2025.

- CORRECTION: the amendment redefines "end of life disposal" (includes waste-to-oil) but excludes conversion to

  category for pyrolysis returning feedstock; paragraph and proposal corrected. The amendment's history note lists
  G.S.R. 73(E) of 23 Jan 2025 as the previous amendment, so the 19 Jan 2026 instrument was not a Rules amendment
  (now described as a CPCB notice, still unseen).
