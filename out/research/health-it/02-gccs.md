## Notes per question

### 1. Count, headcount and share
- No primary NASSCOM-Zinnov 2025 table giving a clean count of HLS GCCs was found. The best reconstruction is **~80+ HLS GCCs and ~250k staff in 2023, rising to ~300k staff (≈15% of ~1.9 mn GCC jobs) in 2025**. Both figures are secondary.
- EY (Sep 2025) counts only top-50 global life-sciences firms: **23 of 50** have India GCCs. That is a floor for pharma/medtech, not a total count.
- One search summary credited a "Zinnov October 2026" piece with the "nearly half of top 50" line. That appears to be the EY Sep 2025 finding, misattributed. Use EY.
- Zinnov-AmCham's healthcare GCC whitepaper (released 19 May 2025, Telangana Life Sciences Foundation foreword) is the main sector source. Its PDF (https://media.zinnov.com/wp-content/uploads/2025/06/zinnov-amcham-india-healthcare-gccs-telangana-transforming-global-innovation-whitepaper.pdf) was not opened. The summary gave: Hyderabad = 11% of India's GCC population, and AI/ML adoption in healthcare GCCs rose from 65% to 86%.

### 2. Largest centres
- **Optum / UnitedHealth Group India:** the size is unclear. One aggregator says ~40,000 (target 45k by 2027), while Revelio shows 1,376 for one "Optum India" entity, which is clearly partial. UHG careers pages list Hyderabad, Chennai, Bengaluru, Mumbai and Gurugram sites. **No official UHG India headcount was found.** Optum's India brochures give only the global figure (~400k Optum employees).
- **Carelon Global Solutions (Elevance):** ~22.9k India employees (Revelio, Sep 2025, down 17% YoY). Sites are Bengaluru, Hyderabad and Gurugram. It was formerly Legato Health Technologies and was renamed Carelon in Jan 2023. Company pages say "25K+" globally, which suggests India is the large majority of Carelon GS (an inference).
- **Cigna / Evernorth:** Cigna Health Solutions India has been in Bengaluru since 2011. The Evernorth Hyderabad hub opened in 2024 with ~1,700+ staff (aggregator).
- **Walgreens:** its first India GCC, in Chennai (Taramani), was announced in Sep 2026 with ~250 jobs. Walgreens had used Indian vendors and WBA entities before, but this is described as its first GCC.
- **Cardinal Health:** has a Bengaluru GCC (job-aggregator listing only, no headcount).
- **CVS Health, Humana, McKesson:** no India headcount found in this search budget. (Gap.)
- **Novartis:** >9,000 in India (~11% of global), mostly at Hyderabad, its largest hub outside Switzerland (Dec 2025). Novartis had earlier rented Hyderabad space for ~8,000 (Scrip). FiercePharma has reported Novartis shifting jobs to India during global restructuring. One aggregator gives ~3,000 for an "AI & Data Centre," which is probably a sub-unit or outdated.
- **Sanofi:** Hyderabad GCC had ~1,000 staff in Jul 2024. The target was raised to ~4,500 in 2026, making it the largest of Sanofi's four global hubs. Functions: R&D, digital, data analytics, medical affairs, finance, people services, commercial ops. It started in 2019 as a medical-services operation.
- **AstraZeneca:** Chennai GITC, >2,500 India staff. Rs 250 cr (Jul 2024, ~1,300 roles) and Rs 176 cr (Sep 2025). Focus: AI, data analytics, ML, data science, supply-chain analytics.
- **GSK, Pfizer, Abbott, GE HealthCare, Philips, Siemens Healthineers:** not searched within budget. (Gap.)

### 3. What the work is
- **Claims:** live 2026 UHG/Optum Hyderabad job postings for "Claims Associate" describe **claims adjudication, adjustments and rework** against productivity and quality targets. There are also Claims Quality Representative and claims-platform business analyst roles. This directly evidences US claims processing in India (job postings: https://careers.unitedhealthgroup.com/job/22444715/claims-associate-hyderabad-in).
- **Tech, analytics and AI:** the aggregator profile of the Optum centre mentions claims technology and analytics. The AstraZeneca and Sanofi releases list AI/ML, data science, digital and data analytics, and medical affairs. Zinnov-AmCham reports 86% AI/ML adoption among healthcare GCCs.
- **Pharmacovigilance and clinical data management:** **no 2025-26 source found** tying specific headcount to these functions. They are widely known GCC functions, but unverified here.
- **Share of a company's US operations or claims running through India:** **no such disclosure found.** The only proportion found is Novartis' ~11% of global workforce in India. Revelio's "India = 39.2% of Optum workforce" figure appeared in one summary, but that summary judged it inconsistent with the same source's India entity count. Treat it as **unverified; do not use without checking**.

### 4. Policy issues 2025-26
- **US HIRE Act (S.2976):** introduced by Sen. Bernie Moreno, 5 Sep 2025 (some sources say October). It proposes a 25% excise tax (new IRC s.5000E) on payments to foreign persons for services benefiting US consumers, plus non-deductibility. Analysts estimate a 46-58% rise in effective cost (MGO CPA, Treelife); GTRI estimates 30-50%. It explicitly covers intra-group payments, so it hits captive GCCs. **Status: no co-sponsors or hearings reported; no 2026 progress found.** Check congress.gov before writing. Sources: https://www.mgocpa.com/perspective/proposed-hire-act-targets-offshoring-with-25-excise-tax-on-foreign-service-payments/ ; https://policyrisk.com/legislation/S2976
- **US health-data offshoring rules:** CMS has required Medicare Advantage/Part D sponsors to disclose offshore subcontractors and attest to PHI safeguards since 2007 guidance, and India is explicitly "offshore." Plans push the attestation down to providers and vendors (e.g. Johns Hopkins Health Plans, Delta Dental, Medical Mutual forms). An HHS OIG-cited article lists **Wisconsin, Alaska, Ohio and Arizona** Medicaid as prohibiting offshore subcontractors. That list is **undated and possibly outdated**. HIPAA itself does not ban offshoring: transfer runs through business-associate agreements (general knowledge, not searched).
- **India DPDP Rules 2025:** notified 13/14 Nov 2025 (sources differ by a day; PIB doc dated 17 Nov 2025: https://static.pib.gov.in/WriteReadData/specificdocs/documents/2025/nov/doc20251117695301.pdf). Under the Act's s.17 exemption, as described by law firms (AZB & Partners, Jan 2026: https://www.azbpartners.com/bank/the-impact-of-the-dpdp-framework-on-gccs-in-india/), **almost all substantive DPDP obligations do not apply when an Indian entity processes personal data of non-Indian data principals under a contract with a foreign entity.** So US patients' data processed in Indian GCCs falls largely outside India's new regime. Indian employees' data stays covered. Phasing: most obligations commence ~May 2027 (18 months), possibly accelerated to Nov 2026 (ministerial statement; unconfirmed). The "Fourth Schedule" angle was not confirmed.
- No 2025-26 US tariff on services was found. The visa (H-1B fee) issue was not searched.
