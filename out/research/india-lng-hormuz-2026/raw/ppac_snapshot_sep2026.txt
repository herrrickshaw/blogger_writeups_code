## **Petroleum Planning & Analysis Cell**

### (Ministry of Petroleum & Natural Gas)

**(Data for Sept'2026)**

Petroleum Planning & Analysis Cell (PPAC), an attached office of the Ministry of Petroleum & Natural Gas (MoPNG), Government of India, collects and analyses data on the Oil and Gas sector. It disseminates many reports on the Oil & Gas sector to the various stakeholders. The data is obtained from the Public Sector companies, Government agencies as well as the Private companies. Given the ever-increasing demand for energy and transition of energy demand to renewables and Biofuels, Policy makers and Analysts need to be well informed about the updated trends in the Oil & Gas industry.

The Snapshot of Oil & Gas data (Monthly Ready Reckoner), released by PPAC, provides

monthly data/information in a single volume for the latest month and historical time series. The Snapshot of Oil & Gas data is also published on website (www.ppac.gov.in) and is

accessible on mobile app-PPAC.

This publication is a concerted effort by all divisions of PPAC. The cooperation of the Oil and Gas industry is acknowledged for their timely inputs. Total in the tables in the publication may not tally due to rounding off.

Imports of POL products registered a de-growth of 35.6% during September 2026 and 47.7% during April-September FY 2026-27 as compared to the corresponding period of the previous year. Decrease in POL products imports during April- September FY 2026-27 was mainly due to reduction in imports of liquefied petroleum gas (LPG), petcoke and fuel oil (FO) etc.

Crude oil imports registered a growth of 6.6% and 0.8% during September 2026 and April-September FY 2026-27 respectively as compared to the corresponding period of the previous year. As compared to net import bill for Oil & Gas for Sept 2025 of $9.6 billion, the net import bill for Oil & Gas for Sept 2026 was $13.4 billion. Out of which, crude oil imports constitutes $15.4 billion, LNG imports $1.2 billion and the exports were $ 4.8 billion during Sept 2026.

### **Highlights for the month**

Indigenous crude oil and condensate production during September 2026 was 2.2 MMT registering a de-growth of 1.4 % in crude oil and condensate production during September 2026 as compared with the corresponding period of the previous year. Around 78.7 % of Production came from Nomination Fields, 11.6 % from Pre-NELP Fields and 9.5 % from NELP Fields, during September 2026. Total Crude oil processed during September 2026 was 23 MMT, which is 9.2 % higher than September 2025 , out of which PSU/JV refiners processed 15.2 MMT and private refiners processed 7.8 MMT of crude oil. Total indigenous crude oil processed was 2 MMT and total Imported crude oil processed was 21 by all Indian refineries (PSU+JV+PVT). There was a growth of 1.4 % in total crude oil processed in April-September during current Financial Year as compared to same period of previous Financial Year.

The price of Brent Crude averaged $116.77/bbl during Sept'2026 as against $90.84/bbl during Aug'2026 and $68.02/bbl during Sept'2025. The Indian basket crude price averaged $116.09/bbl during Sept'2026 as against $90.19/bbl during Aug'2026 and $69.61/bbl during Sept'2025.

Production of petroleum products was 23.9 MMT during September 2026 , which is 8.7 % higher than September 2025. Out of 23.9 MMT, 23.6 MMT was from refinery production & 0.3 MMT was from fractionator. There was a growth of 0.2 % in production of petroleum products in April-September FY-2026-27(P) as compared to same period of FY 2025-26. Out of total POL production, in September 2026, share of major products including HSD is 40.4 %, MS 17.1 %, Naphtha 5.9 %, ATF 5.5 %, Pet Coke 4.9 %, LPG 5.1 %, and rest is shared by Bitumen, FO/LSHS, LDO, Lubes & others.

Note: Reported data may undergo change on receipt of actuals 2 Snapshot of India's Oil & Gas data - Oct, 2026

Exports of POL products registered a de-growth of 24.6% during September 2026 and de-growth of 18.0% during April-September FY 2026-27 as compared to the corresponding period of the previous year. Decrease in POL products exports during April-September FY 2026-27 were mainly due to decrease in exports of high- speed diesel (HSD), motor-spirit (MS), aviation turbine fuel (ATF) and naphtha etc.

The consumption of petroleum products during April-Sept'26, with a volume of 114.8 MMT, reported a de- growth of 3.3 % compared to the volume of 118.7 MMT during the same period of the previous year. On growth side, Consumption was led by 4.9% growth in HSD, 7.1% growth in MS and 0.6% growth in ATF. De- growth recorded in LPG, Naphtha, Bitumen and Petcoke during the period. The consumption of petroleum products during Sept'26, with a volume of 19.3 MMT, reported a growth of 3.4 % compared to the volume of 18.6 MMT during the same period of the previous year. Robust growth in Motor Spirit (+8.0%), High-Speed Diesel (+5.6%) and Petcoke (+5.2%) during the month outweighed de-growth in LPG (-9.0%) and Naphtha (- 31.0%).

Total Natural Gas Consumption (including internal consumption) for the month of Sep 2026 (P) was 5653 MMSCM which was 6.67% lower than the corresponding month of the previous year. (For this analysis, LNG data source is DGCIS except for Aug'26 and Sep'26 for which data is prorated.

LNG import data for the month of Sep 2026 (as per prorated data) was 2957 MMSCM which was 8.55 % lower than the corresponding month of the previous year.

Ethanol blending in Petrol was 20% during Sept 26 and cumulative ethanol blending during ESY Nov 25-Sept 26 of 20% was achieved.
