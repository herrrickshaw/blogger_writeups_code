Known limits, stated rather than hidden. Acronyms collide, and the map used to fall for them: IIT (ISM) Dhanbad is a mining school and was being read as the India Semiconductor Mission; Sanchar, Paryatan, Nyaya and Monument Mitra are unrelated to PM MITRA textile parks; “AIF” in a Competition Commission order is an Alternative Investment Fund, not the Agriculture Infrastructure Fund; and Krishi Unnati Mela is not UNNATI 2024. Thirty-two such rows were removed on 2026-08-04 by an explicit veto list. Others may remain — a row whose title has nothing to do with its chip (the coloured scheme tag shown in the table) is a mapping error, not a policy fact.
