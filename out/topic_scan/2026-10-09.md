# Update and topic scan, 9 October 2026

**Window:** PIB releases from 20 September to 1 October 2026, taken from
`india-trade-sector-policy-recommendations/data/pib_index.sqlite`, which runs to
1 October. News from 1 to 9 October came from web search, because pib.gov.in is
blocked from the cloud session.

**Method:** substantive releases from the ministries this blog covers were
matched against the text of all 349 archived posts (`posts/`), and each match was
read to see whether the post already reflected the release. Figures in the update
notes come from the PIB release named (by PRID) and the news reports cited
alongside it, because the PIB pages themselves could not be opened from the
session. Check them against the PIB text before publishing.

## 4. New article topics

Ranked by fit with the blog's beats and by whether a primary source exists.
Problem pieces need the "What would fix this" section (CLAUDE.md rule 8).

**A. IIP's new 2022-23 base: what moved when the yardstick changed.** The new
series, from June 2026, covers 1,042 products in 463 item groups (up from 839
items and 407 groups) and adds gas supply, water, sewerage and waste. August
2026 shows +8.0% (manufacturing +9.0%, mining &minus;5.6%). Angle: a data seam,
meaning how the old and new series differ for the same months, and what that does
to growth comparisons. Sources: MoSPI IIP releases (PIB 2315957), MoSPI base-revision note.

**B. ASI 2024-25: factory jobs pass 2 crore, but whose factories?** 2.10 crore
persons engaged (+7.19%), GVA &#8377;26.94 lakh cr (+9.59%), 2,66,931 factories.
Tamil Nadu has the most factories (15.4%), Maharashtra the most GVA (15.9%).
Angle: the state ranking against the IEM register's state follow-through
(Gujarat and UP high, Tamil Nadu low on Part B), plus the gap between factory
count and value. Source: MoSPI ASI 2024-25 (PIB 2316981).

**C. Green Energy Corridor III: India is now paying to move renewable power, not just make it.**
&#8377;1.86 lakh cr for intra-state transmission plus 50 GWh of storage; tariff-based
bidding for greenfield lines, cost-plus for brownfield. Angle: curtailment and
evacuation as the binding constraint; compare Phase II (&#8377;12,031 cr, about 20 GW,
seven states). Source: PIB 2316948.

**D. The Draft Petroleum (Amendment) Bill 2026: decriminalising a 1934 Act.** It
replaces section 23's catch-all offence with specific offences, adds civil
penalties up to &#8377;2.5 cr (&#8377;5 cr for a repeat), and fines up to &#8377;25 cr plus
jail for unlicensed activity. Comments close on **30 October 2026**, so the
piece has a deadline. It pairs with `oisd-writes-indias-oil-safety-rules-api`.
Source: PIB 2316851 and the MoPNG draft.

**E. The smallest wheat MSP rise in 17 years, and the stocks behind it.** The
+&#8377;25 rise (about 1%) against the large rises for safflower and mustard. Angle:
MSP as a stock-management tool; pairs with the FCI and MSP posts. Source: PIB 2316941.

**F. Edible oil: two duty cuts in 16 months.** May 2025 (20%&rarr;10%) and
September 2026 (10%&rarr;5%; crude sunflower nil), against the oilseed mission's
self-sufficiency aim. Angle: the mission and the tariff pull in opposite
directions. Sources: PIB 2314297, SEA import data.

**G. India&ndash;New Zealand FTA in force from 20 October 2026.** Check the dairy
and horticulture schedules first. Source: PIB 2313143.

**H. Offshore minerals, second attempt.** Two Andaman Sea blocks are offered after
the first 13-block tranche, launched in November 2024, was cancelled in December
2025 for lack of bids. Angle: why the first round failed. Sources: PIB 2317207 and 2317680.

**I. Hold for results: CAPEX 2026 survey.** MoSPI's private-capex intentions
survey runs from October to December 2026. When it reports, it is the natural
follow-up to the IEM article (intentions against realisations). Source: PIB 2317606.
