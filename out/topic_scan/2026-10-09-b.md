## 1. New article topics, ranked

**A. EPFO wage ceiling: ₹15,000 to ₹25,000, the first rise in twelve years.**
- **What happened:** the Cabinet approved it on 16 September (PIB 2310811, 2310973). It took
  effect on 17 September, as reported.
- **Who it affects:** about 51 lakh more workers come under mandatory provident fund, pension
  (EPS) and insurance (EDLI) cover.
- **Cost:** the government's annual outgo is put at ₹11,339 crore, against about ₹10,250
  crore now; ₹56,696 crore over five years.
- **History:** the ceiling was raised to ₹15,000 in September 2014 and not again until now.
  The minister said the unskilled minimum wage is above ₹15,000 in many states.
- **Angle:** a ceiling with no indexation rule. Questions to answer:
  - What the change costs an employer, per worker, at ₹20,000 and ₹25,000.
  - How the EPS pension maths changes.
  - How many years minimum wages ran ahead of the ceiling.
- **Data:** EPFO payroll releases, state minimum-wage notifications and the Cabinet release.
- **Fix candidates:**
  - Index the ceiling to a published wage measure.
  - Publish the actuarial note on EPS.
- **Coverage:** no existing post covers this. EPFO appears only in passing in two posts.

**B. Coal: 16 rounds of commercial mine auctions, and how many auctioned mines produce.**
- **What happened:**
  - The 16th round was announced on 16 September (PIB 2310924).
  - "Commercial Coal Mine Auctions" (PIB 2309962) and "Coal Distribution Over the Years: From
    Allocation to Auction" (PIB 2314438) are backgrounders.
- **Angle:** blocks auctioned since 2020 against blocks that have reached production, and
  their output against the 2030 target. The question is the same as in this blog's OALP
  piece: awarded is not producing.
- **Data:** Ministry of Coal auction results and the coal controller's production data.
- **Fix candidates:**
  - A published milestone tracker for each block.
  - Penalties that bite on blocks that miss their schedules.

**C. Steel imports and the safeguard-duty question.**
- **What happened:**
  - The Ministry of Steel held an open house on import issues on 24 September (PIB 2311834).
  - Press reports say finished-steel imports from China hit a seven-year high in April to
    August 2026, and that the ministry favours a temporary safeguard duty. One report says
    25%; the 2025 provisional duty was 12%.
  - Demand stayed strong in April to August (PIB 2314464).
- **Angle:** strong domestic demand and record imports at the same time. Who gains from a
  safeguard (producers) and who pays (MSME users of steel).
- **Data:** JPC import and consumption data, DGTR orders and the 2025 safeguard notification.
- **Caution:** the 25% figure and the open-house details need checking against the DGTR or
  Finance ministry notification first.

**D. The ₹10,000 crore SME Growth Fund and the new Integrated Transport and Logistics
Authority.**
- **What happened:** the Cabinet approved both on 6 October 2026, as reported. The fund was
  announced in the 2026-27 Budget; it makes equity investments through an AIF.
- **Angle:** India's track record with government equity funds of funds.
  - The Startup FoF corpus was ₹10,000 crore, set up in 2016. How much reached companies, and
    how fast?
  - The ₹50,000 crore Self-Reliant India Fund (2020): how much has been deployed?
- **Data:** SIDBI FoF disclosures, Parliament answers and the NSIC Venture Capital Fund.
- **Fix candidate:** publish quarterly deployment by fund.
- **Note:** the ITLA could be a section, or a separate piece once its notification is out.

**E. India's domestic tourism, measured for the first time since 2014-15.**
- **What happened:** MoSPI's Domestic Tourism Expenditure Survey, July 2025 to June 2026,
  part of the NSS 80th round with 2,46,113 households (PIB 2313894, 23 September).
- **Findings so far:**
  - 48 of every 100 overnight trips in the 365-day group are for pilgrimage, and 30 for
    health.
  - Business trips cost the most among the 30-day group, at ₹5,949 per trip.
  - 65% of rural and 46% of urban households made a same-day trip in the past month.
- **Angle:** Indians travel for gods and doctors. What that means for tourism policy, which
  targets leisure and foreign visitors.
- **Caution:** the 2014-15 numbers are not directly comparable, because recall periods
  changed. The press note PDF is on mospi.gov.in, which is blocked here, so it needs a Mac
  download.

**F. Central infrastructure projects: the overrun ledger.**
- **What happened:** MoSPI's flash report on central projects of ₹150 crore and above, for
  August 2026, was released on 26 September (PIB 2314852).
- **Scale:** the April 2026 edition tracked 1,981 projects with ₹5.65 lakh crore of cost
  overruns, as reported.
- **Angle:** the sectors and projects with the longest delays. NHAI's new quality checks on
  "abnormally low" bids (PIB 2317061, 30 September) are a possible tie-in.
- **Data:** cspm.gov.in flash reports. Check they can be reached from the Mac.

**G. India–MERCOSUR: expanding a small trade agreement from 2009.**
- **What happened:** negotiations to expand the PTA were launched, and a protocol on
  electronic certificates of origin was signed on 15 September (PIB 2310166, 2310083).
- **Angle:** about 450 tariff lines on each side today. Trade with Brazil and Argentina, and
  what an expansion would add (soybean oil, which links to the edible oil piece).
- **Data:** DGCI&S country data.
