1. **Bengaluru Rs 120 crore: "annual revenue" -> revenue over about two decades (~Rs 6 crore a year); 550 MLD is sewage across five major STPs, not one plant.** Locations: summary bullet (CBG); section 3 paragraph 1; section 3 note. Source: ETV Bharat, 20 Apr 2026, https://www.etvbharat.com/en/state/bengaluru-set-to-roll-out-india-largest-sewage-to-biogas-project-enn26042003645 ("nearly 550 million litres per day of sewage to generate Rs 120 crore in additional revenue over the next two decades", raw/5/694baf.html). The old text overstated annual revenue by about 20x. Secondary report citing a BWSSB statement; no official release found.
2. **Ghaziabad Dundahera STP:** "already-running ... WABAG-built demonstration yielding roughly 1.8 tonnes of CBG per day" and the derived "0.026 t CBG per MLD" benchmark removed; replaced with: WABAG and PEAK announced a planned Bio-CNG plant (BOT/PPP, 70 MLD STP) in May 2026, not operating, and no output figure in the filing. Location: section 3 paragraph 1 and note. Source: VA Tech WABAG press release filed with NSE, 13 May 2026, https://nsearchives.nseindia.com/corporate/VATECH_13052026154947_SIGBio1352026.pdf (raw/5/wabag.txt: 70 MLD STP in Dundaheda, BOT/PPP; no tonnage). 1.8 t/day is in no source found, so the derived ratio is dropped.
3. **STP effluent standards (section 3, plus summary bullet):** conflict resolved. Confirmed: BOD <10, TSS <20, COD <50, total nitrogen <10 mg/L (total phosphorus <1.0 for ponds/lakes); faecal coliform is "desirable <100, permissible <230 MPN/100 mL", not a flat 100. Source: CPCB compliance report in NGT OA 200/2014 quoting the NGT order of 30 Apr 2019 (greentribunal.gov.in; raw/5/c.txt, OCR line "desirable <100 MPN/100ml & permissible < 230 MPN/100 ml, Nitrogen-Total < 10 mg/l"). Caveat in text: CPCB restating the order; order under Supreme Court challenge; NGT order PDF itself not opened.
4. **Dengue (section 4 + note):** 2025 Tamil Nadu = 23,407 cases / 12 deaths (NCVBDC); 2024 = 27,378 / 13 confirmed. Press figures 15,796 and ~20,866 reported as not matching the full-year official figure. Source: NCVBDC "Dengue Cases and Deaths in the Country 2021-2026 (till April)", https://ncvbdc.mohfw.gov.in/Doc/Dengue-Cases-deaths-2021-2026-till-April.pdf (raw/5/dengue.txt, Tamil Nadu row: 2024 27378/13, 2025 23407/12).
