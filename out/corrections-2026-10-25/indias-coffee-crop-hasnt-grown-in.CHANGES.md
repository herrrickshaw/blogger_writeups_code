## FLAG FOR AUTHOR (argument rewritten)
The post's "update" claimed an 11% single-season jump to 403,000 t (the "first genuinely large year-on-year move") and made it the top "What to watch" item. The Coffee Board's own table shows 403,000 t was a September 2025 post-blossom *forecast*; the *final* 2025-26 estimate is 373,000 t, about +2.6% on 2024-25's 363,500 t and about 7.4% below the forecast. The 373,000 figure the post labelled "second-advance" is the final estimate. The post's headline thesis (crop flat for a decade) is therefore strengthened, not broken, but the "trend break" paragraph, the summary bullet and the What-to-watch bullet were rewritten. Please review the wording.
