Andhra Pradesh cabinet 10 Nov 2026: three technology policies, Rs 36,894 crore of projects

Skip to article content
Text Size

Top line

  • 85.1% of the Rs 36,894.32 crore of investment items cleared on 10 November 2026 is one 512 MW AI data centre (Rs 31,387 crore, 1,000 jobs); the other four items carry 7,134 of the 8,134 jobs.
  • 80% is the combined Centre and State capital support for a silicon fab under the amended semiconductor policy, against 65% in Odisha and Tamil Nadu and 56% in Gujarat on the State’s own comparison; no project approved under it was found.
  • Rs 61.3 crore per MW is the cost of the 512 MW data centre, the lowest of four approved Andhra Pradesh projects, which run to Rs 120.9 crore a MW.

What it means: The cabinet total is dominated by a single data centre that was approved by government order 80 days earlier, so most of the Rs 36,894 crore follows an approval already issued by government order.

What to do: The IT, Electronics and Communications Department should list, for each cabinet investment item, the earlier order or board decision it follows, so that new and repeated approvals can be told apart.

What it means: The approved costs range from Rs 61.3 to Rs 120.9 crore a MW, a spread of 1.97 times, so the headline investment figures are not comparable without knowing what each includes, such as power plants, land and later phases.

What to do: The IT, Electronics and Communications Department should publish, for each data centre order, the capacity in MW of IT load, the items inside the investment figure and the incentive caps in rupees.

What would fix this

  1. IT, Electronics and Communications Department: publish the AVGC-XR and robotics policies and G.O.Ms.No.87 with their eligibility guidelines, and state the semiconductor park area and the policy validity date in one table.
  2. IT, Electronics and Communications Department: set a jobs and investment target for the robotics policy and report realised figures against every target each year.
  3. Andhra Pradesh Economic Development Board: list, for each cabinet investment item, the earlier order or board decision it follows, so that repeat approvals are not counted as new.
  4. IT, Electronics and Communications Department: state capacity as MW of IT load for every data centre order, with the items inside the investment figure and the incentive caps in rupees.
  5. Information and Public Relations Department: release each cabinet decision in one dated note, with the government order number once it is issued.

These are proposals from this blog, not recommendations any agency has adopted.

Method and definitions
  • Dates come from reports of 10 to 19 November 2026 and from the dated order G.O.Ms.No.59; weekdays were checked against the calendar. The 12 November dateline on the semiconductor report does not fit the Wednesday release or the order of 16 November, and is flagged rather than corrected.
  • Sums, ratios, per-job and per-MW figures are computed here from the figures shown, and each is checked by an assertion in the builder; the semiconductor percentages were checked for consistency (40 plus 40, 9 plus 4.5, 30 plus 18, 15 plus 7.5, and the rupee caps in crore against tele.net’s million and billion).
  • A target is a figure for a policy period; an outcome is something that has happened. No outcome under the three policies was found.
  • Page text was read for The New Indian Express, Deccan Chronicle, Telugu360, GKToday, BusinessLine and tele.net. The Hindu, Times of India, ETV Bharat, Hans India (a feature article), MediaNama, Economic Times and the copy of G.O.Ms.No.59, hosted by a third party, were read as search excerpts. News sites are secondary.
  • The Renew AP project figures are inferred from totals printed by MediaNama and The New Indian Express and are labelled as such.
What this piece could not establish
  • The text of the AVGC-XR policy, the robotics policy and G.O.Ms.No.87. The incentive terms for AVGC-XR come from one wire report and the Times of India excerpt; the robotics incentives were not found.
  • Any investment or jobs target for the robotics policy itself. The Gudipalli park figures come from a feature article, not from the order.
  • The park area for semiconductors: about 5,000 acres at Tirupati (BusinessLine, tele.net) against a 495-acre park at Reddipalle (Hans India). Whether they are one park or two was not established.
  • Whether the five-year validity of the amended semiconductor policy runs from 2026 or ends with the policy period in 2029.
  • The correct dateline of the semiconductor release (12 November on the page, 16 November for the order, Wednesday 18 November for the release).
  • The incentive paragraphs of G.O.Ms.No.59 and what the 512 MW project’s Rs 31,387 crore includes; the cabinet reports do not say whether the 10 November item added anything to the August order.
  • The Renew AP project’s own capacity and cost, which are inferred, and how the 25 acres approved on 10 November for Reliance relate to up to 854.97 acres in the July order.
  • Operating data centre capacity in Andhra Pradesh, Andhra Pradesh’s present AVGC-XR employment, the source of the 20 lakh professionals figure, and any realised investment or jobs under any of the three policies.
