## Policy figures the posts already agree on (reuse, do not restate differently)
- PM E-DRIVE e-2W incentive: Rs 2,500 a kWh, capped at Rs 5,000 a vehicle; extended to 31 March 2028 by S.O. 4424(E) of 10 August 2026 after about ten days with no subsidy; e-2W allocation Rs 2,767 crore (up from Rs 1,772 crore); claims by 31 December 2027; total outlay Rs 11,900 crore (up from Rs 10,900 crore).
- Chain: S.O. 4259(E) of 29 Sep 2024 notified the scheme; S.O. 3626(E) of 7 Aug 2025 extended it to 31 March 2028 but kept 31 March 2026 for e-2Ws and e-3Ws; S.O. 1617(E) of 27 March 2026 moved the e-2W end date to 31 July 2026, kept e-rickshaws and e-carts to 31 March 2028 and recorded the **L5 e-3W segment as closed on 26 December 2025**.
- Relevance: the L5 three-wheeler incentive had already closed, yet e-3W penetration stayed far above e-2W (30.5% in FY2025-26; 42.9% for L5 passenger three-wheelers in Jan-Jul 2026 per EVreporter). That supports the live post's "tipping point on unsubsidised economics" claim and should be cited, not contradicted.

## Points where the new post could conflict
1. **e-2W share of EV volume**: live post says 58% (FY2025-26); Mercom 61% (Q1 2026), EVreporter 62.14% (Jan-Jul 2026), Ticker 63.2% (October 2026). Different periods and scopes (low-speed e-2Ws, Telangana); the new post should show the share rising and say which series each number belongs to.
2. **October 2026 total**: live post uses about 3.54 lakh (Vahan via Times of India); Outlook Business (also citing The Times of India) says about 2.34 lakh. State the conflict; do not blend.
3. **e-2W penetration**: September 11.6% (FADA, live post) against Ticker's October 9.65%; likely a scope difference, not a fall.
4. **e-rickshaws**: whether the "e-3W" figure includes them differs by source; the government-fleets post says the overall figure is dominated by e-rickshaws. Say how Vahan classes them.
5. **PM E-DRIVE total**: the localisation post's chart still shows the original Rs 10,900 crore with a note that it is now Rs 11,900 crore; use Rs 11,900 crore and mention the original only as history.
