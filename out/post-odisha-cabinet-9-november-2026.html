Odisha cabinet 9 Nov 2026: Rs 4,708.64 crore pumped storage, college Wi-Fi, paddy purchase

Skip to article content
Text Size

Top line

  • 84.8% of the Rs 5,551.83 crore of listed project outlays approved on 9 November 2026 is one 600 MW pumped storage plant at Upper Indravati (Rs 4,708.64 crore, Rs 7.85 crore a MW), the state’s first.
  • Rs 1 crore is the gap between the Chief Secretary’s construction figure (Rs 3,371.88 crore) and the contract price in other reports (Rs 3,372.88 crore); an earlier state approval put the project at Rs 3,394.48 crore, and no report explains the difference.
  • Rs 3,100 a quintal is the paddy price for kharif 2026-27, Rs 659 above the reported support price of Rs 2,441, for a target of 78 lakh tonnes; the policy was approved eight days after procurement began on 1 November.

  3. 27 Jul 2026OHPC issues the letter of intent for the engineering, procurement and construction contract to Megha Engineering and Infrastructures (OHPC, excerpt).

What it means: The cost per MW is Rs 7.85 crore, 15.6% above the proposals pending in the state, and a Rs 1 crore difference between two reported construction figures and the earlier lower approval of Rs 3,394.48 crore are not explained.

What to do: The Energy Department and OHPC should publish the project cost by head, with the GST, land, interest during construction and contingency lines, and state how the earlier Rs 3,394.48 crore approval relates to the new total.

What would fix this

  1. Energy Department and OHPC: publish the Upper Indravati cost by head and reconcile Rs 4,708.64 crore, Rs 3,371.88 crore, Rs 3,372.88 crore and the earlier Rs 3,394.48 crore in one note.
  2. Energy Department: state whether the Upper Indravati plant will bid for the Union viability gap funding approved on 4 November, and how that would change the state loan.
  3. Higher Education Department: publish the list of institutions covered by Shikshanet, the cost per institution and the phase I usage and uptime.
  4. Food Supplies and Consumer Welfare Department: publish the paddy support price and state input assistance as separate lines, with the 2025-26 procurement result by district.
  5. Information and Public Relations Department: release each cabinet note with the amount, source of funds and department for all 13 proposals.

These are proposals from this blog, not recommendations any agency has adopted.

Method and definitions
  • The meeting date is Monday 9 November 2026 (PTI, Swarajya); weekdays were checked against the calendar. The Union Cabinet date of 4 November is taken from this blog’s earlier piece on the storage scheme and was not re-read.
  • Per-MW, per-institution, per-user and percentage figures, the 80:20 split of the cost and the paddy purchase value are computed here from the figures shown, and each is checked by an assertion in the builder. The paddy purchase value assumes the whole 78 lakh tonnes is bought at Rs 3,100 a quintal.
  • A target is a figure for a plan or a season; an outcome is something that has happened. No outcome under any of the three main decisions was found.
  • Every source was read as a search excerpt; no cabinet note, project report or policy text was found. News sites, a farm-advice site and a regional outlet are secondary; the Odisha Chief Minister’s page, OHPC, the Odisha policy and the Lok Sabha reply are official.
  • The sub-total in the Lok Sabha reply was in a garbled excerpt and is reported but not used.
What this piece could not establish
  • The cabinet notes, the Upper Indravati project report and the procurement policy text. The thirteenth proposal was not identified.
  • Why the Chief Secretary’s construction figure (Rs 3,371.88 crore) is Rs 1 crore below the contract price in four other reports (Rs 3,372.88 crore), and what the earlier Rs 3,394.48 crore approval covered.
  • The correct land requirement (151.916 or 164 hectares) and whether the 1,040 MU belongs to the new plant, as OHPC states, or the existing station, as Business Standard says.
  • Whether the plant will get the Union viability gap funding or the state loan terms as reported; the Union release was not re-read for this piece.
  • The Shikshanet coverage count (601, 731 or over 750 institutions) and any usage or cost result from phase I.
  • The paddy support price of Rs 2,441 from a primary source, who bears the Rs 659 and the support-price part of the Rs 3,100, and procurement results for 2025-26: the Lok Sabha annexure excerpt was garbled.
  • What price the farmers who sold between 1 and 9 November received, and what the fair price dealer extension covers: PTI and the Chief Minister’s page describe it differently.
  • Any construction progress, college connected, or tonne of paddy bought under these approvals.
