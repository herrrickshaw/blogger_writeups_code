## Findings
1. On 9 Nov 2026 MOFCOM and the General Administration of Customs published Announcement No. 58 of 2026. Sources: [S&P Global, 9 Nov 2026](https://www.spglobal.com/energy/en/news-research/latest-news/metals/110926-china-extends-rare-earth-export-suspension-to-january-2027); [CNA, 9 Nov 2026](https://www.channelnewsasia.com/east-asia/china-extends-suspensions-new-rare-earths-controls-6535736); MOFCOM text as cited by the [Sanctions Expert feed](https://feed.sanctionsexpert.com/feed/item/347-mofcom-and-customs-extend-suspension-of-six-export-control-announcements-until-january-10) (mofcom.gov.cn/zwgk/zcfb/art/2026/art_91466027f465467985942a639a9485f4.html; primary page not opened).

3. The 10 Jan 2027 end date matches the extended China-US trade arrangement (S&P Global).
