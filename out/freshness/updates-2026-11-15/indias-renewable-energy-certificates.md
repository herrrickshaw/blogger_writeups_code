# indias-renewable-energy-certificates: checked 15 Nov 2026

**Status: confirmed that carbon trading has not started; the post expects compliance trading by October 2026.**

## Findings
1. CERC notified the Carbon Credit Certificate trading regulations on 27 Feb 2026. BEE published a draft detailed transaction procedure on 19 Oct 2026 for 15 days of comments (comments due 24 Nov 2026 per BEE's notice). Source: [BEE notice](https://www.beeindia.gov.in/WriteReadData/RTF1984/RTF-PDF-20510f1d3ee6d4ba_1792664827.pdf).
2. On 23 Oct 2026 CERC heard petitions from IEX, PXIL and HPX for approval to trade carbon credit certificates. Source: [CERC record of proceedings, Petition 453/MP/2026](https://cercind.gov.in/2026/ROP/453-MP-2026-Ors_231026.pdf). A trade-press summary says CERC listed the matter next for 12 Jan 2027 and found BEE's detailed procedure not yet issued ([C-Square Carbon, 10 Nov 2026](https://www.csquarecarbon.com/post/buy-carbon-credit-certificates-power-exchange-india-2026); secondary).
3. No trade had been reported as of 6 Nov 2026 and no price band was published ([International Carbon Trade tracker](https://internationalcarbontrade.com/blogs/indian-carbon-market/), secondary).
4. About 490 obligated entities are in emissions verification, with first certificate issuance expected next ([S&P Global, 21 Oct 2026](https://www.spglobal.com/energy/en/news-research/latest-news/energy-transition/102126-indian-carbon-market-advances-verification-as-first-credit-issuance-nears)).

## Not confirmed
- The REC prices for October 2026 (no exchange page opened) and CERC REC regulation amendments.
- A BEE director's "end of calendar 2027" trading date: Business Standard attributes that remark to a period before 28 Feb 2026, so it is not new.

## Proposed dated note
<p><strong>Update, 15 November 2026:</strong> Compliance trading in carbon credit certificates had not started. The Bureau of Energy Efficiency published a draft transaction procedure on 19 October, with comments due by 24 November, and on 23 October the Central Electricity Regulatory Commission took up petitions from IEX, PXIL and HPX to trade the certificates. No start date has been set.
