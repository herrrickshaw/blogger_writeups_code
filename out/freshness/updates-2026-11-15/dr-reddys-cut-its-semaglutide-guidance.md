# dr-reddys-cut-its-semaglutide-guidance: checked 15 Nov 2026

**Status: partly confirmed.** The post expected testing to finish around the third week of September with supply resuming from November.

## Findings
1. Q2 FY27 results, 23 Oct 2026: revenue Rs 8,671 crore, down 1.5% year on year. The company's results deck says it is "on track to resolve" the semaglutide API out-of-specification issue and lists approvals in ten countries. Sources: [NSE investor presentation, 23 Oct 2026](https://nsearchives.nseindia.com/corporate/DRREDDY_23102026173751_SEintimation_Investor_presentation_23102026_1.pdf); [SEC 6-K results release](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1135951/000157587226000725/rdy0952_ex99-2.htm).
2. On the results call management said commercial supply resumption in November 2026 remains planned, subject to validation of the finished product; most first quantities go to Canada, some to Latin America and Africa. Pen volume guidance stays at 6-7 million in FY27. Sources: [Hindu BusinessLine, 23 Oct 2026](http://www.thehindubusinessline.com/companies/dr-reddys-q2-fy27-net-down-36-at-925-cr/article71616661.ece); [ET Pharma, 24 Oct 2026](https://pharma.economictimes.indiatimes.com/news/financial-performance/dr-reddys-set-to-resume-semaglutide-supplies-in-q3-after-production-disruption/135159779) (transcript summary, not filed text).
3. The July disclosure provision of Rs 2,397 million is the "Rs 240 crore" in the post (Q1 release; deck footnote).
