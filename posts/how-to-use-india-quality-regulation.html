Why it is useful

  • Compliance, policy and procurement readers who ask 'which body actually certifies this product?' get one place that separates the Act, the statutory regulator, the accreditation apex and the mark on the pack.
  • It replaces hunting across a dozen ministry sites: every entry names the authority, legal basis, whether the mark is mandatory or voluntary, and links the mirrored primary source.
  • The run showed the catalogue is internally consistent: the reference checker resolved 144 of 144 source references and the compendium verifier passed all five checks.
  • A quick tally over the entry headers shows the shape of the system: 13 mandatory regimes, 12 voluntary, 6 mixed and 5 accreditation-infrastructure bodies, so 'BIS' is only one of many gatekeepers.

What it is for

india-quality-regulation is a documentation repository, not an application. docs/ holds one page per body (for example BIS Hallmarking, FSSAI, CDSCO, BEE Star Label, Legal Metrology, NABL), grouped into ten categories. Each page has a header block (Authority, Status, Established, Legal basis, Marks issued, Source tier) followed by Scope, Notes and a Primary source section.

Three small Python scripts keep the catalogue honest: generate_coverage.py rebuilds the coverage table from the 'Source tier' field, check_source_refs.py checks every page's source links point at mirrored files, and verify_compendium.py checks the single-file COMPENDIUM.md against docs/.

Who it is for

  • Policy analysts and journalists tracing who regulates a product category
  • Importers, start-ups and MSMEs working out which certification regime applies
  • Students of regulatory design and law
  • Anyone building a regulator knowledge base who wants a sourced starting point
Not for.
  • Legal advice: this is not legal advice; confirm requirements against the current gazette and the regulator's own site
  • Current-status checking: Quality Control Orders and scheme rules change often, so the pages are a snapshot
  • Downloading Indian Standards: the paid IS texts are catalogued, not included

Quick start: commands and what you should see

Rebuild the coverage table and entry badge from the page headers

python3 scripts/generate_coverage.py

Wrote docs/coverage.md: 36 entries (22 statutory text, 14 official page). Then: Wrote badges/entries.json: message='36 bodies & schemes'. Git shows no diff, so the committed table was already in sync.

Check every source reference in the doc pages resolves to a mirrored file

python3 scripts/check_source_refs.py

144 source references checked, all resolve.

Verify the single-file compendium matches docs/

python3 scripts/verify_compendium.py --check all

Five OK lines: headings, fields, sections, anchors, coverage counts.

Sample prompts for your AI assistant

Copy, paste and edit the parts in [brackets].

  • Using COMPENDIUM.md only, which Indian bodies are involved in certifying [product, e.g. a children's toy or a gold chain], and which marks are mandatory?
  • Explain the four-layer model in docs/00-overview.md in plain English with one worked example.
  • List every entry whose Source tier is 'Official page' rather than statutory text and explain why that is acceptable for each.
  • Compare FSSAI and CDSCO: legal basis, who must hold the licence, and what the mark looks like. Cite the page you used.
  • I want to add [regulator] to this repo. Draft the docs page in the same format and tell me which script to run afterwards.

Limits and honest caveats

  • Not legal advice. Check the current gazette notification and the regulator's own site before relying on any requirement.
  • The repo is a snapshot: the latest commit is 24 September 2026, and Quality Control Orders, scheme rules and district rollouts change frequently.
  • The reference checker proves that links point to mirrored files; it does not prove the mirrored text is the latest amendment.
  • Per the README, some .gov.in / .nic.in hosts were unreachable from the author's environment, so a few entries rely on secondary mirrors; each page's Notes section records what was tried.
  • I did not run extract_pdf_text.py or the PDF-extraction verifier (they need poppler and the PDF sources).
  • Individual IS standards are paid BIS products and are listed, not included.
