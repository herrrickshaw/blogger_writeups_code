India's competitive government tenders and Production Linked Incentive (PLI) schemes almost always end the same way: a Press Information Bureau release naming exactly who won, for how much, and where. There is no single portal that aggregates these across ministries — each release stands alone, and finding them means knowing which ministry ran which scheme and reading the actual release text rather than trusting the headline. This piece does that reading for nine categories spanning eight ministries, going back to 2019 where the trail is verifiable. Two rules throughout: every company name below comes from text this piece actually read, not a title or an inference; and where a process is still open, that's stated plainly rather than papered over.

Critical Mineral Recycling Incentive Scheme — PENDING, no names published

A ₹1,500 crore scheme under the National Critical Mineral Mission (Cabinet-approved 3 September 2025). Application window closed 1 April 2026; evaluated by JNARDDC as Project Management Agency. As of 29 April 2026, 58 companies (20 + 38 in two clearance batches) were declared eligible, pledging ~850 KTPA capacity and ~₹5,000 crore investment — but the release fetched for this piece gives only the count, not individual names.

Source: PIB PRID 2256977. Related lead not independently verified: PRID 2277041 (56 critical mineral blocks + 11 exploration licence blocks auctioned, Jun 2026).

Inter-state transmission projects (Ministry of Power)

Tendered via REC Power Development and Consultancy Limited under the Tariff Based Competitive Bidding (TBCB) route.

PIB PRID 2005066 (11 Feb 2024) named the successful bidders for a set of ISTS transmission projects as Power Grid Corporation of India Limited (PGCIL), the consortium of Indigrid 2 Limited & Indigrid 1 Limited, and Apraava Energy Private Limited; five project SPVs were handed over 9 February 2024. The same release separately named Avaada Energy Private Limited and Juniper Green Energy Private Limited as Letter-of-Award recipients for a 100 MW wind-power supply contract to Damodar Valley Corporation.

What this sweep does not cover

This is not an exhaustive census of every Indian government tender ever awarded — it's what surfaced from a systematic but time-bounded search of a local PIB index (2017 to date) across ministries known to run competitive, publicly-announced processes. Several categories were searched and came up empty by title alone, which may mean the winner-naming release exists under different wording rather than that it doesn't exist: Ports, Shipping and Waterways PPP concessionaires; Ministry of Power's TBCB process beyond the one ISTS release above; Railways' Vande Bharat manufacturing-tender winner (a December 2024 release mentions "200 Vande Bharat Sleeper Rakes awarded to Technology partners" without naming them in its title, and was not fetched to check the body text); the Ministry of Defence's Strategic Partnership model shortlists (Project 75(I) submarines and similar); and results from India's first offshore mineral block auction, launched November 2024, which has roadshow and launch coverage but no results release yet indexed by title.

Sources and caveats

Every company name in this piece was transcribed from the body text of the specific PIB (pib.gov.in) release cited beside it, retrieved via its PRID (Press Release ID), not inferred from a release's title. Where a release was found by title search but not independently fetched and read for this piece, it is explicitly marked "unverified lead" rather than treated as a confirmed source. All fetched releases are primary government sources, graded strong. This piece does not verify that any of these contracts were subsequently completed, cancelled, or modified after the cited release date — the Nayakkarpatti Tungsten annulment noted in Section 2 is the one exception found in passing, and even that was not independently confirmed.
