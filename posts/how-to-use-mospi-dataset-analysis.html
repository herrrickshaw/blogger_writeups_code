Why it is useful

  • Who benefits: students, journalists and policy analysts who want India's headline macro numbers in one tidy, sourced place.
  • What it replaces: querying several ministry sites by hand. Each JSON file is a ready-to-load extract with its source and retrieval date.
  • Evidence from our read of the committed files: the snapshot lists CPI, IIP, WPI and RBI forex series with the pull date (2026-07-18), and records which connector calls failed or lagged.
  • It logs its own data problems (for example the NAS indicator call returned an upstream 500), which is useful for judging how far to trust it.
  • Status: legacy. The README says the repo was split and kept for history; use the two successors for new work.

What it is for

The repo is a chronological notebook of questions asked of India's MoSPI data connector (api.mospi.gov.in) and related sources: inflation by state, rupee and reserves, trade balance by HS chapter, import-dependency gaps, and policy coverage. The README lists the 25 MoSPI datasets and the workflow used to pull them.

Charts are static HTML files; data files are JSON. A Colab notebook loads them live. This is policy and economic analysis, not investment or legal advice.

Who it is for

  • Students and researchers wanting a worked example of using India's official statistics
  • Journalists checking a headline inflation or reserves number against a dated source
  • Developers who want JSON extracts of CPI, WPI, IIP and RBI series
Not for.
  • Anything needing current figures: the snapshot is dated 2026-07-18 and the RBI reserves series stops at June 2025
  • Investment or legal decisions
  • Active development: new sections go into the two successor repos

Quick start: commands and what you should see

Read the dated snapshot with Python

python3 -c "import json;d=json.load(open('data/mospi_snapshot_2026-07-18.json'));print(d['cpi']['latest_month'],d['cpi']['general_index'])"

Prints June 2026 and the Rural, Urban and Combined CPI index and year-on-year inflation, as in the sample output.

Limits and honest caveats

  • This is a legacy snapshot. The README says it is kept for history and that new work happens in the two successor repos.
  • Figures are as of 2026-07-18; the RBI reserves series ends at June 2025 and the NAS call fell back to local definitions.
  • We read committed files only. We did not re-pull any MoSPI data or run the notebook.
  • Some charts and sections interpret policy; treat those as the author's analysis, not official findings.
  • Policy and economic analysis only, not investment or legal advice.
