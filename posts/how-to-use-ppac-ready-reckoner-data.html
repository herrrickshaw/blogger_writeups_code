Why it is useful

  • Energy analysts, students and journalists skip the PDF copy-paste: refining, gas, ethanol and sales tables are already CSV.
  • It replaces manual retyping of PPAC's Ready Reckoner, and the extraction script (extract_rr.py) can be pointed at the next edition.
  • The tables cover Table 1.1 economy indicators through Table 6.10 ethanol blending, including gross refining margins by refinery and state-wise fuel sales.
  • Evidence from the run: all 100 CSVs loaded in pandas (3,465 rows). It also surfaced real clean-up work: spaces stripped from state names, '**' markers for missing values, Indian-style numbers like 13,61,343, and one shifted Grand Total row.

What it is for

The repo holds the tables of the PPAC Ready Reckoner (annual, FY2025-26 H1 edition) as CSVs under annual_fy2025-26_h1/csv/, with index.csv mapping each file to its table number, caption and page. extract_rr.py rebuilds the CSVs from a Ready Reckoner PDF using pdfplumber.

The tables are the publisher's figures as printed; this repo extracts them but does not correct them.

Who it is for

  • Energy and fuels analysts who need Indian refining, gas, ethanol and sales data in tabular form
  • Students building dashboards or models on public petroleum statistics
  • Researchers who want a starting point for extracting the next Ready Reckoner edition
Not for.
  • Treating the CSVs as audited: they are machine-extracted from a PDF and need checking against the source before publication
  • Trading or investment decisions. This is not investment advice
  • Ready-to-chart numbers: most tables need cleaning first (see limits)

Limits and honest caveats

  • The repo README is only a banner pointing to the AI how-to; this post is based on the files, the script's docstring and the run.
  • The extraction script was not run: it needs the Ready Reckoner PDF (not in the repo) and pdfplumber.
  • The CSVs are machine-extracted from PDF. Seen in this run: spaces stripped from some text, a shifted Grand Total row in Table 6.10, '**' and '#' source markers, and long footnotes landing in data rows. Verify against the original PDF.
  • Figures are the publisher's as printed; the repo does not validate them. The PPAC licence terms were not found in the repo, so check PPAC's reuse terms before republishing.
  • Edition covered: FY2025-26 H1 only. Not investment advice.
