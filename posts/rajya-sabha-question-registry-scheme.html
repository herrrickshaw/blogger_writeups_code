The upper-house companion to the Lok Sabha Question Registry and the Quarterly Reportage: what the Rajya Sabha asked about these same schemes, with the ministry's answer of record. All three views are classified by one scheme-keyword map, so they filter identically. Use it to find the answer, then read the answer. The underlying data analysis and build scripts are backed up at github.com/herrrickshaw.

Method. Harvested from the rsdoc.nic.in per-question index by subject keyword (same keyword lists as the Lok Sabha registry, from 2019), then classified against the same scheme-regex map and acronym-veto list as the Quarterly Reportage — so “Sanchar Mitra” does not file under PM MITRA and IIT (ISM) Dhanbad does not file under the Semiconductor Mission here either. Generated 2026-08-04 by scripts/build_rs_pq_registry.py in digital-twin-for-ipa.

Known limits, stated rather than hidden. The index matches keywords against the subject line only, so a question whose subject names no scheme is not captured even when its answer discusses one at length — coverage is of scheme-titled questions, not of every mention. Ministries answer as of their date; figures in older answers are superseded by newer ones. A row whose subject has nothing to do with its chip (the coloured scheme tag shown in the table) is a classification bug, not a parliamentary fact.
