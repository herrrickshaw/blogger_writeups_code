This is an index into the register, not a reading of it. Mapping is by keyword against release titles, so it will miss a scheme referred to obliquely and it cannot tell an approval from a progress report — a Parliament answer about PM E-DRIVE and the Cabinet decision creating it look alike here. Use it to find the release, then read the release. 151 of these rows are Cabinet or CCEA items.

Method. Source: the PIB release register (123,169 releases, 2017→today), refreshed daily. A release qualifies as an announcement here if its title matches the scheme-keyword map, or if it is a Cabinet/CCEA approval carrying incentive language. Quarters are calendar quarters, so 2026Q3 is Jul–Sep 2026. Generated 2026-08-04 by scripts/build_reportage.py in digital-twin-for-ipa, where the fuller ministry-wise and state-wise views also live.

Known limits, stated rather than hidden. Acronyms collide, and the map used to fall for them: IIT (ISM) Dhanbad is a mining school and was being read as the India Semiconductor Mission; Sanchar, Paryatan, Nyaya and Monument Mitra are unrelated to PM MITRA textile parks; “AIF” in a Competition Commission order is an Alternative Investment Fund, not the Agriculture Infrastructure Fund; and Krishi Unnati Mela is not UNNATI 2024. Thirty-two such rows were removed on 2026-08-04 by an explicit veto list. Others may remain — a row whose title has nothing to do with its chip (the coloured scheme tag shown in the table) is a mapping error, not a policy fact.
