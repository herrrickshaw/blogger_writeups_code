“Don't tax my patience.”
Ease the long-term capital gains bite and roll back the 2024 hikes — STCG at 20%, LTCG at 12.5%. Reward those who stay invested instead of penalising them.

“Protect me — don't cage me.”
Keep the F&O guardrails (delta-based limits, position caps) that curb gamified losses, but don't shut the small trader out of the market she helped build.

“Give me advice I can trust.”
