Top line

  • $920 million is what five funding rounds announced between 2 and 17 November 2026 add up to (computed; $919.75M); three of them, Applied Compute, LangChain and Model ML, hold 98% of it.
  • 71.2% is the Terminal-Bench 4.1 resolution rate GitHub reports for Copilot Fusion Max, 5.4 points above Opus 5.5 and Fable 5.2 (about 65.8%), in GitHub’s own changelog of 17 November.
  • 65 is Claude Fable 5.2’s score on the Artificial Analysis index at max effort; GPT-6.2 Astra scores 62 and Gemini 4 Hydrogen 51 by default at about a fifth of Astra’s output price.

What changed for items in earlier editions

Edition 04 (9 November) and Edition 03 (31 July) already carry these. The new facts, with the source of each:

  • Copilot Fusion Max. Edition 04 listed its benchmark result as single-source. GitHub’s own changelog of 17 November now gives 71.2% on Terminal-Bench 4.1, 5.4 points ahead of Opus 5.5 and Fable 5.2, at 88% less cost than Fable 5.2, and lists it on public leaderboards hosted by Stanford, Harbor and the Laude Institute. The result is still GitHub’s own measurement.
  • Traversal. The $120M Series B from Edition 04 is at a $1B valuation, co-led by Kleiner Perkins, Redpoint and Khosla.
  • Kimi K3.1 Pro. Released 23 October 2026, with weights due by 4 November. Moonshot reports 65.7% on Terminal-Bench 4.0, against 66.4% for Claude Opus 5.5 (vendor-reported; this is the 4.0 version, not 4.1).
  • Graphify. Edition 04 read 144,768 stars from GitHub’s API on 9 November. Y Combinator S26 is confirmed by Augment Code coverage of June 2026. Star counts quoted elsewhere run from 58K to 135K, so none is repeated here, and two GitHub repositories appear in results, so check which one a count belongs to.
  • Ruflo. Edition 04 read 81,324 stars on 9 November. The project was renamed from Claude Flow in February 2026 (npm package and CLI keep the claude-flow name). A project README reports 12.52M ecosystem npm downloads over the 12 months to 4 October 2026.
  • Kolibri (Edition 03). Konecta launched it on 13–16 June 2026; the 80% pre-built figure is the company’s.

Left out because the fact-check could not support it

The 24 November tracker dashboard carried claims that did not survive a line-by-line check on 25 November, so they are not repeated here: a model called “Kimi K3.1 Pro Prime” with a 59.7% Terminal-Bench 4.1 score (no such product found); Ruflo’s “121M downloads” and “98 agents”; SWE-2 Flash’s $0.10/$0.50 price and 4 December end date; a $8.24B and 117-round November funding total; OpenCode’s 209,405 stars and index score of 53.6; Graphify’s Apache-2.0 licence and ROSS rank; and Copilot’s reported addition of GPT-6.2 and MAI Code 2. The dashboard itself was corrected the same day.

What this piece could not establish
  • Every figure was read from search-result excerpts, several of them from aggregator sites; no source page was opened. Benchmark scores from vendors (Copilot Fusion Max, SWE-2 Flash, Kimi, Scribe) were not reproduced.
  • Claude Fable 5.2’s release date, and its $8/$40 price: the index score is on Artificial Analysis, the price comes from one secondary article.
  • Gemini 4 Carbon and Neon: both exist on Artificial Analysis, but their release dates were not confirmed, so they are not on the board.
  • Whether the Applied Compute round is a Series C: some reports use the label and others call it a growth round. Investor lists differ for Clark and Applied Compute.
  • GitHub star counts were not re-read this edition; the 9 November counts in Edition 04 are the latest verified ones.
  • Who among the five rounds’ investors is new to the company: this piece lists leads only.
