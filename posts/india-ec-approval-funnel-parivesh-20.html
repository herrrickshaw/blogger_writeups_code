The Environmental Clearance approval funnel

Read the counts with care — a proposal is not a project. Only 49% of these EC proposals are fresh new projects. The rest are activity on land that already holds a clearance: 22% are re-appraisals (mostly minor-mineral mining leases expiring and re-cleared on the same plots), plus transfers of EC to new owners, expansions of existing plants, and amendments. So the 54,406 grants below overstate the number of distinct new projects cleared.
