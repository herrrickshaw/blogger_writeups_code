Update: This is a companion reference for From Fuel to Fibre: India's Textile Import-Substitution Loop, published to support that article's segment-level trade tables with the full underlying HSN code list, rather than crowding the main piece.

What this is

The 314-code, 13-label technical-textile segment mapping used throughout the Fuel-to-Fibre article's segment tables, listed here in full. Each code has been cross-checked against the DPIIT's own December 2025 Guidebook on Mapping of HSN Codes to Ministries — the "M/o Textiles confirmed?" column shows whether that specific 8-digit code appears in the Ministry of Textiles' official 2,176-code ownership list (communicated via OM No. 12015/10/2024-TTP, 30 August 2024) or whether it sits administratively under a different Ministry/Department despite being part of this technical-textile segment classification.

Of 314 codes across 13 segment labels (the 12 official NTTM segments, plus Defence Textile), 261 (83%) are confirmed under Ministry of Textiles ownership in the DPIIT's official record. The remaining 53 sit under other ministries — by chapter count, largest first: Chapter 70 glass-fibre articles (9 codes), Chapter 62 apparel/garments (8), Chapter 56 felts/waddings/twines (7), Chapter 63 made-up textiles (5), with Health/Pharmaceuticals (Chapter 30, 4 codes) and Chemicals & Petrochemicals (Chapter 39, 1 code) further down the list, not the largest groups (corrected 9 Aug 2026) — a reminder that "technical textile" as an economic category cuts across administrative boundaries.

Methodology & caveats

  • The base 314-code, 13-label list comes from a working HSN-to-segment spreadsheet compiled locally; it merges an older 8-label classification (Geo-textiles, Agro-textiles, Medical/Hygiene Textiles, Defence Textile, Mobile Textiles, Sports Textiles, Protective Textiles, Building/Construction Textiles) with the National Technical Textiles Mission's current 12-segment naming (Geotech, Agrotech, Meditech, Mobiltech, Sportech, Protech, Buildtech, Packtech, Indutech, Hometech, Clothtech, Specialty Fibres & Composites). No HSN code in the source list is duplicated across two segment labels, so the segment totals reported in the Fuel-to-Fibre article sum correctly without double-counting.
  • This 314-code list is not identical to the Ministry of Textiles' own 207-HSN-code technical-textiles trade-statistics basket (the one behind the official FY2021-22 export/import table cited in the main article) — the two were compiled for different purposes and diverge in coverage per segment, most visibly for Specialty Fibres & Composites, where this list's 31-code basket is considerably broader than the Ministry's narrower trade-reporting definition.
  • Descriptions are taken verbatim (title-cased) from DGCI&S TradeStat's commodity-wise export/import listings where a match exists; 42 of the 314 codes (13.4%), with no FY2024-25 or FY2025-26 trade activity, have no description available.
  • "M/o Textiles confirmed?" reflects ownership in the DPIIT Guidebook only — it is a policy-jurisdiction record, not a trade-value or import-dependency measure. A "No" does not mean the product isn't a genuine technical textile; it means a different Ministry is nominally responsible for that code in the DPIIT's cross-government mapping exercise.
